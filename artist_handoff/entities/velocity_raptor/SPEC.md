## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the fifth Tier-2 slice (T2e, 2026-09-15). A small tameable feathered raptor: a 4x7x11 body, a raked neck to a head with a snout and a four-feather crest, two hat pieces, two arms of an upper and a lower piece with three zero-thickness feathers each, two legs of a thigh, a shin, a foot and a toe claw, and a two-piece tail with four zero-thickness feathers (VelocityRaptorModel.java:98-266, 34 parts), drawn at x0.75. 10 HP (20 tamed), speed 0.55 - the fastest thing on legs in the mod - and an attack of 2 it never uses: it has no attack goal (VelocityRaptor.java:60-70, :87-93). It flees monsters within 8 blocks and panics at 1.6 (:65, :67), is tempted by apples and tamed by one in two (:66, :166-182); tamed it follows its owner at 1.5 (:64), is untamed with a dead bush (:193-204), named with a tag (:211-216) and sat with a sneak-click (:220-224). Hurt on a 1-in-20 tick, or one tick in 250, it grazes dandelions, poppies, red tulips, oak leaves or hay within nine blocks, eating the block and healing 2 (:111-160); it takes at most 10 per hit and 2 per fall (:95-109). It spawns in daylight above y 50 (:265-270; ModEntities.java:592-593) and breeds on apples (:255, :272-276). Read it as a sprinting bird-dinosaur: a quick two-legged stride, the crest and tail feathers fluttering - and drooping as it weakens.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (VelocityRaptorModel.java:275-283): the two legs stride 45 degrees x the walking speed as stiff chains in opposition at 1.625 rad/tick, a 3.9-tick period - a sprint at the 0.55 speed (VelocityRaptor.java:91).
- Four unscaled rhythms: the arms sway 9 degrees in opposition on a 21-tick cosine (:289-299); the six arm feathers flutter 18 degrees in alternation at the stride's rate whether or not it walks (:300-305); the crest feathers flutter 18 degrees at 1.56 rad/tick and the tail feathers roll 45 degrees at 1.75 rad/tick in alternation - both scaled in rate and size by the health fraction, so a wounded raptor's feathers go slow and limp (:284-288, :306-310); the sitting pose flattens the tail feathers (:306).
- The body, neck, head, snout, hats and the two tail pieces are static; the head does not follow the look. The graze (VelocityRaptor.java:142-159) and the sit toggle (:220-224) draw no pose beyond the flat tail feathers.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride fore and aft as stiff chains, opposite each other — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3 * 1.25) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; bl1.xRot = a; bl2.xRot = a + 0.488; bl3.xRot = a; bl4.xRot = a + 0.628; br1..4 = -a + the same rests` (VelocityRaptorModel.java:275-283; wingspeed 1.25 (:21))
- **crest**: the four crest feathers flutter side to side in alternation, fast and wide when healthy, slow and limp when hurt — `hf = health / maxHealth; c = cos(t * 1.25 * 1.25 * hf) * pi * 0.1 * hf; hf1.yRot = hf3.yRot = c; hf2.yRot = hf4.yRot = -c` (VelocityRaptorModel.java:284-288)
- **arms**: the two arms and their feathers sway slowly fore and aft in opposition at all times — `a = cos(t * 0.3) * pi * 0.05; lf1.xRot = a + 0.279; lf2.xRot = a - 0.436; lff1.xRot = a - 0.279; lff2.xRot = a - 0.453; lff3.xRot = a - 1.047; the right arm: -a + the same rests` (VelocityRaptorModel.java:289-299)
- **arm_feathers**: the six arm feathers flutter in alternation at the stride's rate at all times, over the arms' slow sway — `f = cos(t * 1.3 * 1.25) * pi * 0.1; lff1.yRot = lff3.yRot = f; lff2.yRot = -f; rff1.yRot = rff3.yRot = -f; rff2.yRot = f` (VelocityRaptorModel.java:300-305)
- **tail_feathers**: the four tail feathers roll in alternation, wide and fast when healthy, limp when hurt, flat when sitting — `r = sitting ? 0 : cos(t * 1.4 * 1.25 * hf) * pi * 0.25 * hf; tf1.zRot = tf3.zRot = r; tf2.zRot = tf4.zRot = -r` (VelocityRaptorModel.java:306-310)
- `VelocityRaptorModel.poseFrom` (VelocityRaptorModel.java:280-319; orig ModelVelocityRaptor.java:267-302 in `render` (func_78088_a, :261-341; func_78087_a at :349-351 is a bare super call), wingspeed 1.25 from `new ModelVelocityRaptor(1.25f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:423): while the walking speed is above a tenth the leg angle is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed, else 0; the left leg's four parts pitch by it around rests of 0, 0.488, 0 and 0.628 and the right leg's by its negative around the same rests. The health fraction is the health over the max health; the four head feathers yaw alternately by plus and minus the cosine of the age times 1.25 times the wingspeed times the fraction, times pi, times 0.1, times the fraction. The arm angle is the cosine of the age times 0.3, times pi, times 0.05; the left arm feathers pitch by it around 0.279 and -0.436, the left fingers by it around -0.279, -0.453 and -1.047, and the right side by its negative around the same rests; the six fingers yaw alternately by plus and minus the cosine of the age times 1.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.1. The four tail feathers roll alternately by plus and minus the cosine of the age times 1.4 times the wingspeed times the fraction, times pi, times 0.25, times the fraction, or hold 0 while sitting. The walk position and the head angles play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (VelocityRaptorGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
