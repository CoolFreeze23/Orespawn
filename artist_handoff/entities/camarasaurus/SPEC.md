## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A long-necked sauropod pet: four body blocks, a three-piece neck rising to a two-piece head, a four-segment tail and four two-part legs (ModelCamarasaurus.java:48-68; 21 parts on a 256x256 sheet). It is passive and tameable with an apple - one chance in two (Camarasaurus.java:201-224) - then follows its owner (:69), sits and stands on a click (:238-243), takes a name tag (:226-234) and breeds on apples (:68, :276-278); 20 HP, speed 0.2, an unused 1-damage attribute (:83-88) and no target goals at all (:76-79). It floats (:67), flees monsters (:70), panics when hurt (:72), wanders (:74) and heads indoors (:80); when hurt or now and then it browses - it walks to the nearest leaves, vine, grass, fern, cactus or tall flower within 10 blocks and, within 3.5 blocks, eats the block (mobGriefing), heals 1 and burps (:161-194), never while sitting. It spawns above y 50 by day (:295-301); a baby is drawn at half size (CamarasaurusRenderer.java:30-31). Read it as a gentle giant on a tiny hitbox: a slow four-legged stride, the neck curling to follow you, the tail swinging wide when healthy and barely when hurt.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (ModelCamarasaurus.java:188-196): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.65 (wingspeed, :24), x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed swings the front left and back right legs one way and the other two the opposite way - 45 degrees at full stride every 7.4 ticks, the upper and lower parts of each leg as one rigid unit (the back uppers around a -0.15 rad rest). Below the threshold the legs snap to their rests.
- The tail reads the pet's health (:198-212): the health fraction multiplies both the rate (1.5 x 0.65 x hf) and the amplitude (pi x 0.25 x hf) of a sweep taken at a quarter, a half, three quarters and the whole along the four segments, each next segment's position re-derived 5, 8 and 7 units along the previous segment's yaw - an FK chain written by hand on flat bones (when this rig lands a keyframed sweep must key positions too, or the tail must be parented). At full health a 45-degree tip sweep every 6.4 ticks; near death almost still. The `if (false)` at :200-202 is dead code.
- The neck and head follow the look yaw in a curl with no pitch (:214-226): the base piece takes an eighth, the middle a quarter (at the base's position), the top 0.38 (placed 6 units along the middle piece's yaw), and both head pieces the full yaw (placed 7 and 5 units along the previous piece) - so the LookAtPlayerGoal (Camarasaurus.java:73) bends the whole neck toward you. Positions again re-derived by the code.
- The four body blocks never move; there is no sitting branch in the model (the synched sitting flag, toggled at Camarasaurus.java:238-243, changes nothing visible) and no attacking flag or strike.
- The renderer draws it at 0.65 and a baby at half that (CamarasaurusRenderer.java:30-31) over a 0.5 x 1.2 hitbox (ModEntities.java:481-484).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four legs stride, upper and lower parts of a leg together: front left with back right, front right with back left — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount : 0; FLegupleft/FLegdownleft.xRot = a; FLegupright/FLegdownright.xRot = -a; BLegupleft.xRot = -0.15 - a; BLegdownleft.xRot = -a; BLegupright.xRot = -0.15 + a; BLegdownright.xRot = a` (ModelCamarasaurus.java:188-196)
- **tail**: the tail sweeps side to side, each segment wider than the one before - wide and quick when healthy, slow and small when hurt — `hf = health / maxHealth; n = cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 0.65 * hf) * pi * 0.25 * hf; Tail0.yRot = n * 0.25; Tail1.yRot = n * 0.5; Tail2.yRot = n * 0.75; Tail3.yRot = n; each segment's x/z placed along the previous segment's yaw (5, 8, 7 units)` (ModelCamarasaurus.java:198-212)
