## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A big theropod: a 10x18x31 torso with hips and a 25-long tail behind, a neck angled up to a 9x9x12 head and a 7x9x9 snout tip with a hinged jaw beneath, a spike on each side of the head and each flank, two small 2x10x2 forelimbs, and two legs of thigh, shin, heel spur and foot (ModelAlosaurus.java:70-153; 21 parts on a 128x128 sheet). It is hostile: 110 HP, 18 damage, 8 armour, speed 0.35 (MobStats.ALOSAURUS, Alosaurus.java:94-103), 40 XP. Every fifth tick it picks the nearest living thing in sight within 12 blocks - other allosaurs, cryolophosaurs, velocity raptors and creative players excepted (:168-206) - and hands it to its dinosaur melee goal (:77-78): a chase at 1.25, a bite within 4 blocks plus half the prey's width on dice, the synched attacking flag raised for the chase and cleared when the scan comes up empty (:177); a bite knocks the victim back 1.2 blocks and up a little (:142-159). It walks through villages (:84), wanders far (:85), watches players (:86), floats (:76) and spawns in darkness at night above y 50 in a clear column, never near another (:219-229). Read it as a heavy hunter: a striding, tail-balanced walk, the little forelimbs twitching, the jaw hanging almost shut until it hunts - then snapping.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (ModelAlosaurus.java:161-171): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.22, x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed is added to every part of the right leg and subtracted from every part of the left - a 45-degree stride at full speed every 22 ticks; the thigh, shin, heel spur and foot of a leg share one pivot and swing as one rigid unit around their rests (-0.174, +0.506, -0.401, 0 rad). Below the threshold the legs snap to their rests.
- The jaw branches on the synched attacking flag (:172-174): calm it sits 0.1 rad (6 degrees) open and still; attacking it chomps 0.52 rad plus the cosine of the age x 0.45 x pi x 0.18 - 32 degrees either way around a 30-degree gape every 14 ticks.
- The two forelimbs twitch at all times (:175-176): the cosine of the age x 0.1, x pi x 0.05 - 9 degrees around a 30-degree forward rest every 62.8 ticks, both together.
- The torso, hips, tail, neck, head, snout tip and the four spikes never move; nothing reads the look (netHeadYaw unused), so the LookAtPlayerGoal (Alosaurus.java:86) turns nothing visible.
- The attacking flag is raised by the dinosaur melee goal for the chase (Alosaurus.java:77-78, the accepted mapping of the original 1-in-5 combat pass, :161-167) and cleared when the fifth-tick scan finds no prey (:177) - a held state, so the bite (doHurtTarget :143-159, with its 1.2-block knockback) is transport 1.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride in opposition, every part of a leg swinging together as one rigid unit — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.22) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount : 0; rightleg.xRot = -0.174 + a; rightleg2.xRot = 0.506 + a; rightleg3.xRot = -0.401 + a; rightleg4.xRot = a; leftleg* = the same rests - a` (ModelAlosaurus.java:161-171)
- **jaw**: the jaw hangs almost shut at rest and chomps wide when it hunts — `jaw.xRot = attacking ? 0.52 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.45) * pi * 0.18 : 0.1` (ModelAlosaurus.java:172-174)
- **forelimbs**: the two little forelimbs twitch slowly together — `shape11.xRot = shape17.xRot = -0.523 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.1) * pi * 0.05` (ModelAlosaurus.java:175-176)
