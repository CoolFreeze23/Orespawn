## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A giant tameable rubber duck: a flat bottom, a body, a back, a neck, a head with a beak, and two wings. It waddles and, if you are not careful, bites. Read it as a bath toy come alive: the head following your gaze, the wings flapping.

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: the wings flap out and back (the absolute value: they never cross under), mirrored — `lwing.zRot = -|cos(ageInTicks * 1.0) * pi * 0.15|; rwing.zRot = +that` (RubberDuckyModel.setupAnim; RubberDuckyGeoReplacement.java)
- **head**: the head and beak turn with the gaze — `head.yRot = beak.yRot = rad(netHeadYaw)` (RubberDuckyModel.setupAnim; RubberDuckyGeoReplacement.java)
- `RubberDuckyModel.setupAnim` (RubberDuckyModel.java; orig ModelRubberDucky.java:88, 91, 111-114 for the lines the port keeps, `render`, wingspeed 1.0 from `new ModelRubberDucky(1.0f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:502): (1) the head and the beak yaw by the head yaw in radians (orig :88 multiplied it by 0.45 and pitched by 0.65 of the head pitch besides); (2) the wing angle is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.0, times pi, times 0.15, made positive - a flap between 0 and 27 degrees twice per 6.28 ticks - the left wing rolls by its negative, the right by it (orig :91, :111-114; 1.7.10 latched the flap on a random roll, quadrupled it past five kills, stilled it while sitting and yawed the wings by half the angle - the register line). The walk position, the walking speed and the entity's state play no part in the port's pose. The GeckoLib hook is the same (RubberDuckyGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
