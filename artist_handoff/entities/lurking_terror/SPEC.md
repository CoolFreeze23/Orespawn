## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the remainder slice (2026-09-15). Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A flying six-legged horror with a four-way mouth: a body, a head, a thorax and an abdomen; four jaw petals around the head - a right, a left, an upper and a lower jaw, each a jaw plate, a second plate and six teeth - that open outward in two pairs; a three-part tongue that shoots forward as the mouth opens; six legs hanging under it (the four front and middle legs in three parts, the two rear in two) and four wing blades (LurkingTerrorModel.java; 59 parts on a 256x64 sheet). It is hostile to nearly everything alive and in sight - a long list of fellow flyers and plants excepted (EntityLurkingTerror.java:229-252) - within 12 blocks (:211-212): it flies at the target, raises the synched attacking flag (:163) and bites for 6 within 2.4 blocks (:165-167, doHurtTarget); 30 HP, 5 armour (MobStats.LURKING_TERROR, :66-73). It has no goals at all: its flight is its own - random air spots (:142-159), the motion eased toward them (:173-181), the yaw turned a quarter of the way per tick (:183-185), the vertical motion damped to 0.6 (:116-120) - and a hit turns it on its attacker (:197-206); its own kind cannot hurt it (:200). It spawns in dark spots by DAY, above y 10, never near another (:254-266). Read it as a hovering insect nightmare: wings buzzing, legs twitching one at a time, the four jaws flaring open and the tongue lashing out when it attacks.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Four wing blades buzz at all times (LurkingTerrorModel.java:570-574): the cosine of the age x 1.4 (wingspeed 1.0, :21), x pi x 0.2 - 36 degrees around a 26-degree rest every 4.5 ticks - added to the front pair and subtracted from the rear pair.
- The thorax breathes (:568-569): the sine of the age x 0.1, x pi x 0.06 (11 degrees every 62.8 ticks) pitches it, and the abdomen's height follows (14 units x the sine of that angle, about 2.6 units) - a position key on the abdomen.
- The legs twitch at random (:453, :457-480, :493-525): at every boundary of the 9-tick leg cycle a per-entity latch rolls six bits (1 in 3 for each front leg, 1 in 4 for each middle, 1 in 6 for each rear); a leg whose bit is set swings sideways this cycle - the sine of the age x 0.7 x pi x 0.25 (front, 45 deg), 0.15 (middle, 27 deg) or 0.1 (rear, 18 deg) added to its rest (+-0.191 rad on the hips, +-0.675 on the front and middle feet, +-0.34 on the rear legs); the others hang still. A client-side roll, not a server state.
- The mouth (:454, :481-491, :527-567): a second latch on the 7-tick mouth cycle is set every cycle while the synched attacking flag is up (:487-489) and in 1 cycle in 20 at random when calm (:484-486); while set, the absolute value of the sine of the age x 0.9 x pi x 0.35 (63 degrees, a flare every 3.5 ticks) yaws the right jaw open and the left jaw open the other way and pitches the upper jaw up and the lower jaw down - each jaw's second plate and six teeth copying it - and slides the tongue's first piece 5 and its tip 10 units forward per radian (up to 5.5 and 11 units); the tongue's own rotations are zeroed. Latch clear: the jaws sit shut on their +-0.41 rad rests and the tongue is home.
- The body and head never move; nothing scales with walking speed and nothing reads the look. The visible travel is the entity's own flight (EntityLurkingTerror.java:136-185): random air spots, the motion eased toward them, a hit redirecting it at the attacker (:197-206).
- The attacking flag is held: set 1 every ninth think tick while a target is found (EntityLurkingTerror.java:160-164) and 0 when none is (:169) - a state, so the bite (doHurtTarget :166, within 2.4 blocks) is transport 1.

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: the four wing blades buzz fore and aft, the front pair and the rear pair in opposition — `w = cos(t * 1.4 * 1.0) * pi * 0.2; wing_1.xRot = wing_2.xRot = 0.455 + w; wing_3.xRot = wing_4.xRot = 0.455 - w` (LurkingTerrorModel.java:570-574)
- **breath**: a slow breathing: the thorax rocks and the abdomen rides up and down with it — `b = sin(t * 0.1 * 1.0) * pi * 0.06; thorax.xRot = b; abdomen.y = thorax.y - sin(b) * 14` (LurkingTerrorModel.java:568-569)
- **legs**: the six legs twitch sideways one at a time: at each cycle boundary a roll picks which legs swing this cycle, the rest hang still — `s = sin(t * 0.7 * 1.0); leg2/leg2part2.zRot = 0.191 + (bit0 ? s * pi * 0.25 : 0), leg2part3 = 0.675 + the same; leg1 mirrored (-0.191 / -0.675, bit1); leg4 (+) / leg3 (-) with bits 2 / 3 at pi * 0.15; leg6 (-0.34, bit4) / leg5 (+0.34, bit5) at pi * 0.1` (LurkingTerrorModel.java:453, 457-480, 493-525)
- **mouth**: the four jaw petals flare open outward together - right and left sideways, upper and lower up and down - and the tongue shoots forward with them; constantly while attacking, a rare twitch when calm — `m = latch ? abs(sin(t * 0.9 * 1.0) * pi * 0.35) : 0; jaw1 (+ part2, teeth).yRot = m; jaw2 (...).yRot = -m; jaw3 (...).xRot = -m; jaw4 (...).xRot = m; tonguepart1.z = tonguepart2.z - m * 5; tonguepart3.z = tonguepart2.z - m * 10` (LurkingTerrorModel.java:454, 481-491, 527-567)
- `LurkingTerrorModel.poseFrom` (LurkingTerrorModel.java:459-583; orig ModelLurkingTerror.java:445-621 in `render` (func_78088_a, :435-622; func_78087_a at :630-632 is a bare super call), wingspeed 1.0 - the default of orig ModelLurkingTerror.java:15, `new ModelLurkingTerror()` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:461 passes none - the port's WINGSPEED): two per-entity latches drive the legs and the mouth. Each frame the leg rhythm's phase, the age in ticks times 0.7 times the wingspeed reduced modulo two pi and taken absolute, is compared with the last frame's; on each wrap the entity's own random rolls six selector bits into the first latch - two at one in three, two at one in four, two at one in six; the mouth rhythm's phase (0.9 times the wingspeed) likewise rolls the mouth bit at one in twenty, forced on while attacking. The six legs, each three parts, sway about Z by the sine of the age times 0.7 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.25 (the first pair), 0.15 (the second) or 0.1 (the third) around their rests - plus or minus 0.191 on the first two parts and 0.675 on the third for the first two pairs, plus or minus 0.34 for the third pair - only while their bit is set, else they hold the rest. While the mouth bit is set the four jaws with their twenty-four teeth open by the absolute value of the sine of the age times 0.9 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.35 - the side pair about Y, the top and bottom pair about X, each side mirrored - and the three tongue parts are held straight with the second and third placed 5 and 10 units out on the same angle; closed, everything sits at 0. The thorax breathes: it pitches by the sine of the age times 0.1 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.06, and the abdomen follows 14 units of that sine in y. The four wings beat about X by the cosine of the age times 1.4 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.2, around 0.455 (the right wings the negatives). The walk position and speed play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (LurkingTerrorGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
