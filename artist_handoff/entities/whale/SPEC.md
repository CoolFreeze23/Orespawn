## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A passive whale: a 20x12x52 body with a belly slab and a back ridge, a 16x8x22 head with a hinged lower jaw plate, a small dorsal fin, two flippers of a root and a 22-long blade, and a tail of two pieces ending in a pair of flukes (ModelWhale.java:56-124, fourteen parts). 100 HP, speed 0.35, no attack (Whale.java:69-74). It drifts with the current (pushed by fluid, :120-123), is tempted by cod (:62), panics when hurt (:63), breeds on a crystal apple (:61, :239-242) and, stranded, scans up to ten blocks for water and heads there at 1.0, drying out 4 HP at a time when none is found (:134-159); in water it splashes and heals (:161-165). Every 250-500 ticks it blows: a 25-50-tick spout of bubble and splash particles above its back (:46-47, :80-113). It spawns in daylight above y 50 on a 1-in-50 roll with no other whale within 32 (:203-211; ModEntities.java:414-415). Read it as a whale: a slow flipper beat, the tail flukes pumping up and down with a lag along the chain, the jaw barely breathing.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Two gait-scaled rhythms with a rest branch at a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (ModelWhale.java:132-156): on the move the flippers beat 36 degrees x the walking speed at 0.3 rad/tick and the tail pumps 29 degrees x the speed at 0.4 rad/tick, the swing multiplied 0.5 / 1.25 / 2.25 from the first piece to the flukes; at rest the same shapes run slow and small - a 9-degree waft at 0.08 and a 5-degree pump at 0.05 - so a still whale never freezes.
- The jaw plate breathes 3.6 degrees about its 5-degree rest at 0.03 rad/tick, a 10-second cycle (:141-142). The body, belly, back ridge, dorsal fin and head are static; the head does not follow the look.
- The same formulas draw it in water and on land: setupAnim has no water branch, so the swim and the stranded crawl toward water (Whale.java:134-159) differ only in speed. The spout (:80-113) is particles on a timer, not a pose.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two flippers beat up and down together, the roots at half the blades' angle, while the tail pumps with the swing growing from the root to the flukes: broad strokes on the move, a slow waft and a gentle pump at rest — `f = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 0.3) * pi * 0.2 * amount : cos(t * 0.08) * pi * 0.05; lfin2.zRot = 0.436 + f; lfin1.zRot = lfin2.zRot / 2; rfin2.zRot = -0.436 - f; rfin1.zRot = rfin2.zRot / 2; a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 0.4) * pi * 0.16 * amount : cos(t * 0.05) * pi * 0.03; tail1.xRot = a * 0.5; tail2.xRot = a * 1.25; tailfin1.xRot = tailfin2.xRot = a * 2.25; tail2.z = tail1.z + cos(tail1.xRot) * 14; tail2.y = tail1.y - sin(tail1.xRot) * 14; tailfin.z = tail2.z + cos(tail2.xRot) * 8; tailfin.y = tail2.y - sin(tail2.xRot) * 8` (ModelWhale.java:132-139 (the flippers), :144-156 (the tail))
- **jaw**: the jaw plate breathes very slowly open and shut at all times — `jaw.xRot = 0.087 + cos(t * 0.03) * pi * 0.02` (ModelWhale.java:141-142)
