A hostile butterfly on the shared butterfly rig: a one-cube body bar, a one-cube head and four flat wing panels a side — inner and outer forewings, a hindwing and a long tail streamer (ButterflyModel.createBodyLayer, ButterflyModel.java:54-102) — in the vampire skin, a twin of the butterfly's (VampireButterflyRenderer.java:22-23). It is a Monster, not an ambient creature (VampireButterfly.java:56): it wanders on the butterfly flight preset (:79), takes revenge on whatever hurt it and targets the nearest player within 16 blocks (:80-81), then steers straight at its target each tick and bites within touch range (customServerAiStep :111-136) — 2.0 of damage, Hunger for 200 ticks, Weakness for 120, a small knockback, and an attacking-ticks pulse of 10 the model does not read (doHurtTarget :139-154). It whines the mosquito sound when hurt (:176-179), never takes fall damage (:161-168), and spawns under the light-free monster rule (:196-203) wherever a roster places it (no biome modifier or biome file names it in the port's data today). Read it as the butterfly with intent: the same flutter, but it homes in.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One rhythm runs at all times: the eight wing panels roll about the body's long axis at 1.3 rad/tick (a 4.83-tick beat), 45 degrees each way, the left set mirroring the right (ButterflyModel.java:109-115). The body and the head are static; the walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part — the model flaps the same wandering, chasing and biting.
- The flight is the entity's: AmbientFlightGoal's butterfly preset wanders it (VampireButterfly.java:79) and, with a target, customServerAiStep adds a 0.04-per-tick step toward the target's eyes and damps the motion by 0.92 (:120-128), looking at the target (:129); tick :99-104 damps the vertical speed by 0.6. The bite lands when it is within 1.4 blocks plus half the target's width (:131-134).
- The synced ATTACKING_TICKS integer (VampireButterfly.java:61-62) is set to 10 by a landed bite (:151) and counts down each tick (:105-107): a hit pulse the model does not read, and not the contract's DATA_ATTACKING byte.
- The renderer draws the head, then the body, then the wing pairs inner to outer (renderToBuffer, ButterflyModel.java:119-130).
