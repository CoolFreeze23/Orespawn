## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A hostile giant mole: a 32x20x56 body with a shoulder block, a three-block head ending in a snout with a six-spoke nose star, two arms of an upper arm, a hand and four 13-long claws, two legs of the same build, a rump and a stub tail (MolenoidModel.java:104-287, 37 parts). 200 HP, 18 damage, 12 armour, speed 0.35 (EntityMolenoid.java:77-86). On a 1-in-4 tick it picks prey in a 12-block box - non-creative players, monsters and the attackable animals, never another mole (:141-142, :280-302) - seeing through dirt, sand and gravel with its own block ray (:315-358); within 6 blocks plus half the target's width it raises its attacking flag and, within 4, bites on a double dice for 18 with a knockback (:147-151, :115-130), else lobs one to four mole-dirt blocks onto the ground around the target (:152-156, :211-226); farther out it paths at 1.25 (:158), the flag dropped when it finds nothing (:161). It digs as it goes: a three-block column ahead is cleared every tick, up or down toward the prey (:199, :243-268), and mounds of mole dirt are kicked up behind it in proportion to its speed (:175-197); walls cannot hurt it (:107-113). It spawns in the dark at night above y 50 under three blocks of clear air, no other within 16 (:387-397; ModEntities.java:272-274). Read it as a mole: a crawling dig with the arm chains sweeping, the nose star turning, a frantic dig when it is on its prey.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (MolenoidModel.java:295, :328-360): the arm and leg chains sweep 45 degrees x the walking speed about the vertical at 0.65 rad/tick, a 9.7-tick period, the hands and feet at 1.25 times the angle and the claws and toes at 1.5, each piece placed along the one before (15 and 10 px); the arms and legs take the same angle with opposite signs, so a left arm sweeps with the right leg - a crawling dig.
- One branch on the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (EntityMolenoid.java:51-52), raised on the 1-in-4 pass within 6 blocks plus half the target's width (:147-148) and dropped when the pass finds no prey (:161): while it is up the arms dig at 0.85 rad/tick and full 45-degree amplitude whether or not the mole moves (:295), the legs still following the stride (:328).
- The six-spoke nose star rolls through a full turn and back on a 126-tick cosine at all times (:361-366). The body, shoulders, the three head blocks, the rump and the tail are static; the head does not follow the look.
- The bite (doHurtTarget, EntityMolenoid.java:115-130: 18 and a 0.8 knockback, within 4 blocks on the double dice :150-151), the block lob (:152-156, :211-226), the dig ahead (:243-268) and the spoil mounds (:175-197) are world edits on server rolls - no animation events today.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two arm chains and the two leg chains sweep about the vertical like a mole digging, each hand, foot, claw and toe set swinging farther than its limb, the legs against the arms and left against right; while the attacking flag is up the arms dig faster and even when it stands still — `g = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3 * 0.5) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; a = attacking ? cos(t * 1.7 * 0.5) * pi * 0.25 : g; larm.yRot = a + 0.628; lhand.x/z = larm.x/z ± 15 along larm.yRot; lhand.yRot = a * 1.25; lclaw1..4.x/z = lhand.x/z ± 10 along lhand.yRot; lclaw1..4.yRot = a * 1.5 - 0.174; rarm.yRot = a - 0.628; rhand.yRot = a * 1.25; rclaw1..4.yRot = a * 1.5 + 0.174 (the right chain placed the mirror way); lleg.yRot = -g + 0.628; lfoot.yRot = -g * 1.25; ltoe1..4.yRot = -g * 1.5 - 0.261; rleg.yRot = -g - 0.628; rfoot.yRot = -g * 1.25; rtoe1..4.yRot = -g * 1.5 + 0.261; the feet and toes placed along the chain as the hands and claws are` (MolenoidModel.java:295-327 (the arms), :328-360 (the legs); wingspeed 0.5 (:21))
- **nose_star**: the nose star rolls slowly back and forth through a full turn at all times — `s = cos(t * 0.1 * 0.5) * pi; nosestar1.zRot = s; nosestar2.zRot = s + 0.523; nosestar3.zRot = s + 1.047; nosestar4.zRot = s + 1.57; nosestar5.zRot = s - 1.047; nosestar6.zRot = s - 0.523` (MolenoidModel.java:361-366)
