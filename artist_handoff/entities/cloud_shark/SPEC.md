## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A hostile shark that swims through the sky: a boxy body and rear body, a head with a hinged jaw, a top fin, a tail fin and two side fins. It hunts from the clouds. Read it as a shark in the air: side fins sculling, the tail fin sweeping, the jaw working.

### 4.2 The math

- **leftfin**: the left side fin sculls slowly about the vertical around its rest angle — `leftfin.yRot = 1.15 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.7 * 1.0) * pi * 0.15` (ModelCloudShark.java:83-84; CloudSharkGeoReplacement.java)
- **fins**: the right side fin and the tail fin sweep together at one faster rhythm, each with its own amplitude and rest angle — `rightfin.yRot = -0.9 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 1.0) * pi * 0.15; fins.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 1.0) * pi * 0.25` (ModelCloudShark.java:85-87; CloudSharkGeoReplacement.java)
- **jaw**: the jaw works slowly open and shut around a half-open rest — `jaw.xRot = 0.5 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.5 * 1.0) * pi * 0.1` (ModelCloudShark.java:88-89; CloudSharkGeoReplacement.java)
- `ModelCloudShark.setupAnim` (ModelCloudShark.java:82-90; orig ModelCloudShark.java:80-87, `render`, wingspeed 1.0 from `new ModelCloudShark(1.0f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:471): (1) the left side fin yaws by 1.15 radians plus the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.7 (times the wingspeed 1.0), times pi, times 0.15 - a 27-degree scull every 8.98 ticks around a 66-degree rest (orig :81-82); (2) the right side fin yaws by -0.9 radians plus the cosine of the age times 1.5, times pi, times 0.15 - the same 27 degrees every 4.19 ticks around a -52-degree rest (orig :83-84); (3) the tail fin yaws by the cosine of the age times 1.5, times pi, times 0.25 - 45 degrees each way at the right fin's rhythm (orig :85); (4) the jaw pitches by 0.5 radians plus the cosine of the age times 0.5, times pi, times 0.1 - 18 degrees every 12.57 ticks around a 29-degree open rest (orig :86-87). The walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (CloudSharkGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
