## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Sea Monster is a plesiosaur: a front and back body block with four flipper fins, a six-segment neck rising to a small head of upper jaw, hinged lower jaw and two eyes, and a seven-piece tapering tail (ModelSeaMonster.createBodyLayer, ModelSeaMonster.java:72-168). It has 110 HP, 14 attack and 8 armour (MobStats.SEA_MONSTER, createAttributes SeaMonster.java:101-109), swims at 0.55 and crawls on land at 0.25 (aiStep :125-132), wanders far and looks about (:93-98), and every fifth tick takes its stored target or the nearest living thing in a 16-block box, closing to within four blocks plus half its width, raising the attacking flag and biting on a 1-in-4 roll (customServerAiStep :215-247; doHurtTarget :135-147 with a 0.6 knockback). Out of water it seeks the nearest water on a 1-in-25 roll and dries out slowly when it finds none (:194-213); in water it heals 1 on a 1-in-120 roll with a splash (:249-252). Cactus cannot hurt it, hits are refused for 8 ticks after each one and a mob attacker becomes its target (hurt :150-170). It spawns at night in the dark above y 50 with no other within 16 blocks (checkSpawnRules :339-346) in oceans and swamps (the ocean spawns and hostile_sea_monster biome modifiers). Read it as a long-necked swimmer: the tail's travelling wave drives it, the fins row, the neck rears and strikes.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Moving (a walking speed above 0.1) or with the attacking flag set, three speed-scaled waves run: the tail sweeps at 0.65 rad/tick (a 9.7-tick period), the swing growing from a seventh at the base to the full 36 degrees at the tip (ModelSeaMonster.java:175-194); the four flippers row at 0.6 rad/tick, front and back pairs opposite (:195-203); and the neck rears by 0.455 x the speed and nods 45 degrees x the speed along its six segments at 0.45 rad/tick, the head riding its end (:204-226). Still, the tail is straight and the fins and neck stir 3.6 degrees.
- The head and eyes turn half the look yaw (:227-229); the lower jaw chomps 31 degrees at 0.85 rad/tick while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (SeaMonster.java:49, raised at :237 within four blocks plus half the target's width) and breathes 9 degrees at 0.1 rad/tick otherwise (:230-236).
- The two body blocks are static; the walk position plays no part. The entity swims at 0.55 and crawls at 0.25 (aiStep :125-132), so the same waves run faster in the water.
- The bite (doHurtTarget, SeaMonster.java:135-147) is rolled by the fifth-tick scan (:215-247) — nothing in the code fires an animation for it.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the tail sweeps side to side with the swimming or walking speed, the swing growing toward the tip — `a = (amount > 0.1 || attacking) ? cos(t * 1.3 * 0.5) * pi * 0.2 * amount : 0; TailBase.yRot = a/7; Tail2 = a/6; Tail3 = a/5; Tail4 = a/4; Tail5 = a/3; Tail6 = a/2; TailTip = a; positions along the chain by cos/sin x 10 / 7 / 5 / 5 / 5 / 5` (ModelSeaMonster.java:175-194; wingspeed 0.5 (:21))
- **fins**: the four flippers row — the front pair and the back pair opposite — with the speed, and stir faintly at rest — `f = (amount > 0.1 || attacking) ? cos(t * 1.2 * 0.5) * pi * 0.2 * amount : cos(t * 1.2 * 0.5) * pi * 0.02; FinFrontLeft.xRot = f - 0.523, yRot = f + 0.698; FinBackLeft.xRot = -f - 0.523, yRot = -f + 0.698; FinFrontRight.xRot = f - 0.523, yRot = f - 0.698; FinBackRight.xRot = -f - 0.523, yRot = -f - 0.698` (ModelSeaMonster.java:195-203)
- **neck**: the neck rears and nods along its six segments, strongly with the speed or when attacking, faintly at rest; the head rides its end and follows the look — `n = (amount > 0.1 || attacking) ? 0.455 * amount + cos(t * 0.9 * 0.5) * pi * 0.25 * amount : cos(t * 0.3 * 0.5) * pi * 0.02; NeckBase.xRot = 0.455 + n/5; Neck2 = NeckBase + n/4; Neck3 = Neck2 + n/3; Neck4 = Neck3 + n/2; Neck5 = Neck4 - n/2; Neck6 = Neck5 - n/3; TopJaw / eyes placed 5 units past Neck6, yRot = look yaw x 0.5; positions along the chain by sin/cos x 9` (ModelSeaMonster.java:204-229)
- **jaw**: the lower jaw chomps wide while the attacking flag is set and breathes slowly otherwise — `BottomJaw.xRot = attacking ? 0.45 + cos(t * 1.7 * 0.5) * pi * 0.17 : 0.17 + cos(t * 0.2 * 0.5) * pi * 0.05 (placed with the head, yRot the look yaw x 0.5)` (ModelSeaMonster.java:230-236)
