## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A flat ray-like fish: a wide body, a tail and a tail tip. It glides along the water. Read it as a skate: the tail tip flicking with the swimming speed, idling with a slow twitch when still.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

### 4.2 The math

- **tail**: the tail tip flicks fast and wide when the skate moves (the swing scaled by its speed) and twitches slowly and shallowly when it is still, around a 45-degree rest — `Shape1.xRot = 0.785 + (amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.2) * pi * 0.15 * amount : cos(t * 0.4) * pi * 0.05)` (ModelSkate.setupAnim; SkateGeoReplacement.java)
- `ModelSkate.setupAnim` (ModelSkate.java; orig ModelSkate.java:48-49, `render`, wingspeed 1.0 from `new ModelSkate(1.0f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:486, unused by the pose): while the walking speed is above a tenth, the tail tip's swing is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.2, times pi, times 0.15, times the walking speed (up to 27 degrees each way every 5.24 ticks); at or below a tenth it is the cosine of the age times 0.4, times pi, times 0.05 (9 degrees every 15.7 ticks); the tail tip pitches by 0.785 radians plus that swing. The walk position, the head angles and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (SkateGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
