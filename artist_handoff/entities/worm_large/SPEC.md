## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

The largest of the three burrowing worms: a five-ring neck, a five-ring head, eight teeth around the maw, a five-part tail and a tail tip. It erupts from the ground and bites. Read it as a rearing worm: the neck swaying, the head rings following it as one block, the teeth gnashing around the maw, the tail tip circling.

### 4.2 The math

- **neck**: the five neck rings sway as one: a slow pitch around a 40-degree rear, a slower yaw — `neck*.xRot = cos(t*0.25)*pi*0.08 - 0.698; neck*.yRot = cos(t*0.15)*pi*0.07` (WormLargeModel.setupAnim; WormLargeGeoReplacement.java)
- **head**: the five head rings sit 32 units along the neck's direction (a position follow) and sway as one on their own two rhythms — `head*.pos = neck1.pos + (-sin(neckY)*cos(neckX), sin(neckX), -cos(neckX)) * 32 (x, y, z); head*.xRot = cos(t*0.35)*pi*0.15; head*.yRot = cos(t*0.45)*pi*0.05` (WormLargeModel.setupAnim; WormLargeGeoReplacement.java)
- **teeth**: the eight teeth ride 19 units on from the head (a position follow with fixed offsets) and gnash: the top / bottom pair about X, the side pair about Y, the four corners about both — `tooth*.pos = head1.pos + (follow 19 along the head's angles) + offsets; tooth1/2.xRot = headX +- a; tooth3/4.yRot = headY +- a; tooth5..8 both; a = cos(t*0.57)*pi*0.35` (WormLargeModel.setupAnim; WormLargeGeoReplacement.java)
- **tail_tip**: the tail tip circles on one rhythm, its yaw a quarter turn ahead of its pitch — `tailtip.xRot = cos(t*0.63)*pi*0.15 + 0.35; tailtip.yRot = cos(t*0.63 + 1.57075)*pi*0.15` (WormLargeModel.setupAnim; WormLargeGeoReplacement.java)
- `WormLargeModel.setupAnim` (WormLargeModel.java; orig ModelWormLarge.java:185-274, `render`): (1) the neck's pitch is the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.25, times pi, times 0.08, minus 0.698 radians (a 14-degree sway every 25 ticks around a 40-degree rear); its yaw the cosine of the age times 0.15, times pi, times 0.07 (13 degrees every 42 ticks); all five neck rings take both (orig :185-193); (2) the head rings' pivot is the first neck ring's pivot moved 32 units along the neck's direction - z minus the cosine of the pitch times 32, x minus the sine of the yaw times that cosine times 32, y plus the sine of the pitch times 32 - a POSITION write on all five (orig :194-200, :203-211); the head rings pitch by the cosine of the age times 0.35, times pi, times 0.15 (27 degrees every 18 ticks) and yaw by the cosine of the age times 0.45, times pi, times 0.05 (orig :201-202, :212-217); (3) the eight teeth's pivots are the head's pivot moved 19 units the same way, the first tooth 9 units up, the second 18 units below it, the third and fourth 9 units to either side and 9 down, the corners 6 units aside and 3 or 15 down (orig :218-246), then shifted in z by the sine of the head's pitch times 9 (the top / bottom pair, opposite ways), of its yaw times 9 (the side pair) and of both times 6 (the corners) (orig :247-258); the gnash is the cosine of the age times 0.57, times pi, times 0.35 (63 degrees every 11 ticks): the top tooth pitches by the head's pitch plus it and the bottom by the head's pitch minus it, the right tooth yaws by the head's yaw plus it and the left minus it, the four corners pitch and yaw by the head's angles plus or minus it (orig :259-271); (4) the tail tip pitches by the cosine of the age times 0.63, times pi, times 0.15, plus 0.35 radians, and yaws by the cosine of the age times 0.63 plus 1.57075, times pi, times 0.15 - a circle every 10 ticks (orig :272-274). The walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (WormLargeGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations, the follows through `moveTo`).
