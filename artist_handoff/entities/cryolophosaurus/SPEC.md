## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A small crested theropod: a body with hips and a tail behind, a neck to a head with a snout, a jaw beneath and the crest on top, two two-piece arms and two legs of thigh, shin, heel spur and foot (ModelCryolophosaurus.java:47-66; 20 parts on a 128x128 sheet, drawn at half size). It is a timid hunter: 10 HP, 3 damage, 1 armour, speed 0.25 (MobStats.CRYOLOPHOSAURUS, Cryolophosaurus.java:79-87). Every fifth tick it looks 9 blocks around for prey in sight - the big dinosaurs, ghosts, cave fishers, gamma metroids, butterflies, fireflies, mosquitoes, rock bases and creative players excepted (:117-136) - walks at it at 1.25 and bites within 2.2 blocks on timid 1-in-12-or-1-in-14 dice (:94-115, doHurtTarget :111); it retaliates when hit (:71) and, under a modern option, chases its attacker with a dinosaur melee goal whose attacking callback is a no-op (:57-67, :74-77 - it has no attacking flag). It floats (:55), panics (:56), wanders (:68) and spawns in darkness at night or below y 50 (:172-178). Read it as a nervous little raptor: a quick stride on rigid legs, the jaw working slowly all the time, the arms and tail carried still.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (ModelCryolophosaurus.java:179-187): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.75 (wingspeed, :24), x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed is added to every part of the right leg and subtracted from every part of the left - a 45-degree stride at full speed every 6.4 ticks; thigh, shin, heel spur and foot share one pivot and swing as one rigid unit around their rests. Below the threshold the legs snap to their rests.
- The jaw works at all times (:188): -1.15 rad plus the cosine of the age x 0.28 x pi x 0.1 - 18 degrees every 22.4 ticks; there is no attacking branch and no attacking flag (the melee goal's callback is a no-op, Cryolophosaurus.java:74-77).
- The body, neck, head, snout, crest, hips, tail and both arms never move; nothing reads the look (netHeadYaw unused), so the LookAtPlayerGoal (Cryolophosaurus.java:69) turns nothing visible.
- The bite (doHurtTarget :111, within 2.2 blocks on timid dice) has no pose of its own; the renderer draws the whole rig at half size (CryolophosaurusRenderer.java:30).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride in opposition, every part of a leg swinging together as one rigid unit — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.75) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount : 0; rightleg.xRot = -0.279 + a; rightleg2.xRot = 0.384 + a; rightleg3.xRot = -0.68 + a; rightleg4.xRot = a; leftleg* = the same rests - a` (ModelCryolophosaurus.java:179-187)
- **jaw**: the jaw works slowly open and shut around its hinge, whatever the creature is doing — `jaw.xRot = -1.15 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.28) * pi * 0.1` (ModelCryolophosaurus.java:188)
