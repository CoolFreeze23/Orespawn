## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A stocky human thief: a belly block, a chest block, a head that turns with the look, two arms splayed 28 degrees and two legs (ModelBandP.java:31-79; 7 parts on a 64x128 sheet). The same rig wears one of two skins, bandit or pirate, rolled once on its first tick and synched (BandP.java:121-128, BandPRenderer.java:39). It is hostile but tough rather than deadly: 100 HP, 1 damage, 18 armour, speed 0.32 (MobStats.BANDP, :89-98), 1000 XP. Every twelfth tick it picks a non-creative player, a villager, a girlfriend or a boyfriend in sight within 20 blocks (:243-265), walks at it at 1.25 (:164) and, within 3 blocks, hits it (:159-160, doHurtTarget); a hit that lands on a player steals one item - armour first, helmet to boots, else the last filled inventory slot - into a 100-slot stash that spills on death (:178-212, :219-241). It opens doors (:83), heads indoors (:86), walks through villages (:78) and strolls (:80); it spawns by day above y 100 within 36 blocks of a villager, never near another (:300-311). Read it as a swaggering brigand: a heavy stride with the arms swinging opposite the legs and the torso twisting, a fidget at rest, a snatch when it hits.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Two branches on the walking speed (ModelBandP.java:87-95). Walking (above 0.1): the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.4 (wingspeed, :21), x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed swings the left leg forward and the right back (45 degrees at full speed every 12.1 ticks); the arms take the same angle the other way (left arm back with the left leg forward, :99-100); the belly twists about the vertical by half of it (:101) and bounces about x at twice the rate - 0.07 rad plus the cosine of the age x 2.6 x 0.4 x pi x 0.025 x the walking speed (:98).
- Standing (at or below 0.1): the legs and the belly twist hold 0; the belly breathes 0.9 degrees at 0.6 x 0.4 (:93, :98) and the arms sway 3.6 degrees against each other at 0.3 x 0.4 (:94, :99-100) - a fidget every 52 ticks.
- The head follows the look in yaw and pitch (:102-103), so the LookAtPlayerGoal and RandomLookAroundGoal (BandP.java:81-82) are visible; the chest never moves.
- There is no attacking flag: the synched DATA_WHAT (BandP.java:42-43) is the skin roll, not a state; the hit (doHurtTarget :160, within 3 blocks every twelfth tick) and the theft (:161, :178-212) have no pose of their own.
- Nothing floats it: no FloatGoal is registered (:70-87) and its stroll avoids water (:80).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: a marching stride: the legs swing in opposition, the arms swing against the legs, and the belly twists with the step — `walking: a = cos(t * 1.3 * 0.4) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount; lleg.xRot = a; rleg.xRot = -a; larm.xRot = -a; rarm.xRot = a; belly.yRot = -a / 2; standing: a = 0` (ModelBandP.java:87-101)
- **belly_bounce**: the belly bounces twice per stride on the move and breathes barely at rest — `belly.xRot = 0.07 + (walking ? cos(t * 2.6 * 0.4) * pi * 0.025 * limbSwingAmount : cos(t * 0.6 * 0.4) * pi * 0.005)` (ModelBandP.java:89, 93, 98)
- **arm_sway**: at rest the arms sway slowly against each other — `standing: s = cos(t * 0.3 * 0.4) * pi * 0.02; larm.xRot = -s; rarm.xRot = s` (ModelBandP.java:94, 99-100)
- `ModelBandP.setupAnim` (ModelBandP.java:83-104; orig ModelBandP.java:79-94 in `render` (func_78088_a, :71-102; func_78087_a at :110-112 is a bare super call), wingspeed 0.4 from `new ModelBandP(0.4f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:504): while the walking speed is above a tenth the leg swing is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.3 times 0.4, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed, the belly bounce the cosine of the age times 2.6 times 0.4, times pi, times 0.025, times the walking speed, and the arm swing equals the leg swing; at or below it the leg swing is 0, the belly breathes by the cosine of the age times 0.6 times 0.4, times pi, times 0.005 and the arms sway by the cosine of the age times 0.3 times 0.4, times pi, times 0.02; the left leg pitches by the leg swing and the right by its negative, the belly pitches by 0.07 plus the bounce and yaws by minus half the leg swing, the left arm pitches by the negative of the arm swing and the right by it, and the head yaws by the look yaw in radians and pitches by the look pitch in radians. The walk position and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (BandPGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
