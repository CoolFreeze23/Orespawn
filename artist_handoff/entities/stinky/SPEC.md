## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A tameable winged stink dragon: an 8x8x10 body with a chest block and a rump, a two-piece raked neck to a 5x5x5 head with a snout and two two-piece horns, two zero-thickness 18x10 wings, four 8-long legs and a three-piece tail (StinkyModel.java:68-166, twenty parts). 100 HP, speed 0.3, 10 damage (EntityStinky.java:122-127). Beef tames it one time in two (:228-244) and its owner's click sits it (:246-254). On the ground (the synched DATA_ACTIVITY at 1, :68-69, :137-145) it wanders, follows its owner and flees monsters (:98-107) and, one tick in fifty, hunts coal ore within eight blocks and eats it with a burp (:297-299, :313-335); in the air (2 - a 1-in-20 roll on a 1-in-100 tick, a flying or distant owner, or any hit, :215-222, :277-292) it flies from block target to block target (:459-523), hunts monsters within 12 blocks and bites within 3 blocks plus half the target's width (:437-455). It burps up a coal in front of itself one tick in 1750 and farts out one of nineteen skin-dependent items behind one tick in 2000, changing skin as often (:181-190, :205-207, :542-569); it takes no fall damage (:210-213). It spawns in daylight, at most three together (:660-665; ModEntities.java:570-571). Read it as a comic dragon: the Spyro's bouncy trot and wing flap with a longer whipping tail, the legs tucked in flight.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Every rhythm's frequency is multiplied by limbSwingAmount (StinkyModel.java:173): the faster it walks, the faster it moves. Above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 the four legs trot 45 degrees x the speed in diagonal pairs (:179-185) and the two wing planes flap 72 degrees x the speed about their 23-degree rests (:175-177); the tail wags 36 degrees at the root, 58 at the second piece and 94 at the tip (:195-205). Standing still the legs and wings freeze and the tail is held curled hard to one side - the cosine of zero (:195, :199).
- One state branch on the synched DATA_ACTIVITY int (EntityStinky.java:68-69, :137-145): in flight (2) the legs tuck and hold - the front pair back a radian, the rear pair forward a radian (StinkyModel.java:186-193) - while the wings still beat only with the walking speed (:175). The sitting pose straightens the tail (:196-198).
- The head, snout and both horns take the full look yaw and a third of the pitch, the neck half the yaw and a third of the pitch (:207-221). The body, chest block, rump and neck base are static.
- The takeoff is a flag flip (EntityStinky.java:220, :278, :285, :291, :441, :447), the landing the same 1-in-100 roll answering 1 nineteen times in twenty (:277-279), the bite doHurtTarget within 3 blocks plus half the target's width on the 1-in-7 flight scan (:450-453), the coal meal a block eaten with a burp (:329-333), the burp and the fart item drops (:181-190) - none of them animation events.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four legs trot in diagonal pairs and the two wing planes flap together, all faster and wider as it walks faster; in the air the legs tuck - the front pair back, the rear pair forward - and hold, while the wings still follow only the walking speed — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 2.0 * amount) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; activity != 2 ? (Rleg1.xRot = a; Lleg1.xRot = -a; Rleg2.xRot = -a; Lleg2.xRot = a) : (Rleg2.xRot = Lleg2.xRot = -1.0; Rleg1.xRot = Lleg1.xRot = 1.0); w = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 2.3 * amount) * pi * 0.4 * amount : 0; Rwing.zRot = w - 0.4; Lwing.zRot = -w + 0.4` (StinkyModel.java:175-177 (the wings), :179-193 (the legs))
- **tail**: the tail whips side to side with the walk, the tip swinging wildest; standing still it stays curled hard to one side; sitting, it straightens — `a = sitting ? 0 : cos(t * 1.0 * amount) * pi * 0.2; tail2.yRot = a; tail3.z = tail2.z + cos(a) * 4; tail3.x = tail2.x + sin(a) * 4 - 0.5; tail3.yRot = a * 1.6; tail4.z = tail3.z + cos(tail3.yRot) * 3; tail4.x = tail3.x + sin(tail3.yRot) * 3 - 0.5; tail4.yRot = a * 2.6` (StinkyModel.java:195-205)
- `StinkyModel.poseFrom` (StinkyModel.java:180-230; orig ModelStinky.java:166-208 in `render` (func_78088_a, :162-229; func_78087_a at :237-239 is a bare super call); the original's wingspeed 0.65 from `new ModelStinky(0.65f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:490 is not kept by the port, whose `ws` is the walking speed itself - the register line of the hooks' landing): every rhythm's frequency is multiplied by the walking speed. While the walking speed is above a tenth the wing swing is the cosine of the age in ticks times 2.3 times the walking speed, times pi, times 0.4, times the walking speed, else 0; the right wing rolls by it minus 0.4 and the left wing by 0.4 minus it. The leg swing is likewise the cosine of the age times 2.0 times the walking speed, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed above the threshold, else 0: unless the activity is 2, the first right leg and the second left leg pitch by it and the first left leg and the second right leg by its negative; at activity 2 the second legs pitch -1 and the first legs 1. The tail angle is the cosine of the age times 1.0 times the walking speed, times pi, times 0.2, 0 while sitting: the second tail link yaws by it, the third by 1.6 times it and is placed from the second link's pivot (the bind) 4 units along the cosine (z) and sine (x) of the angle, less 0.5 in x, and the fourth yaws by 2.6 times it and is placed from the third 3 units along the cosine and sine of the third's yaw, less 0.5 in x. The head, the snout and the four horn pieces yaw by the look yaw in radians and the neck by half of it; the head, snout, neck and the four horns pitch by a third of the look pitch in radians. The walk position plays no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (StinkyGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
