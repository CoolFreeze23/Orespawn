## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Ghost's heavier sister: a pumpkin-headed scarecrow ghost on a one-block-wide stem body - a 7x5x7 head with a 1x2x1 stem on top, a 5x12x5 shirt over a 1x21x1 body pole, two 15-long arm bars, two 9x8x3 sleeves and a chain hanging from each arm, 16 tall on the left and 10 on the right (GhostSkellyModel.java:48-96), drawn translucent (GhostSkellyRenderer.java:49-51). It passes through blocks (noPhysics, GhostSkelly.java:62), drifts toward a spot beside the nearest player within 16 blocks or to a random air pocket (GhostSkelly.java:123-164) and hurts whoever it touches: 4 contact damage within 1.8 blocks every 20 ticks, no melee goal (GhostSkelly.java:108-121, doHurtTarget :80-83). It spawns at night, in Halloween week anywhere and year-round in snowy taiga, taiga, frozen river, jungle and dark forest (GhostSkelly.java:200-213). Read it as a hanged scarecrow adrift: arms and chains swaying on slow, unequal beats, the pumpkin head spinning full circle now and then.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Two arm assemblies swing as rigid units (GhostSkellyModel.java:109-116): the left arm bar, left sleeve and left chain share one roll about z (cos(t x 0.2) x pi x 0.05, 9 deg) and one sway about y (cos(t x 0.24) x pi x 0.05); the right bar, sleeve and chain share a roll at 0.22 and a sway at 0.26. Four unequal beats, so the two sides never keep step.
- Every arm, sleeve and chain part pivots at the rig's origin (PartPose.offset(0, 0, 0), GhostSkellyModel.java:68-96), so the 15-long arm bars and the hanging chains swing from the body's centre line, not from a shoulder.
- The head swivels in one cycle of three (GhostSkellyModel.java:117-130): newangle = cos(t x 0.05) x 2 pi - two full turns out and back over a 125.7-tick (6.28 s) cycle - applied only in a cycle whose boundary roll (1 in 3, entity.getRandom().nextInt(3) == 1 at :122) set the per-entity latch (RenderInfo rf2 / ri2, GhostSkelly.java:51-57); in the other cycles the head sits square (newangle = 0, :127-129). A client-side roll, not a server state.
- The body pole, the shirt and the stem never move; nothing scales with walking speed (limbSwingAmount unused); the head ignores netHeadYaw, so the LookAtPlayerGoal (GhostSkelly.java:67) turns nothing.
- The visible travel is the entity's own drift, not a pose: customServerAiStep eases the motion toward a flight target beside the nearest player or a random air pocket (GhostSkelly.java:123-164), turns the yaw a sixth of the way per tick (:166-169) and damps the vertical motion to 0.65 each tick (:86-91); it hovers with noPhysics (:62).
- Drawn translucent through entityTranslucent (GhostSkellyRenderer.java:49-51) at x1.05 (GhostSkellyRenderer.java:38).

### 4.2 The math

- **left_roll**: the left arm, its sleeve and its chain roll up and down together as one unit about the body's centre line — `larm.zRot = lsleeve.zRot = lchains.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.2) * pi * 0.05` (GhostSkellyModel.java:109-110)
- **right_roll**: the right arm, sleeve and chain roll together at a slightly faster beat than the left — `rarm.zRot = rsleeve.zRot = rchains.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.22) * pi * 0.05` (GhostSkellyModel.java:111-112)
- **left_sway**: the left arm unit also sways fore and aft about the vertical, on its own beat — `larm.yRot = lsleeve.yRot = lchains.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.24) * pi * 0.05` (GhostSkellyModel.java:113-114)
- **right_sway**: the right arm unit sways fore and aft about the vertical, fastest of the four beats — `rarm.yRot = rsleeve.yRot = rchains.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.26) * pi * 0.05` (GhostSkellyModel.java:115-116)
