## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

The Ghost's heavier sister: a pumpkin-headed scarecrow ghost on a one-block-wide stem body - a 7x5x7 head with a 1x2x1 stem on top, a 5x12x5 shirt over a 1x21x1 body pole, two 15-long arm bars, two 9x8x3 sleeves and a chain hanging from each arm, 16 tall on the left and 10 on the right (GhostSkellyModel.java:62-110), drawn translucent (GhostSkellyRenderer.java:49-51). It passes through blocks (noPhysics, GhostSkelly.java:62), drifts toward a spot beside the nearest player within 16 blocks or to a random air pocket (GhostSkelly.java:123-164) and hurts whoever it touches: 4 contact damage within 1.8 blocks every 20 ticks, no melee goal (GhostSkelly.java:108-121, doHurtTarget :80-83). It spawns at night, in Halloween week anywhere and year-round in snowy taiga, taiga, frozen river, jungle and dark forest (GhostSkelly.java:200-213). Read it as a hanged scarecrow adrift: arms and chains swaying on slow, unequal beats, the pumpkin head spinning full circle now and then.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Two arm assemblies swing as rigid units (GhostSkellyModel.java:123-130): the left arm bar, left sleeve and left chain share one roll about z (cos(t x 0.2) x pi x 0.05, 9 deg) and one sway about y (cos(t x 0.24) x pi x 0.05); the right bar, sleeve and chain share a roll at 0.22 and a sway at 0.26. Four unequal beats, so the two sides never keep step.
- Every arm, sleeve and chain part pivots at the rig's origin (PartPose.offset(0, 0, 0), GhostSkellyModel.java:82-110), so the 15-long arm bars and the hanging chains swing from the body's centre line, not from a shoulder.
- The head swivels in one cycle of three (GhostSkellyModel.java:131-144): newangle = cos(t x 0.05) x 2 pi - two full turns out and back over a 125.7-tick (6.28 s) cycle - applied only in a cycle whose boundary roll (1 in 3, entity.getRandom().nextInt(3) == 1 at :122) set the per-entity latch (RenderInfo rf2 / ri2, GhostSkelly.java:51-57); in the other cycles the head sits square (newangle = 0, :127-129). A client-side roll, not a server state.
- The body pole, the shirt and the stem never move; nothing scales with walking speed (limbSwingAmount unused); the head ignores netHeadYaw, so the LookAtPlayerGoal (GhostSkelly.java:67) turns nothing.
- The visible travel is the entity's own drift, not a pose: customServerAiStep eases the motion toward a flight target beside the nearest player or a random air pocket (GhostSkelly.java:123-164), turns the yaw a sixth of the way per tick (:166-169) and damps the vertical motion to 0.65 each tick (:86-91); it hovers with noPhysics (:62).
- Drawn translucent through entityTranslucent (GhostSkellyRenderer.java:49-51) at x1.05 (GhostSkellyRenderer.java:38).

### 4.2 The math

- **left_roll**: the left arm, its sleeve and its chain roll up and down together as one unit about the body's centre line — `larm.zRot = lsleeve.zRot = lchains.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.2) * pi * 0.05` (GhostSkellyModel.java:123-124)
- **right_roll**: the right arm, sleeve and chain roll together at a slightly faster beat than the left — `rarm.zRot = rsleeve.zRot = rchains.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.22) * pi * 0.05` (GhostSkellyModel.java:125-126)
- **left_sway**: the left arm unit also sways fore and aft about the vertical, on its own beat — `larm.yRot = lsleeve.yRot = lchains.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.24) * pi * 0.05` (GhostSkellyModel.java:127-128)
- **right_sway**: the right arm unit sways fore and aft about the vertical, fastest of the four beats — `rarm.yRot = rsleeve.yRot = rchains.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.26) * pi * 0.05` (GhostSkellyModel.java:129-130)
- `GhostSkellyModel.poseFrom` (GhostSkellyModel.java:124-153; orig ModelGhostSkelly.java:100-121 in `render` (func_78088_a, :92-140; func_78087_a at :148-150 is a bare super call); no wingspeed - `new ModelGhostSkelly()` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:410): the left sleeve, chains and arm roll by the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.2, times pi, times 0.05 and yaw by the cosine of the age times 0.24 at the same amplitude; the right sleeve, chains and arm roll by the cosine at 0.22 and yaw by the cosine at 0.26. The head's swivel is the cosine of the age times 0.05, times pi, times 2 (a full turn), gated by a latch on the entity's render scratch: the phase of the age times 0.05 modulo two pi is compared with the last frame's, and when it has wrapped the entity's own random rolls one in three to set the swivel bit; the head yaws by the swivel while the bit is set and holds 0 otherwise. The walk position, the walking speed and the head angles play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (GhostSkellyGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
