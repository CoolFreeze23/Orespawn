## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A hostile four-legged crawler with a segmented shell, a head with an upper and lower beak, three tusks, a glowing core and two belly plates. Read it as a scuttling armoured beast: tusks quivering, the shell breathing, the lower beak chattering.

### 4.2 The math

- **tusks**: the three tusks quiver together — `*tusk.xRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.81) * pi * 0.08` (GammaMetroidModel.setupAnim; GammaMetroidGeoReplacement.java)
- **shell**: the first shell segment breathes slowly — `shell1.xRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.4) * pi * 0.05 / 4` (GammaMetroidModel.setupAnim; GammaMetroidGeoReplacement.java)
- **beak**: the lower beak chatters open (the absolute value: it never closes past its rest) — `beaklower.xRot = |cos(ageInTicks * 0.75) * pi * 0.1| + 0.14` (GammaMetroidModel.setupAnim; GammaMetroidGeoReplacement.java)
- `GammaMetroidModel.setupAnim` (GammaMetroidModel.java; orig ModelGammaMetroid.java:175, 193-197, 204-206 for the lines the port keeps, `render`, wingspeed 0.45 from `new ModelGammaMetroid(0.45f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:429 - the port multiplies by no wingspeed): (1) the three tusks pitch by the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.81, times pi, times 0.08 - 14 degrees each way every 7.76 ticks (orig :175, the left tusk's line; 1.7.10 gave each tusk its own rhythm and a yaw besides); (2) the first shell segment pitches by the cosine of the age times 0.4, times pi, times 0.05, divided by 4 - 2 degrees every 15.7 ticks (orig :193, :197); (3) the lower beak pitches by the absolute value of the cosine of the age times 0.75, times pi, times 0.1, plus 0.14 radians - a chatter between 8 and 26 degrees, twice per 8.38 ticks (orig :204-206). The walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part in the port's pose (1.7.10 also walked the legs on the walking speed, swayed the other shell segments and stilled them while sitting - the register line). The GeckoLib hook is the same (GammaMetroidGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
