## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A giant hostile bee: a five-part curling abdomen ending in a sting, a main body, a neck and head, two flat wings, two three-part antennae with pom tips, and four pincer plates around the mouth. It hunts on the wing. Read it as a hovering wasp: wings shivering, pincers working, antennae drifting, the abdomen curling under - slowly at rest, fast when it strikes.

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: the two flat wings shiver fast about the body's long axis, mirrored — `WingLeft.zRot = -1.745 - cos(ageInTicks * 1.1 * 2.0) * pi * 0.3; WingRight.zRot = 1.754 + (that cosine) * pi * 0.3` (BeeModel.poseFrom (the classic setupAnim); BeeGeoReplacement.java)
- **pincers**: the four pincer plates open and close about the vertical around a +-0.274 rad rest, mirrored — `Left*.yRot = -0.274 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.3 * 2.0) * pi * 0.1; Right*.yRot = 0.274 - (that)` (BeeModel.poseFrom; BeeGeoReplacement.java)
- **left_antenna**: the left antenna's three parts and pom nod together (each part a little more bent than the last) and roll together at a second rhythm — `LA1/LA2/LA3/LeftPom.xRot = 0.261 / 0.436 / 0.611 / 0 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.21 * 2.0) * pi * 0.06; all four .zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.31 * 2.0) * pi * 0.06` (BeeModel.poseFrom; BeeGeoReplacement.java)
- **right_antenna**: the right antenna the same shape at its own two rhythms — `RA1/RA2/RA3/RightPom.xRot = 0.261 / 0.436 / 0.611 / 0 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.27 * 2.0) * pi * 0.06; all four .zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.37 * 2.0) * pi * 0.06` (BeeModel.poseFrom; BeeGeoReplacement.java)
- **abdomen**: the abdomen curls under in a chain: the root segment pitches, each next segment pitches a step further and hangs from the previous one's end; slow and shallow at rest, fast and deep when attacking — `a = attacking ? cos(t * 0.11 * 2.0) * pi * 0.055 : cos(t * 0.021 * 2.0) * pi * 0.023; Abdomnem5.xRot = 1.099 + a; each next .xRot = previous.xRot + a - 0.35; each next (y, z) = previous (y, z) + (cos, sin)(previous.xRot) * length; Sting.z + 1` (BeeModel.poseFrom; BeeGeoReplacement.java)
