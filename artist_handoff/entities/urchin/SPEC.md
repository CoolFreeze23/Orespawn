## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A hostile crystal urchin: seventeen spines on one pivot - a 30-tall central spine, four 25-long and four 20-long spines fanned upward around it, and eight 8-long spines splayed downward as feet (ModelUrchin.java:64-130) - drawn at x1.25. 25 HP, 10 damage, 4 armour, speed 0.3, fire-immune (Urchin.java:82-89; ModEntities.java:172-175). It sheds flame particles (:118-123) and burns in water, hurting itself and smoking (:124-135). On a 1-in-8 tick it picks the nearest living thing in a 16-block box - not the Crystal dimension's other creatures nor a creative player (:164-184, :209-240) - walks at 1.2 and, within 2.8 blocks, raises its attacking flag and one time in seven bites for 10 and five seconds of fire (:105-109, :171-176). It spawns in the dark in the night half of the day with six air blocks around its head (:268-284) and despawns in daylight unless a Crystal Urchin spawner made it (:138-147). Read it as a spiky crystal star: the feet scuttling, the crown of spines slowly turning and swaying, a fast spin when it is about to bite.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (ModelUrchin.java:146-166): the eight downward spines scuttle 27 degrees x the walking speed about their splay rests, the outer four together at 0.7 rad/tick and the inner four at 1.7, 1.65, 1.75 and 1.8 rad/tick, so the feet never fall into step.
- Two branches on the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (Urchin.java:45-46), which the 1-in-8 pass sets to 1 within 2.8 blocks of a target (:171-173) and clears when it has none (:180-182): calm, the central spine creeps round once every 314 ticks and the eight upward spines shimmer 3.6 degrees on eight slow rhythms (0.035-0.08 rad/tick); attacking, the spine spins once every 31 ticks and the crown shivers 11 degrees at ten times the rate (:167-204). Note the flag stays 1 while the target is merely out of reach (:177-179 sets nothing), so the fast spin also plays during the chase.
- There is no head and no look: every part sits on one pivot at y 16. The bite (doHurtTarget, Urchin.java:105-109: 10 and five seconds of fire, one time in seven on the pass) and the water burn (:133-135) draw no pose.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the eight downward spines scuttle: the outer four rock together, the inner four paddle at four slightly different rates — `n = cos(t * 0.7) * pi * 0.15 * amount; n1..n4 = cos(t * 1.7 / 1.65 / 1.75 / 1.8) * pi * 0.15 * amount (all 0 when amount <= 0.1); if1.xRot = 0.261 + n1; if2.xRot = -0.261 - n2; if3.xRot = n3; if4.xRot = -n4; of1.zRot = -0.523 + n; of2.zRot = 0.523 - n; of3.xRot = -0.523 + n; of4.xRot = 0.523 - n` (ModelUrchin.java:146-166; wingspeed 1.0 (:21))
- **spin**: the central spine turns steadily about the vertical - a slow creep at rest, a fast spin while the attacking flag is up — `center.yRot = (t * (attacking ? 0.2 : 0.02)) mod 2pi` (ModelUrchin.java:167-168, :177-178, :188)
- **crown**: the eight upward spines sway on two axes each at eight unrelated rates: a slow shimmer at rest, a fast shiver while attacking — `n1..n8 = cos(t * r_i) * pi * A with (r, A) = calm (0.07, 0.065, 0.075, 0.08, 0.055, 0.045, 0.035, 0.04; 0.02) or attacking (x10; 0.06); tis1.xRot = 0.261 + n1; tis2.xRot = -0.261 + n2; tis3.xRot = n3; tis4.xRot = n4; tis1.zRot = n5; tis2.zRot = n6; tis3.zRot = 0.261 + n7; tis4.zRot = -0.261 + n8; tos1.xRot = -0.532 + n1; tos2.xRot = n7; tos3.xRot = n3; tos4.xRot = 0.532 + n5; tos1.zRot = n4; tos2.zRot = -0.523 + n6; tos3.zRot = 0.523 + n2; tos4.zRot = n8` (ModelUrchin.java:167-204)
