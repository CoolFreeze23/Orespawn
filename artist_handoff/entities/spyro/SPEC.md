## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A tameable baby dragon: a 5x4x10 torso with two back scales, a raked neck to a two-piece head with a jaw, two nostril nubs, three head scales and two two-piece horns, two zero-thickness 10x4 wings on root nubs, four legs of an upper, a lower and a paw, and a tail of a base, a second piece and two tip pieces with a scale (SpyroModel.java:102-285, 37 parts), drawn at x0.75. 200 HP, speed 0.3, 5 damage (EntitySpyro.java:115-120). Beef tames it one time in two (:225-241); its owner can untame it with a dead bush, put its fire out with ice, light it with flint and steel, name it, sit it with any other item, or evolve it into a Dragon with a diamond (:247-328) - one tick in 100,000 it grows up on its own (:167-187). On the ground (activity 1) it wanders, follows its owner and flees monsters (:91-100); in the air (the synched DATA_ACTIVITY at 2, :65-66, :129-136 - a 1-in-8 roll on a 1-in-100 tick, a flying owner, or an owner more than 16 blocks away, :202-205, :349-362) it flies from block target to block target near its owner (:423-473), hunts monsters within 12 blocks (:389-417, :489-507), bites within 3 blocks plus half the target's width for 5 (:405-408) and, within 8 blocks with its fire lit (DATA_SPYRO_FIRE :67-68), shoots small fireballs (:409-411, :480-487); it takes no fall damage (:210-213). It spawns in daylight above y 50 (:579-584; ModEntities.java:566-567). Read it as a dragon hatchling: a bouncy four-legged trot with the wings flapping, the legs tucked in flight, the tail held bent to one side at rest.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Every rhythm's frequency is multiplied by limbSwingAmount (SpyroModel.java:292): the faster it walks, the faster it moves. Above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 the four legs trot 45 degrees x the speed in diagonal pairs (:302-319) and the two wing planes flap 72 degrees x the speed (:295-300); the tail wags 45 degrees at the base and 72 at the tip (:337-351). Standing still the legs and wings freeze and the tail is held cocked 45 degrees to one side - the cosine of zero (:337, :341).
- Two state branches on the synched DATA_ACTIVITY int (EntitySpyro.java:65-66): in flight (2) the legs tuck and hold - the front pair back a radian, the back pair forward a radian (SpyroModel.java:320-335) - while the wings still beat only with the walking speed (:295); a third value (3) halves the wings, stills the legs and straightens the tail (:297, :303-304, :338-339) but the port's entity never sets it (EntitySpyro.java:350-360, :393, :400 write 1 and 2). The sitting pose straightens the tail (:338-340).
- The head - both head pieces, the jaw, the nostrils, the three head scales and both horns - takes the full look yaw and pitch, the horns splayed 0.785 rad (:353-377). The torso, neck, wing root nubs and back scales are static.
- The takeoff is a flag flip (EntitySpyro.java:204, :352-353, :360, :393, :400), the landing a 1-in-100 roll that mostly answers 1 (:351-353), the bite doHurtTarget within 3 blocks plus half the target's width on the flight pass (:405-408), the fireball shootFireball (:480-487) - none of them animation events.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four legs trot in diagonal pairs, each a stiff chain, and the two wing planes flap together, all faster and wider as it walks faster; in the air the legs tuck - the front legs back, the back legs forward - and hold, while the wings still follow only the walking speed — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 2.0 * amount) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0 (activity 3: 0); activity != 2 ? (LegRightFrontTop = a - 0.087, LegRightFrontBottom = a - 0.17, RightFrontPaw = a, the left front = -a + rests, LegRightBackBottom = -a + 0.139, LegRightBackTop = -a - 0.174, RightBackPaw = -a, the left back = a + rests) : (front pieces = -1.0 + rests; back pieces = 1.0 + rests); w = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 2.3 * amount) * pi * 0.4 * amount : 0; activity == 3 ? w /= 2; WingLeft.zRot = w; WingRight.zRot = -w` (SpyroModel.java:295-300 (the wings), :302-335 (the legs))
- **tail**: the tail wags side to side with the walk, the tip swinging wider than the root; standing still it stays cocked to one side; sitting, it straightens — `a = (sitting || activity == 3) ? 0 : cos(t * 1.2 * amount) * pi * 0.25; TailBack.yRot = ScaleTailPiece.yRot = a; TailFront.z = TailBack.z + cos(a) * 3; TailFront.x = TailBack.x + sin(a) * 3 - 0.5; TailFront.yRot = TailPieceLarge.yRot = TailPieceSmall.yRot = a * 1.6; the tip pieces ride TailFront's position` (SpyroModel.java:337-351)
- `SpyroModel.poseFrom` (SpyroModel.java:299-386; orig ModelSpyro.java:285-365 in `render` (func_78088_a, :281-403; func_78087_a at :411-413 is a bare super call); the original's wingspeed 0.65 from `new ModelSpyro(0.65f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:427 is not kept by the port, whose `ws` is the walking speed itself - the register line of the hooks' landing): every rhythm's frequency is multiplied by the walking speed. While the walking speed is above a tenth the wing swing is the cosine of the age in ticks times 2.3 times the walking speed, times pi, times 0.4, times the walking speed, else 0, halved while the activity is 3 (flying); the left wing rolls by it and the right by its negative. The leg swing is likewise the cosine of the age times 2.0 times the walking speed, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed above the threshold, else 0, and 0 while flying: unless the activity is 2, the right front top, bottom and paw pitch -0.087, -0.17 and 0 plus it, the left front pieces the same rests minus it, the right back bottom, top and paw 0.139, -0.174 and 0 minus it and the left back pieces the same rests plus it; at activity 2 the front pieces sit at those rests plus -1 and the back pieces at theirs plus 1. The tail angle is the cosine of the age times 1.2 times the walking speed, times pi, times 0.25, 0 while sitting or flying: the back tail link and the tail scale yaw by it; the front link, the large and the small tail pieces yaw by 1.6 times it and are placed from the back link's pivot (the bind) 3 units along the cosine (z) and sine (x) of the angle, less 0.5 in x. The two head pieces, the jaw, the two snout pieces, the top scale, the head scale and the back-head scale yaw by the look yaw in radians and pitch by the look pitch in radians; the four horn pieces yaw by the look yaw plus 0.785 (right) or minus 0.785 (left) and pitch by the look pitch minus 0.785. The walk position plays no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (SpyroGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
