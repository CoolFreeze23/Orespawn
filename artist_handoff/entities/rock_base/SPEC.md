## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A mineable rock disguised as a creature. On its first tick it rolls a type (twelve of them: plain, small, coloured, spiky, TNT and four crystal kinds; the Crystal dimension forces the crystal types), shows the cube set for that type, and sits there with a fixed yaw until it is broken for its drops. It never moves and never turns.

## 11. What 'done' looks like for this entity

1. `rockbase.geo.json` returned UNCHANGED (or not at all): every bone name, parent, pivot, rotation and cube as listed in §3 and the shipped file (a `locked` bone renamed, re-parented or deleted is refused by name).
2. No animation file (this tier takes no artist clips; an `rockbase.animation.json` with zero clips is tolerated); texture edits only, if any.
3. Textures at their canvas sizes under the canonical names of §8 (aliases are fanned out on import).
4. Optionally the `.bbmodel` working file. `tools/artist_package.py check <your folder>` must pass before hand-in. It REJECTS: a missing or empty folder, or a missing manifest; no `.animation.json`, one under the wrong name (the sheet names the file), or a second one; a clip not in the sheet (renamed or added; `idle_alt_N` only where the sheet allows it), or a clip delivered for a Tier-3 creature; a wrong `loop` value; a missing `idle` or `walk` (they open the game's switch only TOGETHER — one without the other leaves the creature on its code-driven motion); a missing clip the game already triggers by name; a key on a bone the rig does not have (renamed), or a channel other than rotation / position / scale; a Molang string, a non-numeric or non-finite value, a custom-instruction (`timeline`) key; an event keyframe (`sound_effects` / `particle_effects`) on a LOOPING clip of a phase-locked species — no event keyframes on loops; code-fired events come from the trigger inventory (one-shot clips may carry them; a creature whose controllers are its own GeckoLib controllers — the SPEC's controller kind `native`, the Queen — is exempt: native controllers are not phase-locked, event keys fire per loop); |rotation| > 3600 degrees or |position| > 1024 units; a key later than the clip's `animation_length` (a declared length shorter than the last key); a returned `.geo.json` whose bones, parents, pivots, rotations, cubes, UVs or canvas differ from the shipped rig; a `locked` bone renamed, re-parented or deleted in a returned `.geo.json` (the finding names the bone); a texture that is not this creature's, or whose canvas size changed; a `*_reference_<state>.animation.json` returned under its own name that is not the package's own untouched copy (the reference-only clips the package carries beside the sheet: the classic code sampled at fixed inputs, never shipped — the finding names the file; the untouched copy coming back is warned, not a delivery; copying a reference clip's KEYS into `idle` / `walk` / `aggro_idle` is a delivery and passes). It WARNS on: a key on a `locked` bone (the clip and the bones are named) — Keying a locked bone is allowed; the checker warns, and the hitbox part follows the bone in-game (the consequence, so keep such keys deliberate). Renaming, re-parenting or deleting a locked bone is refused; a `_preview` file delivered (a Blockbench-only aid, never in the jar — ruled 2026-09-06, Q15 (a); leave it out of the delivery); a returned `.geo.json` at all (the shipped rig is used regardless — do not re-export it); a clip length far from the one the sheet states (rule 5: a phase-locked creature's loops at 1.0 s — ruled 2026-09-06, Q14 (a); a native creature's clips at their shipped length; a one-shot at the length its row states); an optional clip not delivered; an alias-named texture (the canonical name is preferred); a `format_version` other than 1.8.0; a number written as a string; an unknown keyframe key; a clip without `animation_length`; an unreadable `.bbmodel`.
