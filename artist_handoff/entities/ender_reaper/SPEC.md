## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A hostile skeletal wraith with a scythe: a bare spine, pelvis and tailbone with no legs at all, a ribcage of forty rib and vertebra pieces, two bony arms hung from shoulder blocks, the right hand carrying a 39-long scythe with a bladed head, two wings of two spars and a zero-thickness sail each, and a boxy skull (ModelEnderReaper.java:161-423, 66 parts). 90 HP, 18 damage, 8 armour, speed 0.37 (EnderReaper.java:199-207). It is an enderman's heir: it hunts only the player who looks at it (the pumpkin-stare test :184-197 selects the target :87-141), screams while it holds that stare (DATA_SCREAMING :39-40, set at :116 and on a hit at :341), teleports away from a starer within 4 blocks (:273-277) and toward a far target it has held 30 ticks (:278-280), flees daylight (:245-254) and water or fire (:258-261), drowns in rain (:226-228) and sheds portal particles (:230-239); a melee goal bites at 18 (:54). It spawns in the dark at night above y 30 with no other reaper within 16 blocks (:366-374), through the dark forest, highlands and lowlands biome modifiers and in the Chaos and Island biomes. Read it as a floating skeleton: no stride, a hover, the wings and the scythe carrying the mood.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Two branches on the synched DATA_SCREAMING boolean (EnderReaper.java:39-40; ModelEnderReaper.java:434-445). Calm, the left forearm is held back and up (xRot -2.436, yRot 1.0, :442-443) and the six wing parts stir 11 degrees about their 45-degree rest at 0.161 rad/tick, a 39-tick period (:444, :446-449). Screaming, the forearm snaps forward (-0.436 / -0.488, :438-439), the wings beat 54 degrees at 0.621 rad/tick, a 10-tick beat (:440), and the scythe swings 45 degrees each way about 1.0 rad at 0.437 rad/tick (:435-437).
- The walk moves only the scythe: above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 the shaft and both blade planes dip from 1.0 rad by |cos(t x 0.299)| x 0.785 x the walking speed (:431-433) — a one-sided dip at a 10.5-tick period, since the absolute value halves the cosine's period; still, the shaft rests at 1.0 rad. Screaming overrides the dip with the full swing (:436).
- The skull yaws with the look at 0.45 of the head yaw, clamped to ±0.45 rad (:450-456); it never pitches. Everything else — the spine, pelvis, tailbone and neck, the forty rib and vertebra pieces, both shoulder blocks, the whole right arm and the left upper arm — is static (:169-391).
- The rig has no legs: it stands on a tailbone (:177-179) and moves at 0.37 (EnderReaper.java:203), so its walk reads as a glide. The flag goes up on the first tick of a held stare (:116) and on any hit (:341); it drops when the nearest player looks away (:121), at the daylight escape (:251), when wet or burning (:259) and whenever the target is gone (:282) — a held state, not a pulse.
- The teleports (:289-336) are instant moves with a sound pair at the origin and the landing (:314-315) and a portal trail — no pose today; the scream is the ambient sound while the flag is up (:351-353).

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: the six wing parts sweep together about the vertical: a slow 11-degree stir when calm, a wide 54-degree beat while screaming — `w = screaming ? cos(t * 2.7 * 0.23) * pi * 0.3 : cos(t * 0.7 * 0.23) * pi * 0.06; lwing1.yRot = lwing2.yRot = lwing3.yRot = 0.785 + w; rwing1.yRot = rwing2.yRot = rwing3.yRot = -0.785 - w` (ModelEnderReaper.java:434-449; wingspeed 0.23 (:20))
- **scythe**: the scythe rolls at the right hand: a one-sided dip with the stride when calm, a full swing either way while screaming — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3 * 0.23) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; scythe1.zRot = scythe2.zRot = scythe3.zRot = 1.0 - |a|; screaming: scythe1/2/3.zRot = 1.0 + cos(t * 1.9 * 0.23) * pi * 0.25` (ModelEnderReaper.java:431-437; wingspeed 0.23 (:20))
- **left_arm**: the left forearm holds one of two poses: back and raised when calm, thrust forward while screaming — `larm1.xRot, larm1.yRot = screaming ? (-0.436, -0.488) : (-2.436, 1.0)` (ModelEnderReaper.java:438-439, :442-443)
