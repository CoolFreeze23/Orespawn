## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A small, round forest animal with a blocky head, buck teeth and a broad flat tail. It waddles on four stubby feet in a brisk trot, chews constantly whether it is walking or not, and slowly rocks its tail. It is timid: it keeps away from monsters and from players, bolts in a panic when hurt, floats in water, and pairs up to breed (the baby is the same rig at half size). Its one job is wood: it looks for logs nearby and chews them down with a chainsaw sound. It never attacks. Read it as a busy, nervous little worker — quick feet, restless jaw, calm tail.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Three independent rhythms run at once: a fast trot on the feet (scaled by walking speed), a chew on the teeth and a slow tail rock; nothing else moves (the head and nose are static).
- Standing still, the feet hang at the bind pose while the teeth and tail keep going: today's 'idle' is the walk at zero speed plus the free-running chew and rock (contract §2.1).
- The head does not track the player: the classic model has no head-yaw channel.
- The default renderer poses it from the classic code; the GeckoLib candidate (behind the dev switch) plays the shipped keyframe transcription of that code since 2026-09-12 (walk, walk_teeth, walk_tail; its idle keys no bone - the always-on teeth and tail are the resting motion) under the modern config, and your clips replace that transcription file for file.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four feet swing fore-and-aft as a trot: right-front with left-rear, left-front with right-rear (the opposite sign); the swing shrinks to nothing when the beaver stands still — `rff.xRot = lrf.xRot = cos(ageInTicks * 3.7) * pi * 0.45 * limbSwingAmount; lff.xRot = rrf.xRot = -(that)` (ModelBeaver.java:90-101; BeaverGeoReplacement.java:52-63)
- **teeth**: the teeth chew continuously, walking or standing — `teeth.xRot = cos(ageInTicks * 2.7) * pi * 0.25` (ModelBeaver.java:100; BeaverGeoReplacement.java:61)
- **tail**: the flat tail rocks slowly up and down, one rock every 12.6 ticks (0.63 s) — `tail.xRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.5) * pi * 0.05` (ModelBeaver.java:101; BeaverGeoReplacement.java:62)
