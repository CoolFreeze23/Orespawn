## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A wheeled gun cart: four wheels on an axle under a drive box, a stand and swivel carrying two barrels and an ammunition box. It spots targets in a huge 30-block box, plants itself and fires a laser ball every 20 ticks out to thirty blocks, and will not shuffle inside six blocks. The wheels roll with the distance travelled; the turret turns with its gaze.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Axle, drive box, stand and swivel are static.
- Wheels: distance-driven (limbSwing), so a keyframe clip cannot reproduce them — Amendment 1 point 2 keeps this rig code-driven under any policy.

### 4.2 The math

- **wheels**: the wheels roll with the distance travelled and stop dead when the cart stops — `w = amount > 0.1 ? |limbSwing*0.15 mod 2pi| : 0; lwheel1/rwheel1.xRot = w; lwheel2/rwheel2.xRot = w + 0.7854` (ModelRobot5.java:102-130; Robot5GeoReplacement.java:33-46)
- **turret**: barrels and ammo box turn with the gaze at half rate — `barrel1/barrel2/ammobox.yRot = rad(netHeadYaw / 2)` (Robot5GeoReplacement.java:47-50)
- `ModelRobot5.setupAnim` (ModelRobot5.java:102-119; orig ModelRobot5.java:98-114, `render`): while the walking speed is above a tenth, the wheel angle is the walk position times 0.15, wrapped at 2 pi and made positive — a rolling turn every 41.9 units of walk position (the port reads limbSwing where the original's `f2 * 0.15f` read the age in ticks) — the two first wheels pitch by that angle and the two second wheels by that angle plus 0.7853982 radians (45 degrees ahead); below a tenth every wheel holds 0; the two barrels and the ammunition box yaw by half the head yaw, in radians (orig :113-114 `f3 / 2.0`). The GeckoLib hook is the same (Robot5GeoReplacement.java:26-47).
