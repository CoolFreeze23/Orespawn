## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A cassowary: a body angled up with a tail behind, a neck base and a long neck bent up and back over it, a head with a beak and a casque crest on top, a wattle ('gobbler') under the throat, and two legs with feet (ModelCassowary.java:39-117; 12 parts on a 64x32 sheet). It is a skittish bird: 10 HP, speed 0.25, an unused 8-damage attribute (Cassowary.java:55-60) - no goal strikes. It floats (:45), breeds on crystal apples (:46, :93-96), runs from monsters AND players (:47-48), panics when hurt (:49), watches other mobs (:50) and wanders (:51); it spawns by day (:104-109) and a chick is drawn at half size (CassowaryRenderer.java:30-31). Read it as a nervous flightless bird: a quick two-legged stride with the neck and wattle bobbing at twice the step, frozen still when it stops.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Everything moves only above a walking speed of 0.1 (ModelCassowary.java:124-130); standing, the bird is frozen - no breath, no head turn (netHeadYaw unused, so the LookAtPlayerGoal at Cassowary.java:50 turns nothing).
- The gait (:125, :131-134): the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.55 (wingspeed, :24), x pi x 0.15, x the walking speed swings the left leg and foot forward and the right leg and foot back - 27 degrees at full stride every 8.8 ticks (the code pairs leg1 with foot2 and leg2 with foot1: they share pivots, so each pair is one leg).
- The neck bobs at twice the stride rate (:126, :135-136): -2.827 rad plus the cosine of the age x 2.6 x 0.55, x pi x 0.1, x the walking speed - 18 degrees every 4.4 ticks - and the wattle takes the same angle around 0.
- The head, beak and crest have no rotation of their own: the code places all three 7 units along the neck's pitch (:137-142), so they ride the bob as a position - an FK step written by hand on flat bones (when the rig lands, key their positions with the neck or parent them to it).
- The body, tail and neck base never move; there is no attacking flag and no strike.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride in opposition, each foot swinging with its leg — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.55) * pi * 0.15 * limbSwingAmount : 0; leg1.xRot = foot2.xRot = a; leg2.xRot = foot1.xRot = -a` (ModelCassowary.java:124-134)
- **neck_bob**: the neck and the wattle bob twice per stride and the head, beak and crest ride up and down on the end of the neck; still when standing — `b = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 2.6 * 0.55) * pi * 0.1 * limbSwingAmount : 0; neck.xRot = -2.827 + b; gobbler.xRot = b; crest.z = beak.z = head.z = neck.z + sin(neck.xRot) * 7; crest.y = beak.y = head.y = neck.y + cos(neck.xRot) * 7` (ModelCassowary.java:126, 135-142)
