## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A night-flying moth on the shared butterfly rig: a one-cube body bar, a one-cube head, and four flat wing panels a side — inner and outer forewings, a hindwing and a long tail streamer (ButterflyModel.createBodyLayer, ButterflyModel.java:54-102), drawn half again as large as the butterfly in one of four moth skins (LunaMothRenderer.java:23, :39-43). It is a butterfly subclass (EntityLunaMoth.java:24) whose flight goal seeks torches when it is sheltered from the sky (LunaMothFlightGoal, registered at :55) and still carries the Islands hunt of its parent, so in the Islands it nips prey for 1.0 on a 1-in-2 roll (EntityButterfly.doHurtTarget, EntityButterfly.java:161-165). It hovers a little heavier than the butterfly (the vertical drag at tick :58-63), spawns only at night, in the air, above y 50 or anywhere in the Islands (checkSpawnRules :66-73) in jungle and taiga swarms (the swarm_luna_moth biome modifiers), and twenty of them burst from Mothra when she dies (Mothra.java:468-475). Read it as a soft, slower flutter than the butterfly's.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One rhythm runs at all times: the eight wing panels roll about the body's long axis at 0.975 rad/tick (a 6.44-tick beat — the butterfly's 1.3 scaled by this renderer's wingspeed 0.75, LunaMothRenderer.java:29), 45 degrees each way, the left set mirroring the right (ButterflyModel.java:109-115). The body and the head are static; the walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part.
- The flight is the entity's: LunaMothFlightGoal (EntityLunaMoth.java:55) steers the delta movement toward torches when sheltered and wanders on the butterfly preset otherwise, and tick :58-63 damps the vertical speed by 0.6 each tick. The model never banks or bobs the body with that motion.
- The renderer draws the head, then the body, then the wing pairs inner to outer (renderToBuffer, ButterflyModel.java:119-130).
- No `attack` row: the moth's only strike is the Islands nip it inherits — LunaMothFlightGoal extends ButterflyIslandsHuntGoal (LunaMothFlightGoal.java:43), whose :83 calls EntityButterfly.doHurtTarget (EntityButterfly.java:161-165) — and the trigger inventory, which reads the entity file and the goal file it names, does not see the parent goal's call; the owner decides whether the nip earns a strike clip when the rig lands.

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: the eight wing panels flap about the body's long axis together, 45 degrees up and down, the left side mirroring the right, at three quarters of the butterfly's rate; nothing else moves — `rightwing.zRot = rightwing2.zRot = rightwing3.zRot = rightwing4.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.75) * pi * 0.25; leftwing*.zRot = -rightwing.zRot` (ButterflyModel.java:109-115 (wingspeed 0.75 from LunaMothRenderer.java:29))
