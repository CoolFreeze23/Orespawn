## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A night phantom: three eight-bladed fans crossed at right angles, spinning in place while the machine drifts through blocks to random points, sheds sparks, aims a spark stream at whoever it is hunting and hurts on contact. Arrows cannot hurt it. It dissolves in daylight unless a spawner made it. Pure spin — the fans turn two degrees every rendered frame, forever.

### 4.2 The math

- **fans**: fan 1 spins about X, fan 2 about Y, fan 3 about Z, all at the same angle; the angle advances after every draw — `shape1__fan.xRot = rad(rf1); shape2__fan.yRot = rad(rf1); shape3__fan.zRot = rad(rf1); rf1 += 2 per frame, wrap > 359 -> 0 (RenderInfo.rf1)` (RotatorModel.java:54-75,76-95; RotatorGeoReplacement.java:56-66)
- `RotatorGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations` (RotatorGeoReplacement.java:58-67) and the classic `RotatorModel.renderToBuffer` (RotatorModel.java:93-118; orig ModelRotator.java:44-78, `render`): no angle is computed from the inputs — the pose reads the per-entity `RenderInfo.rf1` in degrees and turns fan 1 about X, fan 2 about Y and fan 3 about Z by it (each fan is one blade drawn eight times at 0.7853982-radian steps, orig :53-57 / :61-65 / :69-73), then advances `rf1` by 2 degrees and wraps it to 0 once past 359 (RotatorModel.advanceFanSpin :85-90; orig :75-78) — once per draw, so the spin is per rendered frame, not per tick (ENT-S-147); the walk, head and attack inputs play no part.
