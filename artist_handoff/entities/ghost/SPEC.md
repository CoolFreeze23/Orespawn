## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A hovering ghost of the Halloween nights: one 6x21x6 head-and-body column and two 2x11x2 arms splayed 0.33 rad (19 deg) at the shoulders (GhostModel.java:38-49), drawn translucent (GhostRenderer.java:38-40). It spawns at night, in Halloween week anywhere and year-round in snowy taiga, taiga, frozen river, jungle and dark forest (Ghost.java:196-209). Read it as a sheet ghost: a still column, two arms drifting slowly and never quite together.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Four slow cosines on the two arms and nothing else (GhostModel.java:56-59): the left arm rolls about z at 0.30 rad/tick around a -0.33 rad (19 deg) splay and the right at 0.32 around +0.33; the left arm swings about x at 0.34 around -0.33 and the right at 0.36 around +0.33 - each pi x 0.05 = 9 deg either way.
- The four rates are deliberately unequal (periods 20.9, 19.6, 18.5 and 17.5 ticks), so the arms drift in and out of step over the minutes rather than waving in unison; the head-and-body column is static - no line of setupAnim touches it.
- Nothing scales with walking speed (limbSwingAmount is unused, GhostModel.java:54) and nothing turns toward the player (netHeadYaw and headPitch are unused) even though a LookAtPlayerGoal runs (Ghost.java:55).
- The visible travel is the entity's own drift, not a pose: customServerAiStep eases the motion toward a flight target beside the nearest player or a random air pocket (Ghost.java:119-160), turns the yaw a sixth of the way per tick (:162-165) and damps the vertical motion to 0.65 each tick (:76-78); it hovers with noPhysics (:50) and never takes fall damage (:186-188).
- Drawn translucent through entityTranslucent (GhostRenderer.java:38-40) at x0.65 (GhostRenderer.java:45).

### 4.2 The math

- **larm_roll**: the left arm rolls slowly in and out at the shoulder around its splayed rest — `LArm.zRot = -0.33 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.3) * pi * 0.05` (GhostModel.java:56)
- **rarm_roll**: the right arm rolls in and out at its own, slightly faster rate — `RArm.zRot = 0.33 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.32) * pi * 0.05` (GhostModel.java:57)
- **larm_swing**: the left arm swings fore and aft around a rest pitched 19 deg back — `LArm.xRot = -0.33 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.34) * pi * 0.05` (GhostModel.java:58)
- **rarm_swing**: the right arm swings fore and aft around a rest pitched 19 deg forward, faster still — `RArm.xRot = 0.33 + cos(ageInTicks * 0.36) * pi * 0.05` (GhostModel.java:59)
