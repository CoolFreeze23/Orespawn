## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A giant hostile beetle: a two-part body, an eight-part head, a nine-part horn and jaw assembly, and six three-part legs. It charges and bites. Read it as an armoured beetle: the legs sweeping with its stride, the horn and jaws working - slowly at rest, fast when it attacks.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the six legs sweep about the vertical with the walk, every part of a leg together: the left front and right rear pairs one way, the left rear and right front the other, the middle pair opposite — `a = cos(t * 1.0 * 0.45) * pi * 0.12 * amount; lfleg*/rrleg*.yRot = 0.349 + a; lmleg*/rmleg*.yRot = -a; lrleg*/rfleg*.yRot = -0.349 + a` (HerculesBeetleModel.poseFrom; HerculesBeetleGeoReplacement.java)
- **jaws**: the nine horn and jaw parts nod together: a slow shallow breathing at rest, a fast wide chomp when attacking — `a = attacking ? cos(t*0.51*1.0)*pi*0.07 : cos(t*0.051*1.0)*pi*0.01; jaw1/2/5/6.xRot = 0.122 + a; jaw3/4/7/8.xRot = 0 + a; jaw9.xRot = 0.314 + a` (HerculesBeetleModel.poseFrom; HerculesBeetleGeoReplacement.java)
- `HerculesBeetleModel.poseFrom` (HerculesBeetleModel.java:301-323, called from `setupAnim`; orig ModelHerculesBeetle.java:286-302, `render`, wingspeed 1.0 from `new ModelHerculesBeetle(1.0f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:489): (1) the leg sweep is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.0 times 0.45, times pi, times 0.12, times the walking speed (up to 22 degrees each way every 14 ticks; no threshold): the three parts of the left front leg and of the right rear leg yaw by 0.349 radians plus it, the left middle and right middle by its negative, the left rear and right front by -0.349 plus it (orig :286-292); (2) the jaw angle - at rest the cosine of the age times 0.051 times 1.0, times pi, times 0.01 (2 degrees every 123 ticks); attacking the cosine of the age times 0.51 times 1.0, times pi, times 0.07 (13 degrees every 12.3 ticks) - jaws 1, 2, 5 and 6 pitch by 0.122 radians plus it, jaws 3, 4, 7 and 8 by it, jaw 9 by 0.314 plus it (orig :293-302). The walk position and the head angles play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (HerculesBeetleGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
