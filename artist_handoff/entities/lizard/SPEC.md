## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A big sprawling lizard with a sail: three body blocks, a neck carrying an upper jaw with three nose pieces, two angled eyes, two crest ('hat') pieces and eleven upper teeth, a hinged lower jaw with six teeth, four two-part legs with feet and two toes each, a five-segment tail, and along the back a fan of eight fin pieces with seven ridge spines (LizardModel.java createBodyLayer; 71 parts on a 128x128 sheet). It is a hunter rather than a monster: 30 HP, 6 damage (Lizard.java:95-100) - every tenth think tick it looks 12 blocks around for attack squids, spiders, cave spiders and chickens (:134-146, :155-159) and bites within 3.5 blocks (:223-227, doHurtTarget :116-118), raising a synched attacking flag while prey is close (:224, cleared :235). A cod makes it your buddy for 3000-5000 ticks (:121-132); it wanders a lot (:83), floats (:79), seeks water when dry (:202-215), heals slowly (:217), shrugs off cactus (:107), breeds on cod (:81, :261) and has a half-size baby (LizardRenderer.java:41-42). It spawns above y 50 (:272-274). Read it as a monitor lizard: a splayed, wiggling stride, a lazy tail that turns into a lash when prey is close, a jaw that chomps.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (LizardModel.java:533-553): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 1.0 x 0.65 (wingspeed, :21), x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed (45 degrees at full stride every 9.7 ticks) YAWS the upper legs, feet and toes - both front legs one way, both back legs the other, a sprawling wiggle - and PITCHES the lower legs in diagonal pairs (front left and back right forward while front right and back left go back). Below the threshold the legs snap to their rests.
- The lower jaw and its six teeth (LizardModel.java:554-560) hold 0.25 rad (14 deg) open when calm and chomp when the attacking flag is up: 0.52 rad plus the cosine of the age x 0.45 (no wingspeed) x 0.35 - 20 degrees either way around a 30-degree gape every 14 ticks.
- The five-segment tail sways (LizardModel.java:561-577): calm, the cosine of the age x 0.25 x 0.65 x pi x 0.05, attacking the cosine of the age x 1.25 x 0.65 x pi x 0.35 - each segment takes 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0 and 1.25 of it, so the tip swings five times as far as the base (11 degrees calm, 79 attacking), and each next segment's position is re-derived along the previous segment's yaw (12, 9, 7, 7 units): an FK chain written by hand on flat bones - when the rig lands a keyframed sway must key the positions too, or the tail must be parented.
- The head follows the look yaw with no pitch (LizardModel.java:578-659): the neck takes a quarter of netHeadYaw; the upper jaw and everything on it - the five nose pieces, the two eyes (with +-0.78 rad offsets), ten upper teeth and the two crest pieces - take half of it, placed 2 units along the neck's yaw; the lower jaw and its teeth take the same half yaw placed 3 units along. Twenty-eight parts are moved by copying the jaw's position and yaw one by one.
- The three body blocks, the eight sail fins and the seven ridge spines never move; the attacking flag is held server-side while prey is within 3.5 blocks (Lizard.java:223-224) and cleared when no prey is found (:235), so it is a state, not a pulse.
- The renderer halves the scale for a baby (LizardRenderer.java:41-42); water and buddy-following are navigation, not pose (Lizard.java:202-215, :239-241).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the splayed stride: both front upper legs, feet and toes yaw one way and both back ones the other, while the lower legs pitch in diagonal pairs (front left with back right) — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.0 * 0.65) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount : 0; TopFront*Leg / Front*Foot / Toe1,2,3,8 .yRot = a; TopBack*Leg / Back*Foot / Toe4-7 .yRot = -a; BottomFrontLeftLeg.xRot = a; BottomFrontRightLeg.xRot = -a; BottomBackLeftLeg.xRot = -a; BottomBackRightLeg.xRot = a` (LizardModel.java:533-553)
- **jaw**: the lower jaw and its six teeth hang slightly open at rest and chomp wide when prey is close — `BottomJaw.xRot = attacking ? 0.52 + cos(t * 0.45) * 0.35 : 0.25; Tooth9/12/13/14/15/16.xRot = BottomJaw.xRot` (LizardModel.java:554-560)
- **tail**: a lazy side-to-side sway of the whole tail, each segment swinging wider than the one before; when prey is close it becomes a fast, wide lash — `n = attacking ? cos(t * 1.25 * 0.65) * pi * 0.35 : cos(t * 0.25 * 0.65) * pi * 0.05; TailBase1.yRot = n * 0.25; Tail2.yRot = n * 0.5; Tail3.yRot = n * 0.75; Tail4.yRot = n * 1.0; TailTip.yRot = n * 1.25; each segment's x/z placed along the previous segment's yaw (12, 9, 7, 7 units)` (LizardModel.java:561-577)
