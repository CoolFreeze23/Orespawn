## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A small hostile dragon: two horns, a snout, a head with a jaw, a neck, a body, two flat wings, a four-part tail and four two-part legs. It flaps about and snaps. Read it as a hand-sized dragon: wings beating, jaw chattering, the front and back legs trotting.

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: the two flat wings beat about the body's long axis, mirrored — `wing1.zRot = -2.0 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.3) * pi * 0.25; wing2.zRot = 2.0 - (that)` (TerribleTerrorModel.setupAnim; TerribleTerrorGeoReplacement.java)
- **jaw**: the jaw chatters open (the absolute value: never past closed) — `jaw.xRot = |cos(ageInTicks * 0.3) * pi * 0.1|` (TerribleTerrorModel.setupAnim; TerribleTerrorGeoReplacement.java)
- **legs**: the four upper legs trot: front 2 and back 1 forward while front 1 and back 2 swing back — `fl21.xRot = 0.349 + a; fl11.xRot = 0.349 - a; bl21.xRot = -0.349 - a; bl11.xRot = -0.349 + a; a = cos(ageInTicks * 1.25) * pi * 0.35` (TerribleTerrorModel.setupAnim; TerribleTerrorGeoReplacement.java)
- `TerribleTerrorModel.setupAnim` (TerribleTerrorModel.java; orig ModelTerribleTerror.java:171-177, 179, 181, 183 for the lines the port keeps, `render`, wingspeed 1.0 from `new ModelTerribleTerror()` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:457): (1) the wings roll by the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.3, times pi, times 0.25 - 45 degrees each way every 4.83 ticks - the left around -2.0 radians (plus), the right around 2.0 (minus) (orig :171-173); (2) the jaw pitches by the absolute value of the cosine of the age times 0.3, times pi, times 0.1 - a chatter between 0 and 18 degrees twice per 20.9 ticks (orig :174-175); (3) the leg swing is the cosine of the age times 1.25, times pi, times 0.35 (63 degrees every 5.03 ticks): front leg 2 pitches by 0.349 radians plus it, front leg 1 by 0.349 minus it, back leg 2 by -0.349 minus it, back leg 1 by -0.349 plus it (orig :176-177, :179, :181, :183; 1.7.10 also swung the four lower legs and chained the tail - the register line).
