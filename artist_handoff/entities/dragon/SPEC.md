## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Dragon is a tameable, rideable fire- or water-breathing dragon: a three-block body with three back spikes, a two-piece neck to a horned head with an upper and a hinged lower jaw, two wings of three spars and three membrane panels each on a root panel, four legs — two-piece front legs with feet, three-piece hind legs with feet — and a seven-segment spiked tail ending in a fin (ModelDragon.createBodyLayer, ModelDragon.java:136-415; one tail segment is named 'neck1' by the model's author). It has 200 HP and 35 attack (createAttributes, Dragon.java:173-178; double against the Kraken, doHurtTarget :285-302, knockback 1.75). On the ground it sits when told, follows its owner, is tempted by beef, wanders and looks about (goals :140-147) and hunts monsters, Mothra and the Kraken — never players (:942-957); a 1-in-10 roll with prey in sight, a far owner or a rare roll sends it into the air (customServerAiStep :394-401, tick :381-386, handlePassiveBehaviors :873-894). Flying (DATA_ACTIVITY 1, no gravity, :379) it steers itself to a flight target, and on a 1-in-9 roll with a target raises the attacking flag and bites within five blocks plus half the target's width or, within sixteen and with fire, shoots — a small or big fireball, a water ball or an ice ball by its type and fire level (handleAIFlight :670-706, shootProjectileAt :808-860); a tamed one below a quarter health flees (:676-683) and seeks water to heal (:897-908). Beef tames it one time in five (:974-991); an owner mounts it with an empty hand and rides it on the client-predicted RiderFlightController, firing with the strafe keys (:1001-1005, tickRidden :450-455, handleRiderProjectiles :504-567), and gives it fire with flint and steel or gunpowder, takes it with ice, and changes its type with a water or lava bucket (:1040-1096). Being hurt wakes it into flight and turns it on the attacker (hurt :305-344). It spawns in the Island biome (island_biome.json) by day, with no other Dragon within 16 blocks, anywhere in the Islands or else above y 50 (checkSpawnRules :1242-1249). Read it as a real dragon: a heavy four-legged walker on the ground, and a wide slow wing-beat with the legs tucked in the air.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- On the ground the four legs stride diagonally at 1.25 rad/tick (a 5-tick period), each leg a stiff chain, the swing scaled by the entity's TRUE horizontal speed (from its last position, ModelDragon.java:425-432) rather than by limbSwingAmount; in flight (DATA_ACTIVITY 1, Dragon.java:66) every leg tucks one radian and holds (:435-450).
- The wings branch three ways (:469-477): in flight they beat 50 degrees at 0.75 rad/tick (an 8.4-tick beat), the middle spars 4/3 of that and the tips 3/2, each placed at the end of the spar before (:479-513); on the ground they hang folded at −0.85 with a 5-degree stir, and while the attacking flag is set they half-raise to −0.45 and flap 36 degrees at 0.85 rad/tick.
- The tail sways in phase along seven segments, the swing growing toward the tip: 0.45 wide at 0.76 rad/tick in calm flight, 0.75 wide at 0.96 attacking, 0.22 wide at 0.22 on the ground, still in the sitting pose (:516-570). The neck turns a quarter and a half of the look and the head three quarters, the jaws and horns riding it (:592-616); in flight the look is replaced by a filtered turn rate clamped to ±50 degrees (:577-587). The lower jaw chomps 25 degrees at 1.5 rad/tick while attacking and rests at 0.07 (:618-619).
- The body blocks and the three back spikes are static; the walk position plays no part.
- The takeoff and the landing are flag flips (customServerAiStep :394-401, handlePassiveBehaviors :873-894, hurt :315, mobInteract :1004), the bite is doHurtTarget (:285-302) from the flight AI (:693) or the ridden auto-melee (:591), the shot is shootProjectileAt (:808-860) or the rider's strafe keys (:504-567), and the wing sound every 20 ticks in flight (:356-370) is the entity's — none of these are animation events.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: on the ground the four legs stride diagonally with the real speed, each leg a stiff chain; in the air they all tuck and hold — `a = amount > 0.001 ? cos(t * 1.25) * pi * |velocity_xz| * 0.6 : 0; activity != 0 ? (leg4 = 0.557 - 1, leg5 = -0.557 - 1, foot2 = -1, leg3 = 0.557 - 1, leg6 = -0.557 - 1, foot1 = -1, leg2 = -0.632 + 1, leg7 = 0.89 + 1, leg10 = -0.557 + 1, foot3 = 1, leg1 = -0.632 + 1, leg9 = 0.89 + 1, leg11 = -0.557 + 1, foot4 = 1) : (leg4/leg5/foot2 = rest + a; leg3/leg6/foot1 = rest - a; leg2/leg7/leg10/foot3 = rest - a; leg1/leg9/leg11/foot4 = rest + a)` (ModelDragon.java:425-466; ANIM_SPEED 1.0 (:20))
- **wings**: the wings beat wide and slow in flight, hang folded with a faint stir on the ground, and half-raise and flap while attacking on the ground; the beat widens from root to tip — `w = activity != 0 ? cos(t * 0.75) * pi * 0.28 : (attacking ? -0.45 + cos(t * 0.85) * pi * 0.2 : -0.85 + cos(t * 0.2) * pi * 0.028); wing1.zRot = wing15.zRot = w; wing3.zRot = wing8.zRot = 4w/3; wing2.zRot = wing7.zRot = wing9.zRot = 3w/2; wing4/wing14 = -w; wing5/wing10 = -4w/3; wing6/wing11/wing12 = -3w/2; positions along the spars by sin/cos x 7 and 6` (ModelDragon.java:469-513)
- **tail**: the tail sways side to side, the swing growing toward the tip — brisk and wide in flight or when attacking, small and slow on the ground, still when sitting — `ts, amp = attacking ? (0.96, 0.75) : (activity == 0 ? (0.22, 0.22) : (0.76, 0.45)); sitting ? (0, 0); tail1.yRot = cos(t * ts) * pi * 0.04; tail2.yRot = cos(t * ts) * pi * amp * 0.125; neck1 = ... * 0.25; tail3 = ... * 0.375; tail4 = ... * 0.5; tail6 = ... * 0.625; tail5 = ... * 0.75; spike10/7/8/3/9 = the segment they ride; positions along the chain by cos/sin x 6` (ModelDragon.java:422-423, :516-570)
- **mouth**: the lower jaw chomps fast while the attacking flag is set and hangs nearly shut otherwise — `mouth2.xRot = attacking ? 0.4 + cos(t * 1.5) * pi * 0.14 : 0.07` (ModelDragon.java:618-619)
