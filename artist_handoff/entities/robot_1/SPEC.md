## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A knee-high kamikaze drone: a clockwork body with five wind-up keys, two feet and a lot of plating. It wanders, then locks on, sprints at its target with its body spinning faster the closer it gets, trailing smoke and lava, and blows itself up on contact. The keys turn slowly all the time; the feet flap with the distance walked.

### 4.2 The math

- **feet**: the two feet flap in opposite directions with the distance walked; nothing moves while standing — `walk = amount > 0.1 ? cos(limbSwing*1.5)*pi*0.75*amount : 0; rfoot.xRot = -walk; lfoot.xRot = walk` (ModelRobot1.java:214-230; Robot1GeoReplacement.java:34-45)
- **keys**: the five wind-up keys turn slowly and steadily about Z — `keyN.zRot = rad(ageInTicks * 0.75)` (ModelRobot1.java:214-230; Robot1GeoReplacement.java:47-52)
- `ModelRobot1.setupAnim` (ModelRobot1.java:214-227; orig ModelRobot1.java:211-222, `render`): while the walking speed is above a tenth, each foot pitches by the cosine of the walk position times 1.5, times pi, times 0.75, times the walking speed — up to 135 degrees each way at full speed, the left foot positive and the right foot the negative — and holds 0 below a tenth (the port reads the walk POSITION, limbSwing, where the original's `f2 * 1.5f * wingspeed` read the age in ticks); the five wind-up keys turn about Z by the age in ticks times 0.75 degrees (a full turn every 480 ticks, 24 s; orig :218-222 `f2 * 0.75f * wingspeed`, wingspeed 1.0). The head angles and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (Robot1GeoReplacement.java:31-48).
