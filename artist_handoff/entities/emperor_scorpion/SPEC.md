## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

The Emperor Scorpion is a giant hostile scorpion: a wide flat head with two eyes and two hinged mandibles, an eight-segment body, two claw arms of four segments each ending in a pincer, eight five-segment legs (four a side) and an eight-segment tail curling up over the back to a three-piece stinger (EmperorScorpionModel.createBodyLayer, EmperorScorpionModel.java:182-576). It has 350 HP, 35 attack and 20 armour (createAttributes, EntityEmperorScorpion.java:108-115), fights on a bug melee goal whose hit poisons one time in three for 90 ticks (EmperorScorpionPoisonGoal at :96, EmperorScorpionPoisonGoal.java:18-32), and its strike throws the victim three blocks back (doHurtTarget :167-179). Every fourth tick it scans for prey (customServerAiStep :220-226, selectTarget :257-275) and, while it has a target, a 1-in-20 roll drops a baby Scorpion at the midpoint between them (:223-224, spawnBabyScorpionToward :230-243); it avenges hits (:100-101), ignores further damage for 30 ticks after each one (hurt :182-198), hops with a boost (jumpFromGround :158-164) and heals 2 on a rare roll (:206-209). It spawns at night in the dark above y 50 with air above it and no other Emperor within 20 blocks (checkSpawnRules :353-371), in badlands (the hostile_emperor_scorpion biome modifiers). Read it as a heavy armoured crawler: the legs plant one after another, the claws threaten, the tail arcs.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Three rhythms. The eight legs walk on one speed-scaled wave at 0.44 rad/tick (a 14.3-tick stride) in four phases a quarter period apart: every leg segment from the second out yaws with the sweep, the knee, shin and foot are re-positioned along it by sine (an FK chain in code), and the leg lifts on its forward half-wave by 0.47 x speed less the sweep's magnitude (EmperorScorpionModel.java:585-616, :735-769). Standing still the legs rest.
- The two mandible tips scissor about z at 0.11 rad/tick and 9 degrees at rest, 0.55 rad/tick and 27 degrees while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (:617-619; the byte is EntityEmperorScorpion.java:48, set by the melee goal's IntConsumer registered at :96).
- The claws and the tail share one 0.66 rad/tick wave (a 9.5-tick period) but each moves only when a per-entity random latch picks it at the start of a period: at rest each claw moves in 2 periods of 20 and the tail in 1 of 25; attacking each claw in 2 of 4 and the tail in 1 of 3 (:624-650). A moving claw swings its upper segment 27 degrees and closes its pincer finger against the swing; the moving tail flexes every segment about its rest bend and the stinger curls with it (:771-823).
- The head, the eyes, the body segments, the shoulders, the mandible bases and the legs' hip roots are static; the head does not track the look angle and the walk position plays no part.
- The strike is the melee goal's (EmperorScorpionPoisonGoal, a BugMeleeAttackGoal, EntityEmperorScorpion.java:96) and its poison lands on the hit; the baby-scorpion summon (:223-224) and the 30-tick invulnerability after a hit (:183-185) are timed by the AI, not by animation events.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the eight legs sweep about the vertical with the stride in four phases a quarter period apart, each leg lifting as it swings forward; every segment from the second out yaws with the sweep and the knee, shin and foot are re-positioned along it (an FK chain in code) — `a_k = cos(t * 2.0 * 0.22 - k * pi/2) * pi * 0.12 * amount (k = 0..3 for legs 1/5, 2/6, 3/7, 4/8); left LegNSeg2..5.yRot = a_k; right LegNSeg2..4.yRot = -a_k, right LegNSeg5.yRot = a_k; lift = (a_k rising) ? 0.47 * amount - |a_k| : 0; left Seg2.zRot = -lift - 0.929, Seg3.zRot = -lift + 0.632 (right: +lift + 0.929, +lift - 0.632); Seg3/4/5 x,z from sin(a_k) x 6 / 9 / 1, y from sin of the lifts x 11.5` (EmperorScorpionModel.java:585-616 (doLeftLeg :735-751, doRightLeg :753-769); wingspeed 0.22 (:21))
- **mandibles**: the two mandible tips scissor slowly at rest and snap fast and wide while the attacking flag is set — `a = attacking == 0 ? cos(t * 0.5 * 0.22) * pi * 0.05 : cos(t * 2.5 * 0.22) * pi * 0.15; LeftManPart2.zRot = a; RightManPart2.zRot = -a` (EmperorScorpionModel.java:617-619)
- **claws_tail**: the claws and the tail share one wave but each moves only in the periods a per-entity random latch picks for it: at rest each claw in two periods of twenty and the tail in one of twenty-five, attacking each claw in two of four and the tail in one of three; a part not picked holds its rest pose — `a = cos(t * 3.0 * 0.22) * pi * 0.15; at each rising zero-crossing ri1 = attacking ? nextInt(4) : nextInt(20), ri2 = attacking ? nextInt(3) : nextInt(25); doLeftClaw(ri1 in {1,3} ? a : 0); doRightClaw(ri1 in {2,3} ? a : 0); doTail(ri2 == 1 ? a : 0)` (EmperorScorpionModel.java:624-650 (doLeftClaw :771-779, doRightClaw :781-789, doTail :791-823))
- `EmperorScorpionModel.poseFrom` (EmperorScorpionModel.java:588-659, with the helpers doLeftLeg :743-759, doRightLeg :761-777, doLeftClaw :779-787, doRightClaw :789-797 and doTail :799-831; orig ModelEmperorScorpion.java:569-720 in `render` (func_78088_a, with its helpers at :732-820; func_78087_a at :728-730 is a bare super call), wingspeed 0.22 from `new ModelEmperorScorpion(0.22f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:426 - the port's WINGSPEED): four leg pairs walk a quarter turn apart - for pair k (0 to 3) the swing is the cosine of the age in ticks times 2.0 times the wingspeed, less k times 1.570795, times pi, times 0.12, times the walking speed, and a lift of 0.47 times the walking speed less the absolute swing applies when the same cosine 0.1 ticks later is larger (the leg rising); the left leg of the pair (legs 1 to 4) takes the swing, the right leg (5 to 8) its negative.
