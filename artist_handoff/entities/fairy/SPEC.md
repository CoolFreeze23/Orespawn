## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A tiny winged humanoid in one of nine colours: a head, chest, waist and hips, two legs, two arms, two wing pairs standing out behind, and two small back pieces. It flits about, tameable and harmless. Read it as a hovering sprite: four wings beating, arms drifting, the head following your gaze.

### 4.2 The math

- **outer_wings**: the outer wings beat about the vertical, mirrored around their rests — `lwing1.yRot = -0.6 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.0 * 1.5) * pi * 0.35; rwing1.yRot = -2.55 - (that)` (FairyModel.setupAnim; FairyGeoReplacement.java)
- **inner_wings**: the inner wings beat a little slower and shallower — `lwing2.yRot = -0.6 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 0.85) * pi * 0.25; rwing2.yRot = -2.55 - (that)` (FairyModel.setupAnim; FairyGeoReplacement.java)
- **arms**: the arms drift slowly, each on its own pitch and roll rhythms — `larm.xRot = -0.2 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 0.15) * pi * 0.05; rarm.xRot = -0.2 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 0.12) * pi * 0.05; larm.zRot = -0.15 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 0.1) * pi * 0.03; rarm.zRot = 0.15 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 0.11) * pi * 0.03` (FairyModel.setupAnim; FairyGeoReplacement.java)
- **head**: the head follows the gaze at 0.45 rate, never more than 26 degrees to either side, and nods with the pitch — `head.yRot = clamp(rad(netHeadYaw) * 0.45, -0.45, 0.45); head.xRot = rad(headPitch)` (FairyModel.setupAnim; FairyGeoReplacement.java)
- `FairyModel.setupAnim` (FairyModel.java; orig ModelFairy.java:134-149, `render`, wingspeed 1.5 from `new ModelFairy(1.5f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:477): (1) the outer wings yaw by the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.5, times pi, times 0.35 - 63 degrees each way every 4.19 ticks - the left around -0.6 radians (plus), the right around -2.55 (minus) (orig :134-135); (2) the inner wings yaw by the cosine of the age times 1.5 times 0.85, times pi, times 0.25 - 45 degrees every 4.93 ticks - around the same rests (orig :136-137); (3) the head yaws by the head yaw in radians times 0.45, held between -0.45 and 0.45 radians, and pitches by the head pitch in radians (orig :138-145); (4) the left arm pitches by -0.2 radians plus the cosine of the age times 1.5 times 0.15, times pi, times 0.05, the right arm by -0.2 plus the cosine of the age times 1.5 times 0.12, times pi, times 0.05; the left arm rolls by -0.15 plus the cosine of the age times 1.5 times 0.1, times pi, times 0.03, the right by 0.15 plus the cosine of the age times 1.5 times 0.11, times pi, times 0.03 (orig :146-149).
