## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A leaf-block mimic built of five one-block leaf cubes: a body cube, an arm cube at each side and a leg cube under each arm (LeafMonsterModel.java:37-41, a 128x128 sheet). Calm, it is a pile: the body cube drops to the ground between the two leg cubes with the arm cubes sitting on top of them, and the entity snaps itself to the block grid and to a 90-degree yaw every tick (EntityLeafMonster.java:89-104), so it passes for leaves. When prey - an ant, a butterfly, a luna moth or a non-creative player - comes within 4 blocks and in sight (:143-170) it raises the synched attacking flag (:130), rises a full block so the body hangs between the arms above the legs, flails both arms, walks at the prey and bites within 2.2 blocks (:126-139, doHurtTarget :134) for 2 damage; 6 HP, 1 armour (MobStats.LEAF_MONSTER, :57-64). It floats (:52), panics when hurt (:53) and spawns in darkness at night above y 50 (below y 20 in the Islands) with at most four others nearby (:194-206). Read it as an ambush: dead-still leaves, then a two-block figure of flailing blocks.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Two branches on the synched attacking flag (LeafMonsterModel.java:47-70). Calm (attacking == 0, :48-57): the body cube is dropped 16 units to the ground and both arm cubes 16 units onto the leg cubes, every rotation is 0 - a still 3-wide pile of leaf blocks; the entity meanwhile snaps its position to the block centre and its yaw to a multiple of 90 degrees every tick (EntityLeafMonster.java:89-104), so it cannot walk or turn while calm.
- Attacking (:58-69): the body and arms return to the bind pose (the figure rises one block: legs at the ground, body above them, arms at the top); the legs swing with the stride - the cosine of the age x 0.95, x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed above the 0.1 threshold, left and right opposed (45 degrees at full stride every 6.6 ticks); the arms flail regardless of speed - the absolute value of the cosine of the age x 0.7 x pi x 0.55 (99 degrees) yaws both arms outward and pitches both forward, a rectified pump every 4.5 ticks.
- The switch between the branches is instant: the code snaps the body and arms between the two heights on the tick the flag changes (no transition), which is why the `rise` and `sink` extras exist.
- Nothing reads the head look (netHeadYaw unused; no look goal registered, EntityLeafMonster.java:50-55) and nothing scales with health.
- The attacking flag is held: set 1 every fourth think tick while prey is found (EntityLeafMonster.java:126-131) and 0 when none is (:137) - a state, so the strike (doHurtTarget :134, within 2.2 blocks on a roll) is transport 1.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two leg cubes swing fore and aft in opposition with the stride, only while it is attacking — `attacking: a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 0.95) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount : 0; lleg.xRot = a; rleg.xRot = -a; calm: lleg.xRot = rleg.xRot = 0` (LeafMonsterModel.java:56-57, 62-64)
- **arms**: both arm cubes flail: they yaw outward together and pitch forward together, dropping back through the rest each half period — `attacking: m = abs(cos(t * 0.7) * pi * 0.55); rarm.yRot = -m; larm.yRot = m; rarm.xRot = larm.xRot = -m; calm: all 0` (LeafMonsterModel.java:52-55, 65-69)
