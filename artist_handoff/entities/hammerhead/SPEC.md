## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the first Tier-1 slice (T1a, 2026-09-15; hitbox profiles excluded - the classic single hitbox stays until the profile phase). Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Hammerhead is a wide, armoured six-legged beast with a hammer for a head: a chest and abdomen under three rows of back armour and two rear fins, a neck in a collar of armour, a head and snout carrying a horn base with two great horn bars and a horn tip each side, a three-blade crest fan and two whisker ears, six two-piece legs (front, middle and rear pairs) and a stub tail (ModelHammerhead.createBodyLayer, ModelHammerhead.java:100-252). It has 240 HP, 75 attack and 20 armour (MobStats.HAMMERHEAD, createAttributes Hammerhead.java:76-85) and no boss bar (:47-48), wanders far and looks about (:65-69), and every third tick takes its revenge target, its stored target or the nearest living thing in an 18-block box — players, monsters and the attackable non-mobs (customServerAiStep :127-164, isSuitableTarget :189-196) — closing at 1.25 and, within seven blocks plus half the target's width, raising the attacking flag and biting on two dice (:152-156) for 75 with a knockback (doHurtTarget :101-113). Cactus cannot hurt it and whoever does becomes its revenge target (hurt :116-124). It spawns at night in the dark above y 50 under a clear air column with no other Hammerhead within 16 blocks (checkSpawnRules :228-237) in the Chaos biome (chaos_biome.json). Read it as a low, heavy charger: the six legs scissor, the hammer head swings on the neck, and everything nods with the bite.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One speed-scaled stride at 0.429 rad/tick (a 14.7-tick period) above a walking-speed threshold of 0.1: the six legs scissor 18 degrees at top speed, upper and lower piece together, the front left with the rear right, the front right with the rear left, and the middle pair an eighth of a period ahead (ModelHammerhead.java:259-276). Standing still every leg rests.
- The head assembly — neck, collar, horn base, two horn bars and two tips, head, snout, the three-blade fan and the two whisker ears — turns a quarter of the look yaw as one unit at all times (:277-291) and, while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (Hammerhead.java:42, raised at :153 within seven blocks plus half the target's width), nods 23 degrees at the stride's own 0.429 rad/tick about every part's rest pitch (:295-311); with the flag clear it holds the rest.
- The two rear fins sway 5 degrees at 0.099 rad/tick (a 64-tick period) about ±0.349 (:292-294). The chest, abdomen, the three back armour plates and the tail stub are static; the walk position and the head pitch play no part.
- The bite (doHurtTarget, Hammerhead.java:101-113) is rolled by the third-tick scan (:131-163) — nothing in the code fires an animation for it.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the six legs scissor with the walking speed, upper and lower piece together: front-left with rear-right, front-right with rear-left, the middle pair on its own phase — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3 * 0.33) * pi * 0.1 * amount : 0; a2 = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3 * 0.33 + pi/4) * pi * 0.1 * amount : 0; leg_1.xRot = -0.087 + a, leg_1b.xRot = a; leg_1R.xRot = -0.087 - a, leg_1Rb.xRot = -a; leg_2.xRot = -0.052 + a2, leg_2b.xRot = a2; leg_2R.xRot = -0.052 - a2, leg_2Rb.xRot = -a2; leg_3.xRot = -0.349 - a, leg_3b.xRot = -a; leg_3R.xRot = -0.349 + a, leg_3Rb.xRot = a` (ModelHammerhead.java:259-276; wingspeed 0.33 (:21))

a wrong `loop` value; a missing `idle` or `walk` (they open the game's switch only TOGETHER — one without the other leaves the creature on its code-driven motion); a missing clip the game already triggers by name; |rotation| > 3600 degrees or |position| > 1024 units; a key later than the clip's `animation_length` (a declared length shorter than the last key); a returned `.geo.json` whose bones, parents, pivots, rotations, cubes, UVs or canvas differ from the shipped rig;
