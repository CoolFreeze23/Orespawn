## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A sail-backed, crocodile-snouted dinosaur: a torso, a rump and a tail, a neck base rising to a head top, an upper snout, a long snout and a snout tip, a six-piece crest with a ridge piece above the eyes, nineteen sail plates in a row down the spine, two arms of thigh, knee, shin, two toes and a big hooked claw (the model calls them front legs), and two hind legs of leg, calf and foot (ModelBaryonyx.java:79-130; 52 parts on a 128x128 sheet). It is a peaceful grazer: 40 HP, speed 0.25, an 8-damage attribute it never uses (Baryonyx.java:66-71) - no goal strikes. It floats (:57), breeds on crystal apples (:58, :170-173), flees monsters (:59), panics when hurt (:60), watches players (:61) and wanders (:62); every sixty ticks or so it looks for a grass block, walks to it and, within 3.5 blocks, eats it - the block turns to dirt under mobGriefing, it heals 1 and burps (:141-164). It spawns above y 50 by day with at most eight others nearby (:181-188); a baby is drawn at half size (BaryonyxRenderer.java:30-31). Read it as a placid fish-eater: a slow stride on the hind legs, the big front claws waving, the sail and the long snout carried still.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the hind legs (ModelBaryonyx.java:403-409): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.25 (wingspeed, :24), x pi x 0.15, x the walking speed is added to the right thigh, calf and foot and subtracted from the left - a 27-degree stride at full speed every 19.3 ticks, each leg swinging as one unit around its rests (the calf bent -0.17 rad). Below the threshold the legs snap to their rests.
- The two hooked claws wave at all times (:410-412): the cosine of the age x 0.7 x 0.25, x pi x 0.25 - 45 degrees every 35.9 ticks about the arm's axis, the left claw mirroring the right.
- Everything else is static: the torso, rump and tail, the neck, head, snouts, crest and nasal ridge, the nineteen sail plates, and the whole of both arms except the claws (the upper arm, elbow, forearm and toes never move). Nothing reads the look (netHeadYaw unused), so the LookAtPlayerGoal (Baryonyx.java:61) turns nothing visible.
- There is no attacking flag and no strike: the grass bite (Baryonyx.java:152-162) is a block change, a heal and a burp with no pose of its own; the renderer halves the scale for a baby (BaryonyxRenderer.java:30-31).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two hind legs stride in opposition, thigh, calf and foot of a leg swinging together as one unit — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.25) * pi * 0.15 * limbSwingAmount : 0; shape24.xRot = a; shape25.xRot = -0.17 + a; shape26.xRot = a; shape13.xRot = -a; shape15.xRot = -0.17 - a; shape17.xRot = -a` (ModelBaryonyx.java:403-409)
- **claws**: the two big hooked claws wave slowly in and out at all times — `c = cos(ageInTicks * 0.7 * 0.25) * pi * 0.25; shape21.zRot = c; shape16.zRot = -c` (ModelBaryonyx.java:410-412)
