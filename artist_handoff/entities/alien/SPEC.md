## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Alien is a tall hostile biped: a torso and stomach on a neck that carries a three-piece head with four fangs and a two-piece lower jaw, a fifteen-blade crest fan that lies flat until it fights, two arms of two segments each ending in three claw fingers, two legs of a thigh, a two-piece shin and a foot, and a five-segment tail with five spikes (ModelAlien.createBodyLayer, ModelAlien.java:136-360). It has 100 HP, 12 attack and 8 armour (MobStats.ALIEN, createAttributes Alien.java:103-110), walks through villages (:92) and hunts torches to break (AlienTorchSeekGoal, :96) between wanders (:97-99); every eighth tick it looks for a player within 12 blocks (customServerAiStep :221-246, findSomethingToAttack :248-267), closes to 1.2 speed, and within four blocks raises the attacking flag and swings on about 40 percent of those ticks (:229-236) — a hit poisons one time in five and throws the victim back (doHurtTarget :170-194). It shrugs off cactus, retaliates on any mob that hurts it (hurt :197-218), heals 1 on a 1-in-40 roll (:243-245), and spawns in the dark — anywhere in the Islands, else at or below y 50 with a 3x3x3 clear air pocket above (checkSpawnRules :270-278) — in the Mining dimension (mining_biome.json). Read it as a stalking predator: a rolling stride, claws and jaw twitching at rest, the crest flaring open when it attacks.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The legs stride on one speed-scaled wave at 0.88 rad/tick (a 7.1-tick period) and a full 90 degrees at top speed: the foot, the two shin pieces and the thigh of a leg swing together about their own rests, the right leg opposite the left (ModelAlien.java:367-369, :568-580). The torso and stomach are static.
- The fifteen-blade crest fan lies flat and still until the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (Alien.java:48, raised at :230 within four blocks of a target); then it spreads into a fan, 15 degrees per blade, and ripples at 0.268 rad/tick, each blade a twelfth of a period behind the last (:370-435).
- The tail, the jaw and the two arms share one 0.77 rad/tick wave (an 8.2-tick period) but each moves hard only in the periods a per-entity random latch picks for it, re-rolled at every rising zero-crossing (:467-480): at rest the tail in 1 period of 15, the jaw in 1 of 15, each arm in 2 of 15; attacking the tail in 1 of 2, the jaw always, each arm in 2 of 4. The parts not picked sway, chomp or twitch faintly on a slow 0.22 rad/tick wave (:481-506). The jaw and claw waves are rectified (absolute values, :583, :615-643): they open and reach twice per period and never cross their rests.
- The neck turns 0.35 of the look yaw and the head 0.75 of it, the head pieces, fangs and jaw riding the head by position (:436-463); the walk position plays no part.
- The strike (doHurtTarget, Alien.java:170-194) is rolled by customServerAiStep (:229-236) — nothing in the code fires an animation for it.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride with the walking speed, every piece of a leg swinging together about its own rest, the right leg opposite the left — `a = cos(t * 4.0 * 0.22) * pi * 0.5 * amount; lFoot.xRot = a, lShin.xRot = a - 0.4, lShin1.xRot = lThigh.xRot = a - 0.8; rFoot.xRot = -a, rShin.xRot = -a - 0.4, rShin1.xRot = rThigh.xRot = -a - 0.8` (ModelAlien.java:367-369 (doLeftLeg :568-573, doRightLeg :575-580); wingspeed 0.22 (:21))
- **fan**: the crest fan lies flat until the attacking flag is set, then spreads into a fan and ripples blade by blade — `attacking ? (fan.xRot = cos(t * 1.22 * 0.22) * pi * 0.1; fanlK.xRot = fanrK.xRot = cos(t * 1.22 * 0.22 - K * pi/6) * pi * 0.1; fanlK.zRot = 0.261 K, fanrK.zRot = -0.261 K) : (every blade xRot = -1.85, zRot = 0)` (ModelAlien.java:370-435)
- **tail**: the tail lashes hard in the periods a per-entity random latch picks for it and sways gently otherwise, the tip swinging six times the base — `a = ri2 == 1 ? cos(t * 3.5 * 0.22) * pi * 0.5 : cos(t * 0.22) * pi * 0.05; tail1.yRot = spike1.yRot = 0.25a; tail2.yRot = 0.5a; tail3.yRot = 0.8a; tail4.yRot = 1.25a; tail5.yRot = 1.5a; spike4.yRot = tail4.yRot + 0.52, spike5.yRot = tail4.yRot - 0.52; positions along the chain by cos/sin x 10` (ModelAlien.java:468-486 (doTail :586-612))
- **jaw**: the lower jaw chomps wide in the periods the latch picks for it and works faintly otherwise; the wave is rectified, so it opens twice per period — `a = ri3 == 1 ? cos(t * 3.5 * 0.22) * pi * 0.35 : cos(t * 0.22) * pi * 0.02; jaw1.xRot = jaw2.xRot = |a|` (ModelAlien.java:487-493 (doJaw :582-584))
