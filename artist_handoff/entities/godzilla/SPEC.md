## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


Mobzilla (the `godzilla` registry) is the mod's largest boss, a 25-block bipedal kaiju: a three-block body under a spined back, a neck to a head with an upper and a hinged lower jaw, two arms of shoulder, upper arm, forearm and a three-fingered hand, two legs of thigh, upper leg and lower leg on three-toed feet, and an eight-segment tail with spines (ModelGodzilla.createBodyLayer, ModelGodzilla.java:168-537). It has 4000 HP, 175 attack and 21 armour (MobStats.GODZILLA, createAttributes Godzilla.java:148-155; a 6000 constant gates its healing, :66, :311-313), a purple boss bar (:86-87) and four manual hitbox parts that ride the root — bodyLow 8x8, bodyUp 6x6, head 5x5, tail 4x4 (:129-133, :292-295; the head takes full damage, the bodies half, the tail a quarter plus one, :262-270). It wanders and looks at anything living within 50 blocks (:140-145). Its AI (customServerAiStep :655-814) reloads eight cannon shots every 100 ticks, picks a target on a 1-in-5 tick and, by distance and dice: calls lightning on it (100 damage, fire, an explosion and two bolts, :769-770, doLightningAttack :560-578), leaps at it (jumpAtEntity :391-403), closes within seventeen blocks with the attacking flag up and stomps it (:777-784, doHurtTarget :819-847: a 3.2 knockback, and a big creature halved), or beyond 25 blocks fires a volley — a really big fireball and five more from the mouth 22 blocks ahead and 19 up (fireCannon :516-556). Every landing from a jump rings damage out to 10, 15 and 25 blocks (:699-710, doJumpDamage :490-512), and it crushes blocks around and ahead of itself while it walks (:712-733). Damage to it is capped and refused for 20 ticks after each hit (hurt :851-879); it heals 5 on a 1-in-35 roll (:811-813), never falls or burns by lightning (:315-326), and spawns once per world by night above y 50 under open sky on a 1-in-40 roll with a clear 17x11x17 pocket and no other within 64 blocks (checkSpawnRules :184-214) in the Village biome (village_biome.json), calling up its invisible gaze-tracking head sidecar at its first target (:756-763). Read it as slow and vast: every step plants, the arms swing wide when it rages, the tail lashes.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One speed-scaled stride at 0.75 rad/tick (an 8.4-tick period): each foot — nine toe pieces moved as one — slides 35 units back and forth with the wave and lifts 18 units on its forward half, the lower leg rides it and pitches 16 degrees, the upper leg and thigh swing 27 and 18 degrees a quarter turn ahead, and the right leg runs half a period behind the left (ModelGodzilla.java:553-623). Standing still every leg rests.
- The tail sways in phase along eight segments, the tip nine times the base: 9 degrees at 0.75 rad/tick at rest, 36 degrees at 1.75 rad/tick while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (Godzilla.java:83; raised at :779 within seventeen blocks and at :790 when the cannon is aimed) (:625-630, :707-747).
- The arms swing about the vertical with the hands and fingers opening and closing — 3.6 degrees at 0.1 rad/tick at rest, 29 degrees at 1.75 rad/tick attacking, wider at every joint out (:647-704); the lower jaw chomps 22 degrees at 1.5 rad/tick while attacking (:641-645). The head turns 0.55 of the look yaw and pitches with the look, the jaws with it (:632-639).
- The body blocks, neck, shoulders and the back and crest spines are static; the walk position plays no part.
- The stomp (doHurtTarget, Godzilla.java:819-847), the leap (jumpAtEntity :391-403), the landing rings (:699-710), the cannon volley (fireCannon :516-556) and the lightning (doLightningAttack :560-578) are all rolled by customServerAiStep (:655-814) — nothing in the code fires an animation for any of them; the four manual part hitboxes are placed from the root each tick (:292-295).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: each leg strides as a planted foot that slides back and lifts forward, the lower leg following it and the upper leg and thigh swinging a quarter turn ahead; the right leg opposite the left — `c = cos(t * 0.75), c2 = cos(t * 0.75 + pi/4), s = sin(t * 0.75) (right: + pi, + 5pi/4, + pi); LToe*.z = 6 + 35 c amount; LToe*.y = s > 0 ? 16 - s * 18 * amount : 16; LLowerLeg = the foot's position, xRot 0.22 + c * pi * 0.09 * amount; LUpperLeg.xRot = -0.17 + c2 * pi * 0.15 * amount (placed 55 up the lower leg by cos/sin); LThigh.xRot = -0.558 + c2 * pi * 0.1 * amount, z = 2 + 35 c amount / 4` (ModelGodzilla.java:553-623; ANIM_SPEED 1.0 (:20))
