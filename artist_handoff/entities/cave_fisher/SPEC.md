## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A cave crab-spider: a low two-block body with four ridged plates down each side and five spines along the back, a three-piece head with two eyes and a nose, a tail base with a tuft, six legs of six segments each - front, middle and back on each side - and two five-segment arms ending in a three-piece claw (ModelCaveFisher.java:100-174; 75 parts on a 64x32 sheet). It is hostile but small: 10 HP, 4 damage, 4 armour, speed 0.2 (MobStats.CAVE_FISHER, CaveFisher.java:99-107). Every eighth tick it scans 10 blocks for anything alive and in sight that is not a monster, a cave fisher, an ender reaper, an ender knight or a creative player (:136-193) and hands it to its bug melee goal (:82-83): a chase at 1.2, a bite within 2.8 blocks on 1-in-7-or-1-in-8 dice, the synched attacking flag raised in reach and cleared out of it (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:145-176, the caveFisher preset :75). It ignores cactus (:195-200), floats (:81), wanders far (:84) and spawns in darkness at or below y 50 (:223-229). Read it as a scuttling cave crab: six legs rippling in a wave, the two claws snapping up at random - rarely at rest, half the time when it hunts.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait wave on the six legs (ModelCaveFisher.java:495-531): the cosine of the age x 2.0 x 0.62 (wingspeed, :22), x pi x 0.12, x the walking speed yaws every segment of the left front leg one way and the right front the other; the middle pair takes the same wave a quarter period later and the back pair half a period later - a 22-degree sweep at full speed every 5.1 ticks, rippling front to back. No threshold: at a crawl the legs barely move, and they never snap to rest.
- The claws snap at random (:533-552): at every boundary of the 3.4-tick claw cycle (found by comparing the cosine at the age and at the age plus 0.1) a per-entity latch rolls - nextInt(20) when calm, nextInt(4) while the synched attacking flag is up - and if it lands on 1 or 3 both arms play the snap for that cycle: the absolute value of the cosine of the age x 3.0 x 0.62 x pi x 0.15 (27 degrees) lifts all five segments of each arm and the claw base, the upper pincer holding -0.54 and the lower +0.35 rad on top (:556-577). Otherwise the arms lie flat. So the hunt reads as claws snapping five times more often, not as a different pose.
- The body blocks, plates, spines, head, eyes, nose and tail never move; nothing reads the look (netHeadYaw unused), so the LookAtPlayerGoal (CaveFisher.java:85) turns nothing visible.
- The attacking flag lives in the bug melee goal (CaveFisher.java:82-83 hands setAttacking to it): raised on the tick the goal finds the prey in reach and cleared on the tick it does not (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:145-176) - a held state while in reach, read by the generator's inventory through the goal it is handed to (TEST-011 (b), owner 2026-09-14, item 5: classified STATE). The bite is doHurtTarget inside the goal (:172), so the strike is transport 1.
- The renderer draws it at 0.75 (CaveFisherRenderer.java:40) over a flat 1.35 x 0.75 hitbox (ModEntities.java:56-58).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the six legs sweep fore and aft in a wave running front to back, every segment of a leg together, the right side opposite the left — `a0 = cos(t * 2.0 * 0.62) * pi * 0.12 * limbSwingAmount; a1 = cos(t * 2.0 * 0.62 - pi/2) * ...; a2 = cos(t * 2.0 * 0.62 - pi) * ...; LFLeg1-6.yRot = a0; RFLeg1-6.yRot = -a0; LMLeg1-6.yRot = a1; RMLeg1-6.yRot = -a1; LBLeg1-6.yRot = a2; RBLeg1-6.yRot = -a2` (ModelCaveFisher.java:495-531 (poseFrom, called from setupAnim :488))
- **claws**: both arms and claws snap up together in a quick double lift, in a random tenth of the cycles at rest and half of them while hunting — `s = latch ? abs(cos(t * 3.0 * 0.62) * pi * 0.15) : 0; every ArmSeg and ClawBase .xRot = s; ClawTop.xRot = s - 0.54; ClawLow.xRot = s + 0.35 (both arms alike)` (ModelCaveFisher.java:533-577)
