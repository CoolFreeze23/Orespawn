## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the first Tier-1 slice (T1a, 2026-09-15; hitbox profiles excluded - the classic single hitbox stays until the profile phase). The T-Rex is a hostile tyrannosaur: a long torso and hips with five back spines, a neck to a head with a snout, a hinged jaw and two head spikes, two tiny arms, two hip spikes, a tail with an extension, and two four-piece legs (ModelTRex.createBodyLayer, ModelTRex.java:80-219 — the parts carry the modeller's opaque names, shape1 to shape21, mapped to anatomy in the glossary). It has 160 HP, 22 attack and 14 armour (MobStats.TREX, createAttributes TRex.java:249-257; a 200 constant for its own health checks, :272-274), fights on the dinosaur melee goal — a bite within four blocks plus half the target's width on two dice, a chase at 1.25, prey found in a 20-block box (DinosaurMeleeAttackGoal.Presets.trex, TRex.java:76-77) — with a 1.2 knockback (doHurtTarget :314-328), re-picks its target every fifth tick and avenges hits (selectTarget :124-131, RevengeGoal :186), and wanders and looks about between (:78-80). Cactus cannot hurt it (hurt :331-345). It spawns at night in the dark above y 50 under a clear air column with no other T-Rex within 24 blocks (checkSpawnRules :356-365) in the Chaos and Mining biomes (chaos_biome.json, mining_biome.json). Read it as a heavy two-legged stalker: a swinging stride, a snapping jaw, the little arms twitching.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One speed-scaled stride at 1.3 rad/tick (a 4.8-tick period) above a walking-speed threshold of 0.1: the two legs swing 45 degrees at top speed as stiff four-piece chains, the left opposite the right (ModelTRex.java:224-236). Standing still the legs rest.
- The jaw chomps 32 degrees at 0.45 rad/tick about 0.52 while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (TRex.java:33; the melee goal at :76 sets it within reach) and hangs at 0.1 otherwise (:238-240). The two tiny arms wave 9 degrees at 0.1 rad/tick at all times (:242-243).
- The torso, hips, tail and its extension, neck, head, snout, the head and hip spikes and the five back spines are static; the head does not track the look, and the walk position plays no part.
- The bite is the melee goal's (DinosaurMeleeAttackGoal, TRex.java:76-77; doHurtTarget :314-328) — nothing in the code fires an animation for it.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride with the walking speed, each leg swinging as one stiff four-piece chain, the left opposite the right — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; rightleg.xRot = -0.174 + a, rightleg2.xRot = 0.506 + a, rightleg3.xRot = -0.401 + a, rightleg4.xRot = a; leftleg.xRot = -0.174 - a, leftleg2.xRot = 0.506 - a, leftleg3.xRot = -0.401 - a, leftleg4.xRot = -a` (ModelTRex.java:224-236; ANIM_SPEED 1.0 (:20))
- **jaw**: the jaw chomps while the attacking flag is set and hangs nearly shut otherwise — `jaw.xRot = attacking ? 0.52 + cos(t * 0.45) * pi * 0.18 : 0.1` (ModelTRex.java:238-240)
- **arms**: the two tiny arms wave slowly up and down together whatever the creature does — `shape11.xRot = shape17.xRot = -0.523 + cos(t * 0.1) * pi * 0.05` (ModelTRex.java:242-243)
- `ModelTRex.poseFrom` (ModelTRex.java:231-252; orig ModelTRex.java:216-254 in `render` (func_78088_a, :211-255; func_78087_a at :263-265 is a bare super call); the original's wingspeed 0.2 from `new ModelTRex(0.2f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:417 is not the port's, whose ANIM_SPEED is 1.0 - the hooks' landing recorded the departure): while the walking speed is above a tenth the swing is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.3, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed, else 0; the four right leg pieces pitch -0.174, 0.506, -0.401 and 0 plus it, the four left pieces the same rests minus it. Attacking, the jaw pitches 0.52 plus the cosine of the age times 0.45, times pi, times 0.18; else 0.1. The two arms (the rig's shape17 and shape11) pitch -0.523 plus the cosine of the age times 0.1, times pi, times 0.05. The walk position and the head angles play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (TRexGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
