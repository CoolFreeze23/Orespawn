## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A small passive fish: a boxy body and head with a mouth and hinged jaw, a dorsal fin, four pectoral fins, a bottom fin, a two-part tail with two caudal fins and two lower bottom fins. It swims in water and is harmless. Read it as a fish that never stops finning: every fin at its own slow rhythm.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Several independent rhythms run at all times, none scaled by walking speed; the classic code has no rest pose. The transcription ships idle (no bone) plus one clip per rhythm: the primary group (`primary_group`; the first group when the seed names none) carries the bare `walk` - a label, not a semantic: the contract's fly -> walk fallback is what plays a flyer's walk in flight (owner 2026-09-13, addendum item 26 (2)) - and every other rhythm is `walk_<group>`, so the GeckoLib candidate plays the transcription behind the switch.
- Six rhythms: four pectoral fins each at its own rate (the left front one is the primary group: its clip is the bare walk), the lower fin pair, the jaw; the body, head, tail and caudal fins are static. The pectoral fins bind at -/+0.335 rad about Y and the jaw at -0.228 rad about X, so the transcription's keys carry the offset (base minus bind). The pectoral and lower fins are rotated zero-thickness cubes: the seam lights them by their true normals (ENT-S-161).
- The default renderer poses it from the classic code; the GeckoLib candidate sits behind the dev switch (-Dorespawn.dev.geckolibRenderers) and your clips replace the shipped transcription file for file.

### 4.2 The math

- **pectoral1**: the left front pectoral fin sculls about the vertical around its rest angle — `Pectoralfin1.yRot = 0.4 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.7) * pi * 0.15` (ModelGoldFish.java:125-126; GoldFishGeoReplacement.java)
- **pectoral2**: the right front pectoral fin sculls a little slower, mirrored — `Pectoralfin2.yRot = -0.4 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.2 * 0.7) * pi * 0.15` (ModelGoldFish.java:127-128; GoldFishGeoReplacement.java)
- **pectoral3**: the left rear pectoral fin, slower again — `Pectoralfin3.yRot = 0.4 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.1 * 0.7) * pi * 0.15` (ModelGoldFish.java:129-130; GoldFishGeoReplacement.java)
- **pectoral4**: the right rear pectoral fin, the slowest of the four, mirrored — `Pectoralfin4.yRot = -0.4 + cos(ageInTicks * 1.0 * 0.7) * pi * 0.15` (ModelGoldFish.java:131-132; GoldFishGeoReplacement.java)
- **bottomfins**: the two lower fins sweep about the vertical, mirrored — `Bottomfin1.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 1.7 * 0.7) * pi * 0.25; Bottomfin2.yRot = -(that)` (ModelGoldFish.java:133-134; GoldFishGeoReplacement.java)
