## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A giant flying mantis: a thorax and a swept abdomen, a two-piece neck carrying a two-piece head with two eyes and two antennae, two three-piece raptorial arms folded under the neck, four four-piece walking legs splayed wide, and four wings - a front and a rear pair (MantisModel.java; 36 parts on a 256x256 sheet). It is hostile and heavy: 120 HP, 16 damage, 10 armour (MobStats.MANTIS, EntityMantis.java:68-76), 100 XP. It has no goals (:56-66): its flight is its own - random air spots 4 to 12 blocks away (:147-166), the motion eased toward them (:185-193), the yaw turned a quarter of the way per tick (:195-197), the vertical motion damped to 0.6 (:119-122) - and every eighth think tick it hunts players, any monster, butterflies, cockateils and fairies within 16 blocks and in sight, never anything in water (:167-183, :229-277), raising the synched attacking flag (:173), flying above the prey and striking within 5 blocks plus half the prey's width (:175-179, doHurtTarget) for 16. A hit makes it retaliate (:213-222); water hurts it (:123-125); it heals slowly (:199-201) and spawns in clear air above y 50 by day, never near another (:279-290). Read it as a hovering predator: wings a blur, the raptorial arms folded and breathing at rest, unfolding into wide slashes when it attacks.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The four wings beat at all times (MantisModel.java:289-294): the cosine of the age x 0.9 x 2.0 (wingspeed, :21) - a 3.5-tick period, a blur - x pi x 0.25 on the front pair (45 degrees around a 40-degree spread) and x pi x 0.35 on the rear pair (63 degrees around a 20-degree spread, the opposite way), left and right mirrored.
- The raptorial arms branch on the synched attacking flag (:295-315). Calm: a 2-degree breathing (the cosine of the age x 0.051 x 2.0 x pi x 0.013, every 61.6 ticks) around a -0.2 rad fold. Attacking: 45-degree swings (the cosine of the age x 0.51 x 2.0 x pi x 0.25, every 6.2 ticks) around a -0.698 rad (40-degree) rest - the left arm's upper segment and claw take the angle, its forearm the negative, and the right arm the mirror, so the arms alternate and jack-knife open and shut.
- Each forearm is placed 22 units along its upper segment's pitch and each claw 17 units along the forearm's (:303-304, :306-307, :310-311, :313-314): a hand-written FK chain on flat bones - when this rig lands a keyframed strike must key the positions too, or the arm bones must be parented.
- The legs, neck, head, eyes, antennae, thorax and abdomen never move; nothing scales with walking speed (limbSwingAmount unused) and nothing reads the look. The visible travel is the entity's own flight (EntityMantis.java:141-197): random air spots, the motion eased toward them, a dive above the prey (:174).
- The attacking flag is held: set 1 every eighth think tick while a target is found (EntityMantis.java:167-173) and 0 when none is (:181) - a state, so the strike (doHurtTarget :178, within 5 blocks plus half the prey's width) is transport 1.

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: all four wings beat up and down about the body's axis at one fast rhythm, the rear pair wider and opposite to the front pair, left and right mirrored — `f = cos(t * 0.9 * 2.0) * pi * 0.25; lfwing.zRot = -0.698 - f; rfwing.zRot = 0.698 + f; r = cos(t * 0.9 * 2.0) * pi * 0.35; lrwing.zRot = -0.349 + r; rrwing.zRot = 0.349 - r` (MantisModel.java:289-294)
