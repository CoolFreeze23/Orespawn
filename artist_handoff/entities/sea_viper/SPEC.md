## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A hostile sea serpent: a 12x8x6 head with an upper mouth, a hinged lower jaw, two eyes, two fangs and a three-piece forked tongue, a neck, and a body of twenty-two segments that taper from 8x8 to 2x5 over 150 units, ending in a tail tip (ModelSeaViper.java:93-228, 34 parts). 160 HP, 22 damage, 12 armour; speed 0.75 in water and 0.25 on land (SeaViper.java:59-60, :136-144, :201-207), a water-bound navigator that breathes underwater (:148-159). On a 1-in-5 tick it picks the nearest living thing in an 18-block box - a non-creative player, any monster, or the attackable animals, never another viper (:301-319, :336-357) - chases at 1.5 and, within 4.5 blocks plus half the target's width, rolls its bite on a 1-in-5 cadence (:119; DinosaurMeleeAttackGoal.Presets.seaViper; BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:159-174), landing 22 with a knockback (:209-226) and poison one time in two (SeaViperBiteGoal.java:22-31); the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (:56-57) is held at 1 while it stands in reach (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:168). Stranded, it hunts up to twelve blocks for water at 1.33 and dries out (:277-296); in water it heals with a splash (:321-324). It spawns above y 50 in daylight, no other within 16 (:411-419; ModEntities.java:159-160). Read it as a serpent: a travelling S-wave down the whole body that stiffens into a fixed S at rest, the jaw chattering and the tongue flicking when it closes in.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait-scaled wave with no threshold (ModelSeaViper.java:249-272; doseg :331-343): the base segment yaws 18 degrees x the walking speed at 0.65 rad/tick and every segment behind it yaws 36 degrees x the speed with a pi/4 lag per index - a wave eight segments long travelling toward the tail - each segment placed 9 px along the one before; as the speed drops the wave blends into a fixed S-curve (cos of -pi/4 x index, up to 1 rad), so a resting viper lies coiled, not straight.
- Two branches on the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (SeaViper.java:56-57; held at 1 while it stands within 4.5 blocks plus half the target's width, BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:168, dropped at :132 / :145 / :176): attacking, the lower jaw chatters 31 degrees about 0.65 rad at 0.85 rad/tick and the tongue flicks 13 degrees at 2.35 rad/tick, sliding 2.5 px in and out (:280-291); calm, the jaw breathes 4 degrees about 0.45 rad at 0.1 rad/tick and the tongue flicks 5 degrees at 0.85 rad/tick (:294-303).
- The head, upper mouth, eyes, fangs, lower jaw and tongue yaw together at half the head yaw (:312-324), the lower jaw's pivot trailing the head's by 2 px along that yaw (:318-319) and the forks splayed 0.436 rad; nothing pitches. The neck is static.
- The same wave draws it in water (0.75) and on land (0.25, SeaViper.java:59-60): setupAnim has no water branch. The bite (doHurtTarget :209-226 from the goal's swing roll, BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:170-173) and the heal splash (:321-324) are not animation events.

### 4.2 The math

- **body_wave**: a travelling wave runs down the whole body from the neck to the tail tip while it moves, and stiffens into a fixed S-shape as it comes to rest — `tBase.yRot = cos(t * 1.3 * 0.5) * pi * 0.1 * amount; for each segment (index f = 2, 2, 3, ..., 21): seg.z = prev.z + cos(prev.yRot) * 9 * |cos(prev.xRot)|; seg.x = prev.x + sin(prev.yRot) * 9 * |cos(prev.xRot)|; seg.yRot = cos(t * 1.3 * 0.5 - pi/4 * f) * pi * 0.2 * amount + cos(-pi/4 * f) * (1 - amount)` (ModelSeaViper.java:249-272 (the chain), :331-343 (doseg); wingspeed 0.5 (:14); a negative limbSwingAmount is clamped to 0 (:244-246))
- **jaw**: the lower jaw breathes slowly nearly shut at rest and chatters wide and fast while the attacking flag is up — `MouthBottom.xRot = attacking ? 0.65 + cos(t * 1.7 * 0.5) * pi * 0.17 : 0.45 + cos(t * 0.2 * 0.5) * pi * 0.02` (ModelSeaViper.java:280-283, :292-295)
- **tongue**: the forked tongue flicks and slides in and out - slowly at rest, fast while attacking — `f = attacking ? cos(t * 4.7 * 0.5) * pi * 0.07 : cos(t * 1.7 * 0.5) * pi * 0.03; ToungBase.xRot = 0.261 + f; MiddleTounge.xRot = 0.174 + f; ForkLeft.xRot = ForkRight.xRot = 0.087 + f; z = (attacking ? cos(t * 1.5 * 0.5) : cos(t * 0.5 * 0.5)) * pi * 0.05 * 16 added to each tongue part's initial z` (ModelSeaViper.java:285-291, :297-303, :306-309)
