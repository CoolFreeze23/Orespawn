## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A tiny passive ant: a three-part thorax, a two-part abdomen, a head with two jaws and six two-part legs, drawn at a quarter of the rig. It wanders and panics when hurt; right-clicking it carries the player to the Utopia dimension (the ant-dimension mechanic of 1.7.10). Read it as a small scurrying insect: legs in step with its speed, jaws never quite still.

## 2. Size and scale in-game

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm scaled by walking speed and one always-on rhythm: the classic code swings the twelve leg parts by limbSwingAmount (still when the creature stands, full at a run) and works the jaws whether or not it moves; it has no rest pose of its own. The transcription ships idle (no bone), walk (the legs; the controller scales the delta by walking speed - contract section 5.1, P3) and walk_jaws.
- The legs bind rotated about Y and Z and animate about X; the jaws bind unrotated.
- Shared rig (design Q9, one profile per registry path): the Ant, Rainbow Ant, Red Ant, Termite and Unstable Ant draw one geo and one clip file (the Ant's) under their own registry names, each with its own texture, shadow and scale; a clip delivered under any of the five names is played by all five.
- The default renderer poses it from the classic code; the GeckoLib candidate sits behind the dev switch (-Dorespawn.dev.geckolibRenderers) and your clips replace the shipped transcription file for file.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: all twelve leg parts swing about the sideways axis in step with walking speed: the left middle, right front and right rear legs one way, the right middle, left rear and left front legs the other; still when the creature stands — `llegbot1.xRot = llegtop1.xRot = rlegtop2.xRot = rlegbot2.xRot = rlegtop3.xRot = rlegbot3.xRot = cos(ageInTicks * 2.7) * pi * 0.45 * limbSwingAmount; rlegtop1, rlegbot1, llegtop2, llegbot2, llegtop3, llegbot3 = -(that)` (AntModel.java:166-176 (orig ModelAnt.java:162-172); AntGeoReplacement.java)
- **jaws**: the two jaws open and close slowly about the vertical, mirrored, whether or not the creature moves — `jawsl.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.4) * pi * 0.05; jawsr.yRot = -(that)` (AntModel.java:177-178 (orig ModelAnt.java:173-174); AntGeoReplacement.java)
