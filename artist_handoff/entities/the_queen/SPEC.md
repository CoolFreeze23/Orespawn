## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

The Queen is a flying, three-headed boss the size of a building: a long armoured body on two clawed legs, a pair of enormous segmented wings, three snaking necks each ending in a spiked, jawed head, and a whip tail tipped with a stinger. Dormant she hovers and idles with the blue texture; woken (hit, or a player too close) she turns red, shifts into an attack stance and circles, streaming fireballs and thunderbolts from the heads. In contact range she picks one of four strikes — a bite, a tail whip left or right, or a roar — and the hurt lands on the impact frame. In a 'mood' she either terraforms flowers and butterflies (happy) or spews a cloud of purple bombs (mad). Ten of her bones carry hitboxes that follow the animation, so the heads, wings, tail and legs must move like solid, heavy things.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Native GeckoLib: two controllers. `Movement` (5-tick blend) loops `idle` while dormant, plays `idle_to_attack` once while transitionTicks > 0, then loops `attack` (the awake stance) while isAwake; it stops on death. `Actions` (5-tick blend) is silent until a goal triggers `bite`, `tail_left` -> tail_whip_left, `tail_right` -> tail_whip_right, `roar`, or `death`.
- Every clip keys most of the 110 bones with position and rotation channels at 12 keys per second; the shipped file is 935 KB.
- The heads' hurt and the fireball streams are timed by the AI (QueenPrimaryGoal / TheQueen.customServerAiStep), not by animation events.
