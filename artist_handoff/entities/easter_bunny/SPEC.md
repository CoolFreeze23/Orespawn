## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A rabbit: a body and an upper body, a head with a nose and two long ears, two front paws, two legs with feet and a tail (ModelEasterBunny.java:37-49; 13 parts on a 64x128 sheet). It is a skittish holiday animal: 10 HP, speed 0.45, an unused 8-damage attribute (EasterBunny.java:59-64) - no goal strikes. It floats (:49), breeds on crystal apples (:50, :93-96), runs from monsters AND players (:51-52), panics when hurt (:53), watches other mobs (:54) and strolls avoiding water (:55); about once in 600 ticks it lays a stack of one to three random spawn eggs from a 115-slot table (:104-237). It spawns only at Easter, above y 50, by day, never within 32 blocks of another (:245-256); a kit is drawn at half size (EasterBunnyRenderer.java:39-41). Read it as a quick, nervous hare: a fast two-legged hop with the ears flapping at half the stride rate, the ears twitching gently when it stops.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Two branches on the walking speed (ModelEasterBunny.java:115-121). On the move (above 0.1): the cosine of the age x 2.6 x 0.55 (wingspeed, :21), x pi x 0.15, x the walking speed swings the left leg and foot forward and the right pair back - 27 degrees at full speed every 4.4 ticks (:122-123); the ears take the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.55, x pi x 0.1, x the walking speed - 18 degrees every 8.8 ticks, half the stride rate - the left around -0.226 rad and the right, negated, around -0.418 (:124-125).
- At a standstill the legs hold 0 and the ears keep the same rhythm at a tenth of the amplitude - a 1.8-degree twitch every 8.8 ticks (:120).
- The body, upper body, head, nose, paws and tail never move; nothing reads the look (netHeadYaw unused), so the LookAtPlayerGoal (EasterBunny.java:54) turns nothing visible. There is no attacking flag and no strike; the egg laying (:110-111, :116-237) is a drop with no pose of its own.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs hop in opposition, each foot swinging with its leg — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 2.6 * 0.55) * pi * 0.15 * limbSwingAmount : 0; lleg.xRot = lfoot.xRot = a; rleg.xRot = rfoot.xRot = -a` (ModelEasterBunny.java:115-123)
- **ears**: the ears flap in opposition at half the stride rate on the move and twitch faintly at rest — `e = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.55) * pi * 0.1 * limbSwingAmount : cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.55) * pi * 0.01; lear.xRot = -0.226 + e; rear.xRot = -0.418 - e` (ModelEasterBunny.java:115-121, 124-125)
