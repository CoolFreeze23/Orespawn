## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A small hostile jellyfish: a bell body and four two-part tentacles. It drifts and stings. Read it as a drifting jelly: every tentacle root swaying on its own pair of rhythms, every tip hanging from its root and swaying on two more.

### 4.2 The math

- **tentacle_1**: the first tentacle's root sways on two rhythms; its tip hangs 7 units down the root's direction and sways on two more — `t11.xRot = cos(t*0.55)*pi*0.15; t11.zRot = cos(t*0.35)*pi*0.1; t12.z = t11.z + sin(t11.xRot)*7; t12.x = t11.x - sin(t11.zRot)*cos(t11.xRot)*7; t12.y = t11.y + cos(t11.zRot)*cos(t11.xRot)*7; t12.xRot = cos(t*0.45)*pi*0.15; t12.zRot = cos(t*0.25)*pi*0.1` (ModelIrukandji.setupAnim; IrukandjiGeoReplacement.tentacle)
- **tentacle_2**: the second tentacle the same shape at its own rhythms — `as tentacle_1 with 0.65, 0.45, 0.55, 0.35` (ModelIrukandji.setupAnim; IrukandjiGeoReplacement.tentacle)
- **tentacle_3**: the third tentacle the same shape at its own rhythms — `as tentacle_1 with 0.5, 0.3, 0.4, 0.2` (ModelIrukandji.setupAnim; IrukandjiGeoReplacement.tentacle)
- **tentacle_4**: the fourth tentacle the same shape at its own rhythms — `as tentacle_1 with 0.57, 0.37, 0.48, 0.29` (ModelIrukandji.setupAnim; IrukandjiGeoReplacement.tentacle)
- `ModelIrukandji.setupAnim` (ModelIrukandji.java; orig ModelIrukandji.java:90-133, `render`): four identical blocks, one per tentacle, each with four rhythms - for tentacle 1 (root t11, tip t12): the root pitches by the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.55, times pi, times 0.15 (27 degrees every 11.4 ticks); the tip's z becomes the root's z plus the sine of that pitch times 7; the root rolls by the cosine of the age times 0.35, times pi, times 0.1 (18 degrees every 18 ticks); the tip's x becomes the root's x minus the sine of that roll times the cosine of the pitch times 7, and its y the root's y plus the cosine of the roll times the cosine of the pitch times 7 - the tip hangs 7 units along the root's direction, a POSITION write (orig :90-98); the tip then pitches by the cosine of the age times 0.45, times pi, times 0.15 and rolls by the cosine of the age times 0.25, times pi, times 0.1 (orig :99-100).
