## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the first Tier-1 slice (T1a, 2026-09-15; hitbox profiles excluded - the classic single hitbox stays until the profile phase). Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Leonopteryx is the canonical registry of Leon, the tameable, rideable flying lion-dragon — one EntityLeon class behind both ids, `leon` the save-compat alias (ModEntities.java:530-547); this sheet and `leon`'s describe the same rig and code. The rig is a chest and abdomen, a three-segment neck to a crested head with an upper and a lower jaw, three sails above and three below the jaws, eye ridges and two-piece antennae, two-segment arms each carrying two wing panels, three-finger wing fans with claws at their tips, a broad back panel and a long tail-fin strip a side, and two-segment legs with feet — every part twice over, one skeleton for standing and one for flight (LeonModel.createBodyLayer, LeonModel.java:257-751; standingParts / flyingParts :233-255; one set visible per frame, :774-776). It has 250 HP and 55 attack (createAttributes, EntityLeon.java:231-238; four times that against the Kraken, and 55 through the Ender Dragon's parts, doHurtTarget :431-461, knockback 1.25). Standing it wanders, follows its owner, is tempted by beef and looks about (goals :153-158) and takes to the air when it finds prey or on a rare roll (alwaysDo :594-630); flying it steers itself to a flight target, hunts monsters — and players while untamed — on a 1-in-8 roll and bites within 7 blocks plus half the target's width (flyWithoutRider :633-797), a tamed one fleeing when below a quarter health (:704-712). A diamond block tames it at once and beef one time in three; an item sits and stands it, a dead bush untames it, an empty hand mounts it (mobInteract :871-962); ridden, it flies on the client-predicted RiderFlightController (:97-108, tickRidden :346-352) and bites for its rider within 9 blocks (flyWithRider :556-578). Being hurt wakes it into flight and turns it on the attacker (hurt :464-484). It spawns by day above y 50 on a 1-in-16 dice with no other Leon within 48 blocks (checkSpawnRules :976-985); the Chaos biome lists `leon` (chaos_biome.json). Read it as two creatures on one rig: a heavy walker, and a wide-winged flyer whose whole body pumps with the beat.
