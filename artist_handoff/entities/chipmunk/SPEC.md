## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A tiny chipmunk: a body and a body-tail block, a neck, a head with a nose, two cheeks, a mouth and two ears, two hidden hat pieces, four legs and a two-segment tail (ModelChipmunk.java:42-59; 18 parts on a 64x32 sheet, drawn at 0.9 and half that as a baby). It is a skittish pet from the cannon-fodder family (Chipmunk.java:45-47): 5 HP, speed 0.38, an unused 1-damage attribute (:82-87) - it never strikes. An apple tames it one time in two (:131-155), a dead bush releases it (:157-172), a name tag names it, a click sits it (:184-189); a carrot, potato or quinoa gives it a team hat and activates it (EntityCannonFodder.java:83-136). It floats (:65), breeds (:66), follows its owner (:67), runs from monsters and players (:68, :73), panics (:72), watches players and mobs (:74-75), wanders (:76), shelters indoors (:79), heals slowly (:107-109) and now and then digs the dirt or farmland out from under itself (:111-118). It spawns above y 50 with at most two others near (:227-232). Read it as a nervous rodent: a quick scamper, the head swivelling with the look, the tail wagging - frozen mid-wag when it sits.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (ModelChipmunk.java:161-167): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 2.3 (ANIM_SPEED 1.0, :20), x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed swings the front right and back left legs one way and the other pair the opposite - a 45-degree scamper at full speed every 2.7 ticks. Below the threshold the legs snap to their rests.
- The head follows 0.45 of the look yaw (:169-178) and takes nine parts with it - the nose, both ears, the mouth, both cheeks and the two hidden hat pieces - each set to the same yaw one by one; no pitch.
- The tail (:180-185): unless the chipmunk is in its sitting pose, the base takes 0.306 rad plus a slow wag (the cosine of the age x 0.25, x pi x 0.06 - 11 degrees every 25 ticks) plus a stride bob (the cosine of the age x 1.3, x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed - 45 degrees at full speed every 4.8 ticks, no threshold), and the tip takes the base's angle plus another 0.306. Sitting, the branch is skipped and the tail keeps whatever angle it had.
- The body, rump and neck never move; the two hat pieces are hidden every frame (:187-188). There is no attacking flag; the strike is the cannon-fodder base's, once activated (IS_ACTIVATED 2): on a 1-in-5 tick a target within 3 blocks is hurt for 3 on the Chipmunk's own row of the species table (swing gate 6; EntityCannonFodder.java:273-290, a direct mobAttack hurt the trigger inventory reads now - TEST-011 (c), owner 2026-09-14, item 5).
- The renderer halves the scale for a baby (ChipmunkRenderer.java:39-43).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: a fast four-legged scamper: front right with back left, front left with back right — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 2.3 * 1.0) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount : 0; leg1.xRot = leg3.xRot = a; leg2.xRot = leg4.xRot = -a` (ModelChipmunk.java:161-167)
- **tail_wag**: the tail wags slowly up and down at rest and bobs hard with the stride; when the chipmunk sits the tail stays wherever it was — `if not sitting: tail1.xRot = 0.306 + cos(t * 0.25) * pi * 0.06 + cos(t * 1.3 * 1.0) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount; tail2.xRot = 0.306 + tail1.xRot` (ModelChipmunk.java:180-185)
