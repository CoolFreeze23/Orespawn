## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Kraken is a flying squid the size of a tower: a mantle head with two eyes, a siphon jet and an eight-flap mouth ringed by 41 teeth, a front, centre and back body, a three-piece tail with two fins, four eight-segment tentacles dangling below and two eight-segment arm tentacles ending in suckers (ModelKraken.createBodyLayer, ModelKraken.java:249-594). It has 1000 HP and 40 attack (MobStats.KRAKEN, createAttributes Kraken.java:107-115), a blue boss bar (:57-58), fears no fire (:131-133) and never touches the ground: it steers itself between flight targets it re-picks on arrival or a 1-in-250 roll (customServerAiStep :261-290, pickNewFlightTarget :292-358, applyFlightMovement :453-460), brings rain and thunder wherever it goes (:204-220) and drops a lightning bolt sixteen blocks below itself on a 1-in-400 roll (:251-259). On a 1-in-8 tick it looks for the nearest survival player in sight, or half the time for any living prey — ground-bound things and unridden mounts (:663-685) — flies to fifteen blocks above them and, when the point fifteen below itself is within reach, GRABS them (searchForPrey :371-396, attackWithSomething :541-552): the victim is carried fifteen blocks beneath it toward y 200, squeezed on a 1-in-50 roll and let go above y 190, on a 1-in-250 roll or when the Kraken is hurt (handleCaughtEntity :398-444, hurt :562-586). Hurt by a player above quarter health it turns on them (:565-570); below a quarter it climbs, and at an eighth of its health above y 130 it calls ten more Krakens (:332-357). It leaves the world above y 256 or after 3600 ticks (:287-289, :136-145), and spawns above y 50 under a clear column (checkSpawnRules :718-723). Read it as a slow airborne squid: tentacles trailing in long waves, the two arms snapping down for the grab.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The two tail fins roll on their own rhythms — the right 27 degrees at 0.43 rad/tick, the left 25 degrees at 0.32 — so they never agree (ModelKraken.java:599-600). The head, eyes, jet, the three body blocks and the tail itself are static; nothing reads the walking speed, the walk position or the look angles.
