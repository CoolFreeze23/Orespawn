## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the remainder slice (2026-09-15; hitbox profiles excluded - the classic single hitbox stays until the profile phase). The Princess is the Prince's sister on the same tiny three-headed rig with three power orbs added: a body with a back fin, a neck base and three two-piece necks each ending in a head with a snout, a head fin and a hinged jaw, two one-piece legs, three-panel wings a side, a five-piece tail and three spinning power orbs, one over each neck (ModelThePrincess.createBodyLayer, ModelThePrincess.java:100-179). She is a TamableAnimal with 400 HP, 0.32 speed and 10 attack (createAttributes ThePrincess.java:125-133; her bite is 9, :209-218), tames herself to the nearest player within ten blocks (:251-258), follows, is tempted by beef, looks about and wanders (:104-123), and flies at activity 2 with no physics (:165; a 1-in-100 tick rolls a 1-in-20 flight, :285-286; when hurt, when her owner flies or is far, :220-224, :290-311). Her brain is the Prince's (doMovement :466-515): on a 1-in-7 tick with a target she flies at it with the attacking flag up and bites within three blocks plus half its width, or fires a fireball, a thunderbolt or an ice ball from three blocks ahead between five and twelve blocks with her fire lit and her head aimed (:643-691). Her power builds one a tick, five while attacking, and at 500 she either fires three purple powers if attacking (firePurplePower :330-340) or blooms — terraforms the ground below her and hatches butterflies and cockateils (bloom :400-425) — the level synced as DATA_POWER (:242-244, :263-277), sparkling with fireworks above 400 (:178-190). Her three necks rise and fall on head extensions random-walked between 0 and 60 (:167-173). She spawns wherever she is placed (checkSpawnRules :791-794; no biome file names her). Read her as the Prince with orbs: quick little wing-beats, three bobbing heads, and three spheres spinning on their own axes.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The two little legs stride 45 degrees at top speed at 1.3 rad/tick above a walking-speed threshold of 0.1, and tuck up at −1 rad in flight unless she is attacking (ModelThePrincess.java:197-206; the activity byte is ThePrincess.java:66, 2 in the air).
- The wings beat 72 degrees x the walking speed at 1.495 rad/tick when she moves or the attacking flag is set, and stir 7 degrees at 0.195 otherwise (:187-195) — the beat is scaled by the walking speed even in flight. The tail wags along five segments (the rig has no tail1), the tip 4.6 times the root — 11 degrees at 0.585 rad/tick at rest, 22 at 0.845 attacking, still when sitting (:208-227).
- The three heads share the look: two thirds of the look yaw and pitch go to the heads on the side the look turns to and half of that to the others, each upper neck half its head's yaw (:229-264, :278-289); the lower necks pitch by the three head extensions, which random-walk between 0 and 60 degrees (ThePrincess.java:167-173; ModelThePrincess.java:291-296). The jaws chomp 36 degrees on three rates while attacking (:266-276).
- The three power orbs tumble on every axis by fixed increments per render frame (:319-354) — a frame-rate-dependent spin the model keeps in its own part state; the body, back fin and neck base are static.
- The bite (doHurtTarget, ThePrincess.java:209-218), the canon trio (:643-691), the purple-power volley (:330-340) and the bloom (:400-425) are rolled by doMovement and customServerAiStep — nothing in the code fires an animation for them.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two little legs stride with the walking speed on the ground and tuck up in flight — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 2.0 * 0.65) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; (activity != 2 || attacking) ? (Rleg1.xRot = a, Lleg1.xRot = -a) : (Rleg1.xRot = Lleg1.xRot = -1)` (ModelThePrincess.java:197-206; wingspeed 0.65 (:21))
- **wings**: the wings beat fast and wide with the speed or when attacking, and stir slowly at rest — `w = (amount > 0.1 || attacking) ? cos(t * 2.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.4 * amount : cos(t * 0.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.04; Rwing.zRot = w - 0.4; Rwing2.zRot = w - 0.6; Rwing3.zRot = w - 0.2; Lwing.zRot = -w + 0.4; Lwing2.zRot = -w + 0.6; Lwing3.zRot = -w + 0.2` (ModelThePrincess.java:187-195)
- **tail**: the tail wags, the swing growing toward the tip — faster and wider while attacking, still when sitting — `g = sitting ? 0 : (attacking ? cos(t * 1.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.12 : cos(t * 0.9 * 0.65) * pi * 0.06); tail2.yRot = g; tail3 = 1.6g; tail4 = 2.6g; Tail5 = 3.6g; Tail6 = 4.6g; positions along the chain` (ModelThePrincess.java:208-227)
- **jaws**: the three jaws chomp on three slightly different rates while the attacking flag is set — `Ljx = attacking ? 0.2 + cos(t * 1.9 * 0.65) * pi * 0.2 : 0; Rjx = attacking ? 0.2 + cos(t * 2.1 * 0.65) * pi * 0.2 : 0; jx = attacking ? 0.2 + cos(t * 2.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.2 : 0; Ljaw.xRot = pitch_L + Ljx; jaw.xRot = pitch_C + jx; Rjaw.xRot = pitch_R + Rjx` (ModelThePrincess.java:266-289)
- **power_orbs**: the three power orbs tumble on all three axes at their own rates — the code adds a fixed increment per RENDER FRAME, not per tick, so the spin runs at the frame rate — `each frame: Lpower.xRot += 0.03, .yRot += 0.035, .zRot += 0.05; Cpower += 0.04 / 0.046 / 0.13; Rpower += 0.05 / 0.065 / 0.03; each wrapped to (-pi, pi]` (ModelThePrincess.java:319-354)
- `ModelThePrincess.poseFrom` (ModelThePrincess.java:197-369; orig ModelThePrincess.java:289-485 in `render` (func_78088_a, :283-487; func_78087_a at :495-497 is a bare super call), wingspeed 0.65 from `new ModelThePrincess(0.65f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:511 - the port's WINGSPEED; the Prince's pose with three power orbs): while the walking speed is above a tenth or the entity is attacking, the wing angle is the cosine of the age in ticks times 2.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.4, times the walking speed, else the cosine of the age times 0.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.04; the right wing's three parts roll by it minus 0.4, 0.6 and 0.2, the left wing's by its negative plus 0.4, 0.6 and 0.2. While the walking speed is above a tenth the legs pitch by the cosine of the age times 2.0 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed (the right positive, the left negative), else 0 - unless the activity is 2 and the entity is not attacking, when both tuck at -1.0. The tail sway is the cosine of the age times 0.9 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.06, stilled to 0 while ordered to sit and replaced by the cosine of the age times 1.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.12 while attacking: the first ring yaws by it and the four rings after by 1.6, 2.6, 3.6 and 4.6 times it, each placed 6, 5, 4 and 4 units from the ring before along the cosine (z) and sine (x) of the previous ring's yaw. The look: two thirds of the look yaw and pitch go to the side head on the side the head turns to - the left heads when the yaw is negative, else the right - and half of that to the centre head and the other side head; each head's head, snout, fin and jaw yaw by its share in radians and its neck by half of it; the heads pitch by their pitch share, the jaws adding, while attacking, 0.2 plus the cosine of the age times 1.9 (the left), 2.1 (the right) and 2.3 (the centre) times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.2. The three necks pitch by the entity's head extensions in degrees (0 at rest) and each head group (head, snout, fin, jaw) is placed 7 units from its neck - minus the cosine of the neck's pitch in y, minus the sine of its pitch in z, minus the sine of its yaw times the sine of its pitch in x; the jaws' earlier writes from the snout are dead. The three power orbs advance every axis by a constant PER RENDERED FRAME on the model's own parts - 0.03, 0.04 and 0.05 about X, 0.035, 0.046 and 0.065 about Y, 0.05, 0.13 and 0.03 about Z (left, centre, right) - each wrapped below pi: not a function of time but of the frames drawn, frame-rate dependent in the classic, and carried the same way by the hook (each bone's previous frame plus the step).
