## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

The middle burrowing worm: a tail, a body, a head with an inner copy and four teeth. It erupts and bites. Read it as a three-link chain rearing up: the tail swaying at the root, the body hanging from it, the head from the body, the teeth snapping around the maw.

### 4.2 The math

- **tail**: the root link sways on a pitch and a roll rhythm — `tail.xRot = cos(t*0.45)*pi*0.1; tail.zRot = cos(t*0.25)*pi*0.08` (WormMediumModel.setupAnim; WormMediumGeoReplacement.java)
- **body**: the body hangs 12 units along the tail's direction (a position follow) and sways on its own two rhythms — `body.z = tail.z - sin(tailX)*12; body.x = tail.x + sin(tailZ)*cos(tailX)*12; body.y = tail.y - 12 + (12 - cos(tailZ)*cos(tailX)*12); body.xRot = cos(t*0.35)*pi*0.1; body.zRot = cos(t*0.15)*pi*0.07` (WormMediumModel.setupAnim; WormMediumGeoReplacement.java)
- **head**: the head and its inner copy hang 12 units along the body's direction and nod around a 36-degree rear — `head.pos = body.pos followed the same way by 12; head.xRot = 0.62 + cos(t*0.55)*pi*0.15; head.zRot = cos(t*0.25)*pi*0.05` (WormMediumModel.setupAnim; WormMediumGeoReplacement.java)
- **teeth**: the four teeth ride one link past the head and snap: the top / bottom pair about X, the side pair about Z — `tooth*.pos = head.pos followed by 12 (+-1 in z for the top / bottom pair, +-1 in x for the side pair); tooth1/2.xRot = headX -/+ (0.4 + a); tooth3/4.zRot = headZ +/- (0.4 + a); a = cos(t*0.55)*pi*0.15` (WormMediumModel.setupAnim; WormMediumGeoReplacement.java)
- `WormMediumModel.setupAnim` (WormMediumModel.java; orig ModelWormMedium.java:79-125, `render`): (1) the tail pitches by the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.45, times pi, times 0.1 (18 degrees every 14 ticks) and rolls by the cosine of the age times 0.25, times pi, times 0.08 (orig :79, :83); (2) the body's pivot is the tail's moved 12 units along the tail's angles - z minus the sine of the pitch times 12, x plus the sine of the roll times the cosine of the pitch times 12, y minus 12 plus (12 minus the cosine of the roll times the cosine of the pitch times 12) - a POSITION write (orig :80-87); the body pitches by the cosine of the age times 0.35, times pi, times 0.1 and rolls by the cosine of the age times 0.15, times pi, times 0.07 (orig :88, :92); (3) the head and its inner copy follow the body the same way by 12 units (orig :89-96), pitch by 0.62 radians plus the cosine of the age times 0.55, times pi, times 0.15 (27 degrees every 11.4 ticks around a 36-degree rear) and roll by the cosine of the age times 0.25, times pi, times 0.05 (orig :97-98); (4) the four teeth take the head's pitch and roll and follow it by 12 units the same way (orig :99-117), the top tooth a unit forward and the bottom a unit back in z, the right a unit out and the left a unit in along x (orig :118-119, :122-123); the snap is the cosine of the age times 0.55, times pi, times 0.15: the top tooth's pitch loses 0.4 radians plus it, the bottom's gains it; the right tooth's roll gains 0.4 plus it, the left's loses it (orig :120-121, :124-125). The walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (WormMediumGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations, the follows through `moveTo`).
