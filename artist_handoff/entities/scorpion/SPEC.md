## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A hostile scorpion: a 6x4x8 body with a small head, eight 11-long legs splayed to the sides, two arms of an upper arm, a forearm and a claw, and a six-piece tail curling up over the back to a stinger (ScorpionModel.java:74-182, 22 parts), drawn at x0.75. 15 HP, 4 damage, 10 armour, speed 0.2 (EntityScorpion.java:80-88). On a 1-in-6 tick it picks prey in an 8-block box: velocity raptors, spiders, creepers and anything living that is not a monster - animals, villagers, survival players - never another scorpion or the emperor (:140-143, :193-209); it chases at 1.2 and, within 3 blocks plus half the target's width, rolls its sting on a 1-in-6 cadence (:67-68; BugMeleeAttackGoal.Params.scorpion; BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:159-174), one hit in three playing scorpion_attack at the victim (:151-160); the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (:43-44) is held at 1 while it stands in reach (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:168). Cactus cannot hurt it (:166-170). It spawns in the dark, at night or below y 50 by day (:211-218; ModEntities.java:287-289). Read it as a scorpion: eight legs rippling in a wave, the claws snapping and the tail whipping at random - rarely at rest, constantly when it is on its prey.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm with no threshold (ScorpionModel.java:193-204): the eight legs sweep 22 degrees x the walking speed about their splay rests at 1.24 rad/tick (a 5-tick period), each pair a quarter cycle behind the one ahead, the right side the mirror of the left - a wave down each flank at the 0.2 speed (EntityScorpion.java:84).
- The claws and the tail play on a per-entity latch (:207-233; EntityScorpion.java:46-53): a 3.4-tick cosine at 1.86 rad/tick is rerolled at every negative-to-positive zero crossing - at rest the left claw plays one cycle in ten, the right one in ten, the tail one in twenty-five; with the synched DATA_ATTACKING int at 1 (held while it stands within 3 blocks plus half the target's width, BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:168) the claws each play one cycle in two and the tail one in three (:221-227). A claw cycle is a 27-degree pinch of the upper arm against its claw with the forearm placed along the arm (:264-276); a tail cycle is a 27-degree curl that grows along segments 1-3 and folds segment 4 back 1.5 times as far, the stinger riding the chain (:278-292). Unselected, each holds its zero pose.
- The body and head are static; the head does not follow the look. The sting is doHurtTarget (EntityScorpion.java:151-160, 4 of damage, scorpion_attack at the victim one time in three) from the goal's swing roll (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:170-173, nextInt(5) == 0 or nextInt(6) == 1 on a 1-in-6 cadence tick in reach) - not an animation event.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the eight legs sweep fore and aft in a wave that runs from the front pair to the rear, the right side mirroring the left — `a_k = cos(t * 2.0 * 0.62 - k * pi/2) * pi * 0.12 * amount for k = 0..3; lleg1.yRot = a_0 + 0.49; lleg2.yRot = a_1 + 0.24; lleg3.yRot = a_2 - 0.24; lleg4.yRot = a_3 - 0.49; rleg1.yRot = -a_0 + 2.65; rleg2.yRot = -a_1 + 2.9; rleg3.yRot = -a_2 - 2.9; rleg4.yRot = -a_3 - 2.65` (ScorpionModel.java:193-204; wingspeed 0.62 (:21))
- **claws**: the claws snap at random: a quick pinch of the upper arm and claw against each other, rare at rest and constant when the scorpion is on its prey — `c = cos(t * 3.0 * 0.62) * pi * 0.15; at each negative-to-positive zero crossing: ri1 = attacking ? nextInt(4) : nextInt(20); left = (ri1 == 1 || ri1 == 3) ? c : 0; right = (ri1 == 2 || ri1 == 3) ? c : 0; larm2.yRot = 0.52 + left; larm1.z = larm2.z - sin(larm2.yRot) * 4.5; lclaw.z = larm1.z - 3; lclaw.yRot = 0.381 - left; rarm2.yRot = 2.61 - right; rarm1.z = rarm2.z - sin(rarm2.yRot) * 4.5; rclaw.z = rarm1.z - 3; rclaw.yRot = -0.381 + right` (ScorpionModel.java:207-229, :264-276; the RenderInfo latch (:207; EntityScorpion.java:46-53, :104-107))
- **tail**: the tail whips up and over at random: a curl that grows along the segments and snaps the stinger forward, rare at rest and frequent when it is on its prey — `w = (ri2 == 1) ? cos(t * 3.0 * 0.62) * pi * 0.15 : 0 with ri2 = attacking ? nextInt(3) : nextInt(25) rerolled at each zero crossing; tail1.xRot = 0.26 + w; tail2.xRot = tail1.xRot + 0.769 + w; tail3.xRot = tail2.xRot + 0.701 + w; tail4.xRot = tail3.xRot - 5.501 - 1.5 w - 0.4; each segment placed along the one before (4, 4, 3, 4, 4 px by sin / cos of its pitch)` (ScorpionModel.java:207-233, :278-292)
