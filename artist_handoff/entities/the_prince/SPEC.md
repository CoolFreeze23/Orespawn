## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Prince is the royal family's hatchling, a tiny three-headed dragon: a body with a back fin, a neck base and three two-piece necks each ending in a head with a snout, a head fin and a hinged jaw, two one-piece legs, three-panel wings a side, and a six-piece tail (ModelThePrince.createBodyLayer, ModelThePrince.java:96-171). It is a TamableAnimal with 500 HP, 0.32 speed and 10 attack (createAttributes ThePrince.java:127-135) that tames itself to the nearest player within ten blocks (:259-266) and then follows, is tempted by beef, looks about and wanders (:106-124), defending its owner and hunting monsters once tame. Its activity byte is 1 on the ground and 2 in the air (no physics when flying, :185; a 1-in-100 tick rolls a 1-in-20 flight, :274-275; it flies when hurt, when its owner flies or is far, :233-239, :279-300). On a 1-in-7 tick with a target it flies at it with the attacking flag up and bites within three blocks plus half the target's width (doHurtTarget :223-230, counting its kills) or, between five and twelve blocks with its fire lit and its head aimed, fires a fireball, a thunderbolt or an ice ball from three blocks ahead (doMovement :358-403, firecanon / firecanonl / firecanoni :482-552); a tamed one below a quarter health flees. Its three necks rise and fall on synced extensions (0 to 60, random-walked, :187-202). After 25 kills, 10 feedings and 10 days it grows into the Prince Teen (:305, transformToTeen :553-560); a diamond block heals it and clears it to grow, a diamond grows it (:616-634), ice and flint and steel take and give its fire (:661-677). It spawns wherever it is placed (checkSpawnRules :750-753; no biome file names it). Read it as a hatchling: quick little wing-beats, three bobbing heads, a tail that wags.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The two little legs stride 45 degrees at top speed at 1.3 rad/tick above a walking-speed threshold of 0.1, and tuck up at −1 rad in flight unless it is attacking (ModelThePrince.java:189-198; the activity byte is ThePrince.java:63, 2 in the air).
- The wings beat 72 degrees x the walking speed at 1.495 rad/tick (a 4.2-tick beat) when it moves or the attacking flag is set, and stir 7 degrees at 0.195 otherwise (:179-187) — the beat is scaled by the walking speed even in flight, so a hovering Prince's wings barely move in the code. The tail wags along five segments, the tip 4.6 times the root — 11 degrees at 0.585 rad/tick at rest, 22 at 0.845 attacking, still when sitting (:200-219).
- The three heads share the look: two thirds of the look yaw and pitch go to the heads on the side the look turns to and half of that to the others, each upper neck half its head's yaw (:221-256, :270-281); the lower necks pitch by the three synced head extensions, which random-walk between 0 and 60 degrees (ThePrince.java:187-202; ModelThePrince.java:283-288). The jaws chomp 36 degrees on three rates while attacking (:258-268).
- The body, back fin, neck base and tail base are static; the snouts and head fins ride their heads.
- The bite (doHurtTarget, ThePrince.java:223-230), the canon trio (:482-552) and the growth into the Teen (:553-560) are rolled by doMovement and customServerAiStep — nothing in the code fires an animation for them.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two little legs stride with the walking speed on the ground and tuck up in flight — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 2.0 * 0.65) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; (activity != 2 || attacking) ? (Rleg1.xRot = a, Lleg1.xRot = -a) : (Rleg1.xRot = Lleg1.xRot = -1)` (ModelThePrince.java:189-198; wingspeed 0.65 (:21))
- **wings**: the wings beat fast and wide with the speed or when attacking, and stir slowly at rest — `w = (amount > 0.1 || attacking) ? cos(t * 2.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.4 * amount : cos(t * 0.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.04; Rwing.zRot = w - 0.4; Rwing2.zRot = w - 0.6; Rwing3.zRot = w - 0.2; Lwing.zRot = -w + 0.4; Lwing2.zRot = -w + 0.6; Lwing3.zRot = -w + 0.2` (ModelThePrince.java:179-187)
- **tail**: the tail wags, the swing growing toward the tip — faster and wider while attacking, still when sitting — `g = sitting ? 0 : (attacking ? cos(t * 1.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.12 : cos(t * 0.9 * 0.65) * pi * 0.06); tail2.yRot = g; tail3 = 1.6g; tail4 = 2.6g; Tail5 = 3.6g; Tail6 = 4.6g; positions along the chain` (ModelThePrince.java:200-219)
- **jaws**: the three jaws chomp on three slightly different rates while the attacking flag is set — `Ljx = attacking ? 0.2 + cos(t * 1.9 * 0.65) * pi * 0.2 : 0; Rjx = attacking ? 0.2 + cos(t * 2.1 * 0.65) * pi * 0.2 : 0; jx = attacking ? 0.2 + cos(t * 2.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.2 : 0; Ljaw.xRot = pitch_L + Ljx; jaw.xRot = pitch_C + jx; Rjaw.xRot = pitch_R + Rjx` (ModelThePrince.java:258-281)
