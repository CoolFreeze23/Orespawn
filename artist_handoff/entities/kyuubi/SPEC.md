## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A burning fox demon that walks upright: a chest and body, a head (built turned 180 degrees and turned back by the code) with two five-piece flame horns sweeping back from it, two-part arms and legs, and a nine-segment tail curling up and over its back - and every body part has a 'Fire' twin drawn over it, 21 pairs on a 512x256 sheet (KyuubiModel.java createBodyLayer). It is hostile: 125 HP, 10 attack, 10 armour (EntityKyuubi.java:65-72, MobStats.KYUUBI), fire-immune (ModEntities.java:254) and sets itself alight every few ticks (:82-89); water burns it (:90-91). Every tenth think tick it picks the nearest living thing that is not a monster within 12 blocks and in sight (:110-114, :131-154), walks at it and, within 8 blocks, hurls a small fireball from 1.25 blocks up on a roll (:115-125); it never bites. It floats (:57), panics when hurt (:58) and spawns anywhere (:180-184). Read it as a flaming, loping biped: arms and legs pumping with the stride, the tail and the two horns rippling in slow travelling waves, the head following the player.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the limbs (KyuubiModel.java:323-348): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 1.1 x 0.5 (wingspeed), x pi x 0.2, x the walking speed pitches the right thigh and shin forward and the left pair back (rests +0.59 / -0.15 and +0.26 / -0.44 rad); the arms take the same cosine at pi x 0.08 x the walking speed (no threshold) plus a 2-degree breathing at 0.5 x 0.5 (:337), the right arm with the right leg. Each shin and forearm is slid 8 units along its upper part's pitch (:328-329, :334-335, :341-342, :347-348) - a hand-written FK chain on flat bones.
- Every body part has a Fire twin (bodyFire, chestFire, headFire, the arm, leg and horn twins) posed to exactly the same angle as its part (:325, :327, :332, :334, :338, :340, :344, :346); the chest, body and tail root never move.
- The head turns to the look (:350-351): the head part is built facing backward (its geo rotation is 180 deg about y) and the code adds pi to netHeadYaw, so `head` faces forward; `headFire` takes the look yaw straight. The horns hang from headFire's yaw: five pieces per side, each placed along the previous piece's yaw (3.6, 2, 4, 3, 2 units) and turned by +-0.244 rad plus a cosine at 1.3 x 0.5 lagging pi/4 per piece (:352-368) - an 18-degree wave running base to tip every 9.7 ticks, mirrored left and right.
- The nine-segment tail carries two travelling waves (:369-410): a sideways one - 36 degrees per segment at 0.9 x 0.5, lagging pi/4 per segment, each segment's x placed along the previous yaw - and a slower up-and-down one at 0.5 x 0.5, 18 degrees around a curl that rises from -0.26 rad at the base to 2.0 rad at the tip, y and z placed along the previous pitch. The tail's bones are flat (no parents) in the rig, so when this rig lands a keyframed wave must key positions as well as rotations, or the tail must be re-parented.
- Nothing in the pose reads the attack: the fireball leaves from 1.25 blocks up (EntityKyuubi.java:120-122) with no wind-up; the entity sets itself on fire every ~10 ticks (:82-89) and puffs smoke when water hurts it (:90-98).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the legs and arms pump with the stride at one rhythm - the right leg and right arm one way, the left leg and left arm the other - the legs three times as wide as the arms; each fire twin moves exactly with its part — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.1 * 0.5) * pi * 0.2 * limbSwingAmount : 0; rtLegUpper(+Fire).xRot = 0.59 + a; rtLegLower(+Fire).xRot = -0.15 + a; lfLegUpper(+Fire).xRot = 0.26 - a; lfLegLower(+Fire).xRot = -0.44 - a; b = cos(t * 1.1 * 0.5) * pi * 0.08 * limbSwingAmount (+ the arm_breath term); rtArmUpper(+Fire).xRot = b; rtArmLower(+Fire).xRot = 0.48 + b; lfArmUpper(+Fire).xRot = -b; lfArmLower(+Fire).xRot = 0.48 - b; every lower part's z = sin(upper xRot) * 8` (KyuubiModel.java:323-348)
- **arm_breath**: a barely visible slow breathing of the arms, present even when standing — `rtArm*.xRot += cos(t * 0.5 * 0.5) * pi * 0.01; lfArm*.xRot -= the same` (KyuubiModel.java:337-348)
- **horns**: a travelling wave runs from the base to the tip of each flame horn, the two sides mirrored, the whole horn turning with the head — `lfHornK.yRot = headFire.yRot + 0.244 + cos(t * 1.3 * 0.5 - (K-1) * pi/4) * pi * 0.1; rtHornK.yRot = headFire.yRot - 0.244 - cos(t * 1.3 * 0.5 - (K-1) * pi/4) * pi * 0.1; each piece's x/z placed along the previous piece's yaw (3.6, 2, 4, 3, 2 units)` (KyuubiModel.java:352-368)
- **tail_sway**: a sideways wave travels up the nine-segment tail from the base to the tip — `tailK.yRot = cos(t * 0.9 * 0.5 - (K-1) * pi/4) * pi * 0.2; tail(K+1).x = tailK.x - sin(tailK.yRot) * L_K (L = 3, 4, 3.5, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1)` (KyuubiModel.java:369-385)
- **tail_curl**: a slower up-and-down ripple runs along the curled tail, the segments' y/z placed along the previous segment's pitch — `tailK.xRot = rest_K + cos(t * 0.5 * 0.5 - (K-1) * pi/4) * pi * 0.1; tail(K+1).y = tailK.y + sin(tailK.xRot) * L_K; tail(K+1).z = tailK.z - cos(tailK.xRot) * L_K` (KyuubiModel.java:386-410)
