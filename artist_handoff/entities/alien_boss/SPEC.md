## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

The Alien Boss is the leader of the WTF-Alien dungeon, a port addition promoted from the Alien (AlienBoss.java:22-39: 1.7.10 registered only the Alien) on the same rig: a torso and stomach on a neck carrying a three-piece head with four fangs and a two-piece lower jaw, a fifteen-blade crest fan, two-segment arms with three claw fingers each, legs of a thigh, a two-piece shin and a foot, and a five-segment spiked tail (ModelAlien.createBodyLayer, ModelAlien.java:136-360), drawn by the Alien's renderer at the Alien's scale (OreSpawnClient.java:53). It has 320 HP, 24 attack, 12 armour, 0.6 knockback resistance; its createAttributes speed of 0.55 (:60-68) is overridden to the Alien's 0.65 by the shared constructor (Alien.java:73), inherits the Alien's goals — village walking, torch breaking, wandering (Alien.java:82-101) — and adds a player hunt (AlienBoss.java:57). Its eighth-tick scan, its four-block attacking flag and its ~40 percent swing are the Alien's (Alien.java:221-246); a hit poisons one time in five (Alien.java:170-194) and withers one time in three for 60 ticks (AlienBoss.java:76-83). Every 200 ticks while its target lives it summons an Alien beside itself, up to three nearby (:86-117); it is immune to its own minions (:126-132) and never despawns (:135-138). It has no natural spawn: the dungeon places it. Read it as the Alien made heavier: the same stalking stride, a wider threat display, and the summon.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The legs stride on one speed-scaled wave at 0.88 rad/tick (a 7.1-tick period) and a full 90 degrees at top speed: the foot, the two shin pieces and the thigh of a leg swing together about their own rests, the right leg opposite the left (ModelAlien.java:367-369, :568-580). The torso and stomach are static.
- The fifteen-blade crest fan lies flat and still until the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (Alien.java:48, raised at :230 within four blocks of a target — the boss inherits the scan, AlienBoss.java:86-87); then it spreads into a fan, 15 degrees per blade, and ripples at 0.268 rad/tick, each blade a twelfth of a period behind the last (:370-435).
- The tail, the jaw and the two arms share one 0.77 rad/tick wave (an 8.2-tick period) but each moves hard only in the periods a per-entity random latch picks for it, re-rolled at every rising zero-crossing (:467-480): at rest the tail in 1 period of 15, the jaw in 1 of 15, each arm in 2 of 15; attacking the tail in 1 of 2, the jaw always, each arm in 2 of 4. The parts not picked sway, chomp or twitch faintly on a slow 0.22 rad/tick wave (:481-506). The jaw and claw waves are rectified (absolute values, :583, :615-643): they open and reach twice per period and never cross their rests.
- The neck turns 0.35 of the look yaw and the head 0.75 of it, the head pieces, fangs and jaw riding the head by position (:436-463); the walk position plays no part.
- The strike (Alien.doHurtTarget :170-194 with the boss's wither at AlienBoss.java:76-83) is rolled by the Alien's customServerAiStep (Alien.java:229-236), and the summon (AlienBoss.java:86-117) fires on a 200-tick cooldown — nothing in the code fires an animation for either.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride with the walking speed, every piece of a leg swinging together about its own rest, the right leg opposite the left — `a = cos(t * 4.0 * 0.22) * pi * 0.5 * amount; lFoot.xRot = a, lShin.xRot = a - 0.4, lShin1.xRot = lThigh.xRot = a - 0.8; rFoot.xRot = -a, rShin.xRot = -a - 0.4, rShin1.xRot = rThigh.xRot = -a - 0.8` (ModelAlien.java:367-369 (doLeftLeg :568-573, doRightLeg :575-580); wingspeed 0.22 (:21))
- **fan**: the crest fan lies flat until the attacking flag is set, then spreads into a fan and ripples blade by blade — `attacking ? (fan.xRot = cos(t * 1.22 * 0.22) * pi * 0.1; fanlK.xRot = fanrK.xRot = cos(t * 1.22 * 0.22 - K * pi/6) * pi * 0.1; fanlK.zRot = 0.261 K, fanrK.zRot = -0.261 K) : (every blade xRot = -1.85, zRot = 0)` (ModelAlien.java:370-435)
