## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A horned, long-tailed dungeon lizard built sideways: a body with three spines, a neck, a three-piece head with two eyes, two horns and a horn base on each side carrying four horn segments, two three-piece jaws that flap open sideways, a shoulder each side, two legs of thigh, shin, heel, two foot pieces and three toes, and a seven-segment tail with eleven spines along it (ModelDungeonBeast.java:89-152; 64 parts on a 128x64 sheet). The model is laid along the x axis and turned 90 degrees about the vertical when drawn (:523-528), so its 'z' rotations are the fore-and-aft swings in the world. It is hostile: 65 HP, 12 damage, 6 armour, speed 0.29 (MobStats.DUNGEON_BEAST, DungeonBeast.java:87-95), 60 XP. Every eighth tick it scans 16 blocks for anything alive and in sight - rats, dungeon beasts, rotators, peacocks, irukandji, skates, whales, flounders and creative players excepted (:123-185) - and hands it to its bug melee goal (:72-73): a chase at 1.2, a bite within 2.8 blocks on 1-in-7-or-1-in-8 dice, the synched attacking flag raised in reach and cleared out of it (:116-121; BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:145-176). It ignores wall and cactus damage (:212-218), floats (:71), strolls (:74) and spawns in darkness - in the Crystal dimension only at y 25-28 under an open ring (:220-238). Read it as a heavy crawling lizard: a stride on splayed legs, a wave running down the back spines, the tail swinging in a lash when it hunts, the jaws flapping open sideways.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (ModelDungeonBeast.java:425-434): the cosine of the age x 1.4 x 0.62 (wingspeed, :22), x pi x 0.22, x the walking speed swings the right thigh, shin, heel and both foot pieces one way and the left set the other - 40 degrees at full speed every 7.2 ticks, no threshold - and the middle toe of each foot with its leg around a -0.785 rad rest; the other toes are still. The rotations are about z because the model is built along the x axis and turned 90 degrees at render (:523-528).
- A wave runs down the fourteen spines (:424, :435-448): the cosine of the age x 0.5 x 0.62, x pi x 0.07 (12.6 degrees every 20.3 ticks), each spine a further pi/8 along the wave than the one before, the tail-side spines negated.
- The tail sways (:449-488): the cosine of the age x 0.75 x 0.62, x pi x 0.25, x an amount that is the walking speed when calm and a fixed 1.25 when the synched attacking flag is up, taken at 0.25 through 1.75 along the seven segments (an 11-degree base and a 79-degree tip at amount 1, a 98-degree tip lash when attacking); each next segment's position is re-derived 6, 5, 4.5, 4, 3 and 3 units along the previous segment's yaw and the tail spines are placed with their segment - an FK chain written by hand on flat bones (when the rig lands a keyframed sway must key the positions too, or the tail must be parented).
- The jaws flap on a latch (:492-518): at every boundary of the 5.1-tick jaw cycle a per-entity roll picks nextInt(15) when calm - the flap plays for that cycle on a 0 - and 0 while attacking, so the two three-piece jaws flap 27 degrees open and shut every cycle during a hunt and about one cycle in fifteen at rest; otherwise they sit at their rests.
- The body, neck, head pieces, eyes, horns, shoulders and the outer toes never move; nothing reads the look. The attacking flag lives in the bug melee goal (DungeonBeast.java:72-73 hands setAttacking to it; raised in reach and cleared out of it, BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:145-176) - a held state, read by the generator's inventory through the goal it is handed to (TEST-011 (b), owner 2026-09-14, item 5: classified STATE); the bite is doHurtTarget inside the goal (:172), transport 1.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride in opposition, thigh, shin, heel, both foot pieces and the middle toe of a leg swinging together — `n = cos(ageInTicks * 1.4 * 0.62) * pi * 0.22 * limbSwingAmount; rleg1/rleg2/rheel/rfoot/rfoot2.zRot = n; rtoe2.zRot = -0.785 + n; lleg1/lleg2/lheel/lfoot/lfoot2.zRot = -n; ltoe2.zRot = -0.785 - n` (ModelDungeonBeast.java:425-434)
- **spines**: a slow ripple runs from the shoulders down the spines to the tail tip — `spine_k.xRot = (+ or -) cos(ageInTicks * 0.5 * 0.62 + k * pi/8) * pi * 0.07 for k = 0..13 in the order bodys1-3, t1s1-3, t2s1-3, t3s1-2, t4s1, t5s1, t6s1 (t2s* onward negated); pi4 in the code is pi/8 (0.3927)` (ModelDungeonBeast.java:424, 435-448)
- **tail**: the tail sweeps side to side with the stride, wider toward the tip and still when standing; when it hunts the sweep runs full and wide whether it moves or not — `amp = attacking ? 1.25 : limbSwingAmount; n = cos(ageInTicks * 0.75 * 0.62) * pi * 0.25 * amp; tail1.yRot = n * 0.25 (t1s1-3 the same); tail2.yRot = n * 0.5 ... tail7.yRot = n * 1.75; each segment's x/z placed along the previous segment's yaw (6, 5, 4.5, 4, 3, 3 units) and its spines placed with it` (ModelDungeonBeast.java:449-488)
- **jaws**: the two three-piece jaws flap open sideways and shut - constantly while it hunts, a rare twitch at rest — `j = latch ? cos(ageInTicks * 2.0 * 0.62) * pi * 0.15 : 0; ljaw1.yRot = -0.349 + j; ljaw2.yRot = 0.349 + j; ljaw3.yRot = 0.523 + j; rjaw1.yRot = 0.349 - j; rjaw2.yRot = -0.349 - j; rjaw3.yRot = -0.523 - j` (ModelDungeonBeast.java:492-518)
