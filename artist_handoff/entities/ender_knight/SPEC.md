## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A towering armoured enderman: a pelvis, three spine pieces and four ribs up to a neck, collars and shoulders, a head, two three-piece arms - the right one holding a sword of handle and blade - two legs of thigh, two shin pieces and a hip, four foot pieces each, and a two-piece cape (ModelEnderKnight.java:64-103; 40 parts on a 512x512 sheet; 2.9 blocks tall). It is a hostile enderman in mail: 60 HP, 12 damage, 6 armour, speed 0.32, a 64-block follow range (MobStats.ENDER_KNIGHT, EnderKnight.java:202-210). It attacks a player who looks at it - the nearest player within 64 blocks whose gaze lines up with its chest and who has no pumpkin on (:90-144, :187-200), the enderman stare rule - or whoever hurts it (:63-74), closing with a vanilla melee goal (:56). While it has a target it SCREAMS (a synched flag, :40-41, :119, :342) and teleports: away when the starer comes within 4 blocks, toward a target more than 16 blocks away (:264-286), at random when hit by an arrow or magic (:339-349), in daylight (:243-255) and when wet or burning (:257-262, :228-230); portal particles stream off it (:232-241). It spawns in darkness at night above y 30 (:366-374). Read it as an enderman knight: a long-legged stride, the cape stirring, arms folded and the sword low at rest - then, screaming, the arms thrown up and the sword whirling.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (ModelEnderKnight.java:276-291): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.21 (wingspeed, :21), x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed is added to every piece of the left leg (thigh, two shin pieces around -0.1 rad, four foot pieces, the second around +0.6) and subtracted from the right - a 45-degree stride at full speed every 23 ticks, each leg one rigid unit; the cape's lower piece rolls by a quarter of it. Below the threshold the legs snap to their rests.
- The cape's lower piece also stirs at all times (:292): the cosine of the age x 0.7 x 0.21, x pi x 0.02 - 3.6 degrees every 42.7 ticks; the upper piece is static.
- The head turns 0.45 of the look yaw, clamped to +-0.45 rad (:293-299), no pitch.
- The arms and sword branch on the synched screaming flag (:300-322). Calm: the upper arms at -0.5 rad, the forearms at -1.0 and yawed +1.0 (left) and -1.0 (right) - folded across the chest - the sword handle and blade at 0.35; nothing moves. Screaming: the cosine of the age x 2.7 x 0.21, x pi x 0.3 (54 degrees every 11.1 ticks) is added to -1.2 on the four upper-arm pieces and to -1.8 on the forearms, both arms together, and one and a half times it to 0.5 on the sword - the arms thrown high and pumping, the sword whirling 81 degrees. Each forearm is placed 10 units along its upper arm's pitch and the sword 7 units along the right forearm's (:323-328): a hand-written FK chain on flat bones (when the rig lands a keyframed swing must key the positions too, or the arms must be parented).
- The pelvis, spine, ribs, neck, collars, shoulders, hips and upper cape never move. The screaming flag is a held state (EnderKnight.java:119 on a stare, :342 on a hit; cleared at :124, :252, :260, :283) under its own name, which the generator's inventory reads as the held aggro state (TEST-011 (c), owner 2026-09-14, item 5: no attacking flag, a vanilla MeleeAttackGoal, the flag raised in the target goal's findTarget and cleared on target loss); the strike is vanilla's MeleeAttackGoal (:56), whose doHurtTarget call is in vanilla code - the inventory records it as the strike site now.
- The teleports (EnderKnight.java:290-337) are instant position changes with portal particles and the enderman teleport sound at both ends; the body itself does not animate them.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the long legs stride in opposition, every piece of a leg together, and the cape swings a little with the step — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.21) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount : 0; lfoot1/3/4.xRot = a; lfoot2.xRot = 0.6 + a; lleg1.xRot = a; lleg2/lleg3.xRot = -0.1 + a; the right pieces the same rests - a; cape2.zRot = a / 4` (ModelEnderKnight.java:276-291)
- **cape_sway**: the cape's lower piece stirs slowly fore and aft — `cape2.xRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.7 * 0.21) * pi * 0.02` (ModelEnderKnight.java:292)
- **arms**: at rest the arms are folded across the chest with the sword held low; screaming, both arms rise high and pump together with the sword whirling wider still — `s = cos(ageInTicks * 2.7 * 0.21) * pi * 0.3; screaming: larm2/larm3/rarm2/rarm3.xRot = -1.2 + s; larm1/rarm1.xRot = -1.8 + s; handle/blade.xRot = 0.5 + s * 1.5; calm: larm2/larm3/rarm2/rarm3.xRot = -0.5; larm1.xRot = rarm1.xRot = -1.0, larm1.yRot = 1.0, rarm1.yRot = -1.0; handle/blade.xRot = 0.35; larm1/rarm1 y/z placed 10 units along larm2/rarm2's pitch; handle/blade placed 7 units along rarm1's pitch (+1 on y)` (ModelEnderKnight.java:300-328)
