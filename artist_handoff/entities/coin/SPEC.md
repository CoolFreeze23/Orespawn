## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A single gold coin standing on edge, hovering in place in daylight above height 50. It has 1 HP, no sound, no reaction and no movement; it only turns about its vertical axis, swinging through a full turn and back every 28.6 seconds. Players collect it. There is nothing to animate beyond the spin the code already does.

### 4.2 The math

- **spin**: the coin turns about its vertical axis, swinging through a full turn and back every 571 ticks (28.6 s) — `coin.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.05 * 0.22) * pi   (WINGSPEED = 0.22)` (ModelCoin.java:26,47-50; CoinGeoReplacement.java:39-42)
- `ModelCoin.setupAnim` (ModelCoin.java:47-50; orig ModelCoin.java:28-32, `render`, the `f2 * 0.05f * wingspeed` line): the disc's yaw is the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.05, times the wingspeed 0.22 (ModelCoin.java:26; the Coin's registration in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn), times pi — a swing from +180 degrees through -180 and back, one full swing every 571.2 ticks (2 pi / 0.011, 28.6 s), never a constant-rate spin; the walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same line (CoinGeoReplacement.java:37-40).
