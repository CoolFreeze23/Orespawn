## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A hostile armoured bug that spits acid: a fifteen-plate abdomen on a three-piece hip hub, four legs of a three-piece coxa, a root, four femur plates, a tibia and two tarsus plates each, an upper jaw of a base and three plates with five teeth, a twelve-plate lower jaw, two four-piece palps and two eyes (SpitBugModel.java:212-681, 93 parts), drawn at x0.75. 100 HP, 10 damage, 12 armour, speed 0.33 (EntitySpitBug.java:97-105). It hops forward and up when it jumps (:145-156), takes one hit per 15 ticks and ignores falls and cactus (:177-202). It spawns in the dark, by day only on a 2-in-20 roll, with clear air about it (:305-313; ModEntities.java:292-294). Read it as a heavy beetle: four legs scuttling with a lift, the upper jaw twitching at rest and chomping fast when it fights, a spit from the mouth.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm with no threshold (SpitBugModel.java:691-706): a sine at 1.1 rad/tick (a 5.7-tick period) sweeps each leg's eight moving pieces 22 degrees x the walking speed about the root's splay, the left front and left rear in opposition and the right pair the same half a cycle later; on the rising half of the sine a |cos| lift of the same size bends the femur plates up and the tibia and tarsus down - the step. Every leg is placed link by link along its yaw (14, 14 and 8 px; :813-943).
- One branch on the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (EntitySpitBug.java:45-46), held at 1 while it stands within 3 blocks plus half the target's width (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:168) or while an acid burst streams (SpitBugAcidAttackGoal.java:60) and dropped otherwise (:132, :145, :176): the three upper jaw plates and front teeth lift 2.7 degrees on a slow one-sided wave at rest and chomp 18 degrees at 1.43 rad/tick while it is up (:707-713).
- The hip hub, the three coxa pieces of each leg, the fifteen abdomen plates, the upper jaw base, teeth 4 and 5, the twelve lower jaw plates, both palps and the eyes are static; there is no head bone and no look. The bite (doHurtTarget, EntitySpitBug.java:158-175), the acid rounds (SpitBugAcidAttackGoal.java:66-93) and the hop (EntitySpitBug.java:145-156) are not animation events.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four legs sweep about the vertical in two opposed pairs and each leg lifts at the knee as it swings forward, the whole leg placed link by link — `s = sin(t * 2.0 * 0.55) * pi * 0.12 * amount; up = (sin at t + 0.1 > s) ? |cos(t * 2.0 * 0.55) * pi * 0.12 * amount| : 0; leftFront(s, up); leftRear(-s, up); s' = sin(t * 2.0 * 0.55 + pi) * pi * 0.12 * amount (up' likewise); rightFront(-s', up'); rightRear(s', up'); per leg: root and every piece yRot = rest + angle; femur plates xRot = rest + up; tibia xRot = 0.669 - up; tarsus xRot = -0.48 - up; positions chained by cos / sin of the yaw x 14, 14, 8` (SpitBugModel.java:691-706 (the phases), :813-943 (the four leg helpers); wingspeed 0.55 (:21))
