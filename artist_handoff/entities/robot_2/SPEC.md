## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A six-block-tall heavy melee chassis: thick legs, a boxy head that turns with its gaze, and two three-segment arms it windmills when it fights. Its signature move is terrain destruction — a landed swing rips blocks out from under the target and every attack tick shreds the ground around it (mobGriefing on); idle, it sometimes throws a 50-tick tantrum at nothing. Slow legs, wild arms.

### 4.2 The math

- **legs**: a slow, stiff stride: the whole left leg swings against the whole right leg (the two segments of a leg move together) — `walk = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t*0.3)*pi*0.12*amount : 0; lleg1.xRot = lleg2.xRot = walk; rleg1.xRot = rleg2.xRot = -walk` (ModelRobot2.java:131-160; Robot2GeoReplacement.java:36-46)
- **arms**: while attacking, at every zero-crossing of sin(t*20deg) the code rolls which arms windmill (1 = right, 2 = left, 3 = both, re-rolled until non-zero); the chosen arm(s) spin a full circle about X; not attacking, both arms hang — `armAngle = rad(t*20); rarm*.xRot = (ri1 in {1,3}) ? armAngle : 0; larm*.xRot = (ri1 in {2,3}) ? armAngle : 0; ri1 lives in the client RenderInfo` (ModelRobot2.java:131-160; Robot2GeoReplacement.java:48-70)
- **head**: the head turns to follow the gaze — `head.yRot = rad(netHeadYaw)` (Robot2GeoReplacement.java:47)
- `ModelRobot2.poseFrom` (ModelRobot2.java:137-188, called from `setupAnim` :131-135; orig ModelRobot2.java:133-170, `render`): (1) the legs — while the walking speed is above a tenth, the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.3, times pi, times 0.12, times the walking speed (up to 21.6 degrees; orig :133-137), the left leg's two parts positive and the right leg's the negative, still below a tenth; (2) the head yaws by the head yaw in radians; (3) the arm choice — at every upward zero crossing of the sine of the age times 20 degrees (the sine 1.5 degrees ahead positive while the sine now is negative) the per-entity `ri1` is reset to 0 and, while attacking, re-rolled from the entity's random until it is 1, 2 or 3 (orig :139-151); (4) the arms — the right arm's three parts pitch by the age times 20 degrees (a full turn every 18 ticks) when `ri1` is 1 or 3, the left arm's when it is 2 or 3, otherwise 0 (orig :152-170). At rest (not attacking) `ri1` stays 0 and only the legs move. The GeckoLib hook is the same (Robot2GeoReplacement.java:34-71).
