## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A drifting purple orb of three concentric rings, each ring six spokes, spinning on different axes at different speeds and shedding firework sparks. It floats through blocks, wanders to random points, sometimes homes on a target and hurts on contact, and the 'type 10' bomb form (the Queen's mad-mood cloud) explodes when it expires. It is an effect made into a creature: pure spin.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Each ring is one classic part drawn six times at 60-degree steps (the __fan groups hold the roll; the __iN clones carry the static step) — Slice 4c's render-instance expansion; the carry groups repeat the earlier rolls the way the original's nested glRotatef calls did.
- Every rendered frame re-rolls the three angles from the level's random (ENT-S-146, fixed 2026-09-06: the earlier port spun the rings at 7.3 / 5.1 / 3.7 degrees per tick, which the original never did); there is no period, no walk, no head.

### 4.2 The math
