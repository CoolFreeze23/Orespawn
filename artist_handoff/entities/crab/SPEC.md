## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A giant crab at one of three sizes: a six-block body (a shell top and bottom, two side plates, a rear block and a face block), two two-piece eyestalks angled outward, two mouthparts, two five-piece claw arms ending in a hinged pincer, and eight three-segment legs drawn from three leg parts (ModelCrab.java:47-71; 25 classic parts on a 256x512 sheet, 46 bones in the rig). The 1.7.10 model drew those three leg parts eight times at eight poses - four legs a side, front to back - so the rig carries twenty-four leg bones drawn from the three parts (`leg1__i0`..`leg1__i7`, `leg2__i0`.., `leg3__i0`..: the converter's render_instances form, owner 2026-09-14, item 3; draws 0-3 the left side at z 0 / 10 / 20 / 30, draws 4-7 the right); the port's setupAnim re-poses the parts eight times in a row (ModelCrab.java:183-234) but its renderToBuffer draws each part once, so the game shows three leg segments - one leg - today (ANIM-025) until the Crab's slice lands the draw fix. It is a hostile scavenger scaled 0.25, 0.5 or 1.0 at spawn (0.35 from a spawner; Crab.java:147-159) with its stats, speed, reach, knockback, XP and hitbox scaled with it (:161-186, :201-229, :130-137): 250 x scale HP, 24 x scale damage, 16 + 2 x scale armour. Every fifth tick it looks 16 blocks for a non-creative player, any monster, a lizard, a rubber ducky, a villager, a girlfriend, a boyfriend or an attackable non-mob (:365-395), raises the synched attacking flag within 6 blocks plus half the prey's width (scaled, :307-309), swings on a 40 percent roll (:311-321, doHurtTarget :212-229 with knockback) and clears the flag when no target is found (:326). It seeks water when dry and dries out without it (:277-290), heals in water (:330-335), ignores cactus and its own kind, takes a hit only every 8 ticks and turns on a mob that hits it (:231-248); it spawns above y 50 by day, rarely in the Crystal dimension (:443-456). Read it as a sidling crab: legs scuttling, eyestalks and mouthparts twitching - faster and wider when it hunts - claws waving, then snapping.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The legs (ModelCrab.java:183-234): the cosine of the age x 1.7, x pi x 0.15, x the walking speed (27 degrees at full speed every 3.7 ticks, no threshold) is added to or subtracted from a +-pi/2 rest on the three leg parts, which the code positions and poses eight times in a row - four on the left side (x = +36, the model's +x side, vanilla's left_arm convention) at z = 0, 10, 20, 30 with alternating sign, four on the right (x = -36) negated. The 1.7.10 original rendered the parts at each pose, and the rig carries every one: twenty-four leg bones, one per part per pose (`leg1__i0`..`leg3__i7`; owner 2026-09-14, item 3), so the artist animates eight three-segment legs. The port's renderToBuffer draws each part once, so only the last pose (x = -36, z = 30, pi/2 + the swing) shows in-game today - three segments, one leg (ANIM-025, the register line stays); the draw fix lands with the Crab's slice, when the game draws all eight.
- Everything else branches on the synched attacking flag (:235-265). Calm: the eyestalks wobble 9 degrees - the left pitching at 0.35 and rolling at 0.25 around +0.54 rad, the right pitching at 0.3 and rolling at 0.45 around -0.54; the mouthparts work 9 degrees at 0.25 around +-0.72; the claws open and shut 5 (left, at 0.15) and 4 (right, at 0.13) degrees around their rests. Attacking: the eyes 18 degrees at 0.45 / 0.35 / 0.4 / 0.55, the mouth 27 degrees at 0.45, the claws 23 (left, at 0.35) and 22 (right, at 0.43) degrees - every rhythm faster and two to five times wider.
- Each claw is five flat bones: the shoulder and arm pieces (Lclaw1, Lclaw2 and the right pair) never move; the hand base and fixed pincer take the wave and the moving pincer its negative, so the claw snaps about the hand. The shell blocks, side plates, rear and face blocks never move; nothing reads the look (netHeadYaw unused), so the LookAtPlayerGoal (Crab.java:92) turns nothing visible.
- The attacking flag is held: set 1 every fifth think tick while the prey is within reach (Crab.java:307-309) and 0 when no target is found (:326) - a state, so the swing (doHurtTarget :212-229, with a knockback of 1.15 x scale) is transport 1.
- The renderer scales the whole rig by the crab's synched size (CrabRenderer.java:37-38): a 0.25 crab is a quarter of the rig, a 1.0 crab the full 5-block-wide monster - author at the rig's scale.
