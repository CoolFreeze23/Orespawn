## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A small passive cricket: a body, a head, an abdomen, four front / rear legs and four hind-leg parts. It hops about and chirps. Read it as a cricket at a walk: the four front / rear legs swinging with its speed, the big hind legs held cocked.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four front / rear legs swing about the vertical with the walk - the front pair one way, the rear pair the other - and hold still below a tenth of walking speed — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.0) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; lfleg.yRot = 0.47 + a; rfleg.yRot = -0.54 + a; lrleg.yRot = -0.296 - a; rrleg.yRot = 0.384 - a` (CricketModel.setupAnim; CricketGeoReplacement.java)
- **hind_legs**: the hind legs hold a cocked pose (written every frame) — `lleg1/rleg1.yRot = +-0.436; lleg2/rleg2.yRot = +-0.349; upper .xRot = 0.558; lower .xRot = -0.366` (CricketModel.setupAnim; CricketGeoReplacement.java)
- `CricketModel.setupAnim` (CricketModel.java; orig ModelCricket.java:104-108, 117-125, `render`, wingspeed 2.5 from `new ModelCricket(2.5f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:510 - the port's frequency is 1.0): while the walking speed is above a tenth, the leg swing is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.0, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed (up to 45 degrees each way every 6.28 ticks); at or below a tenth it is 0; the left front leg yaws by 0.47 radians plus it, the right front by -0.54 plus it, the left rear by -0.296 minus it, the right rear by 0.384 minus it (orig :104-108); the hind legs are written to constants every frame - the upper parts yaw by 0.436 (left) / -0.436 (right) and pitch by 0.558, the lower parts yaw by 0.349 / -0.349 and pitch by -0.366 (orig :117-125, the not-singing branch; 1.7.10 swung them while singing - the register line). The walk position, the head angles and the entity's state play no part in the port's pose. The GeckoLib hook is the same (CricketGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
