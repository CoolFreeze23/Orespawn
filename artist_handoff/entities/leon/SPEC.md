## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


Leon is the tameable, rideable flying lion-dragon — this registry is the save-compat alias of `leonopteryx`, one EntityLeon class behind both ids (ModEntities.java:530-547). The rig is a chest and abdomen, a three-segment neck to a crested head with an upper and a lower jaw, three sails above and three below the jaws, eye ridges and two-piece antennae, two-segment arms each carrying two wing panels, three-finger wing fans with claws at their tips, a broad back panel and a long tail-fin strip a side, and two-segment legs with feet — every part twice over, one skeleton for standing and one for flight (LeonModel.createBodyLayer, LeonModel.java:257-751; standingParts / flyingParts :233-255; one set visible per frame, :774-776). It has 250 HP and 55 attack (createAttributes, EntityLeon.java:231-238; four times that against the Kraken, and 55 through the Ender Dragon's parts, doHurtTarget :431-461, knockback 1.25). Standing it wanders, follows its owner, is tempted by beef and looks about (goals :153-158) and takes to the air when it finds prey or on a rare roll (alwaysDo :594-630); flying it steers itself to a flight target, hunts monsters — and players while untamed — on a 1-in-8 roll and bites within 7 blocks plus half the target's width (flyWithoutRider :633-797), a tamed one fleeing when below a quarter health (:704-712). A diamond block tames it at once and beef one time in three; an item sits and stands it, a dead bush untames it, an empty hand mounts it (mobInteract :871-962); ridden, it flies on the client-predicted RiderFlightController (:97-108, tickRidden :346-352) and bites for its rider within 9 blocks (flyWithRider :556-578). Being hurt wakes it into flight and turns it on the attacker (hurt :464-484). It spawns by day above y 50 on a 1-in-16 dice with no other Leon within 48 blocks (checkSpawnRules :976-985) in the Chaos biome (chaos_biome.json). Read it as two creatures on one rig: a heavy walker, and a wide-winged flyer whose whole body pumps with the beat.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: standing, the legs stride with the walking speed, the arms and the two wing panels a side swing half as far with them, and the broad back panels sway a tenth — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.8 * 0.22) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; leg_1_L.xRot = -0.611 + a, leg_1_R.xRot = -0.611 - a; leg_2_L.xRot = 0.611 + a, leg_2_R.xRot = 0.611 - a (feet by position); wing_3_R.yRot = 0.523 - a/10, wing_3_L.yRot = -0.523 - a/10; arm_1_L.xRot = -0.07 - a/2, arm_1_R.xRot = -0.07 + a/2; wing_1_L/R.xRot = -0.17 -/+ a/2; arm_2_L/R.xRot = -0.471 -/+ a/2; wing_2_L/R.xRot = -0.523 -/+ a/2 (positions along the chain)` (LeonModel.java:761-766, :778-799, :813-816 (the standing branch, activity 0); wingspeed 0.22 (:21))

- **breath**: standing, the chest rocks and the lower jaw works a few degrees in a slow breath — `c = cos(t * 0.6 * 0.22) * pi * 0.02; chest.xRot = chest_ridge.xRot = -0.436 + c/8; bottom_jaw.xRot = -1.308 + c/2; lower_sail1.xRot = 0.297 + c/2; lower_sail2.xRot = 0.384 + c/2; lower_sail_3.xRot = -0.384 + c/2` (LeonModel.java:830-835 (the standing branch))

- **fly_legs**: flying, the legs hang tucked; attacking they reach forward and paddle, shins and feet swinging wider than the thighs — `f = attacking ? cos(t * 3.6 * 0.22) * pi * 0.1 : 0; rest = attacking ? -pi/4 : pi/2; fleg_1_L.xRot = -0.1 + rest + f, fleg_1_R.xRot = -0.1 + rest - f; fleg_2_L.xRot = (attacking ? 0.2 : 0.1) + rest + 1.5f (right −); ffootL.xRot = attacking ? -pi/4 + 2f : pi (right −); positions along the chain` (LeonModel.java:879-924 (the flight branch))
