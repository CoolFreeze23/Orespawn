## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

The Island anchor from the Crystal dimension's skies. Three small cubes tumble slowly around a point in the sky while the entity itself does not move (its movement speed is 0): it is the anchor of a floating sand island the code builds around it and drifts a block at a time. The tumble is decorative; the island is terrain.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Each of the three cubes spins on all three axes at rates near 0.05 rad/tick (a turn every 108-126 ticks), with the rates offset by 0.001 so the three never line up.
- No walk, no head, no state: the entity has no AI goals; its work is block placement in tick().
