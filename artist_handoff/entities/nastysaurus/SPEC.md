## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A hostile theropod the size of a house: a chest and a belly, a three-piece neck to a long skull with a hinged two-piece lower jaw and twenty-three teeth, two three-piece legs on seven-piece clawed feet, a four-piece tail and three back spikes (ModelNastysaurus.java:152-386, 59 parts), drawn at x1.5. 200 HP, 32 damage, 17 armour, speed 0.35 (Nastysaurus.java:266-275). On a 1-in-5 tick it picks the nearest living thing in a 32-block box - anything but its own kind, the Cryolophosaurus, the Velocity Raptor and a creative player (:141-175, :240-264) - chases it at 1.25 and, within 4.5 blocks plus half the target's width, rolls its bite on a 1-in-5 cadence (:89-90; DinosaurMeleeAttackGoal.Presets.nastysaurus; BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:159-174), landing 32 with a 1.2 knockback (:311-326). The synched DATA_ATTACKING int (:41-42) is held at 1 while it stands in reach and dropped when it chases or stands down (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:132, :145, :168, :176). It spawns in the dark at night above y 50 under five blocks of clear air, no other within 16 (:358-368; ModEntities.java:117-118). Read it as a big theropod: a heavy two-legged stride with the feet lifting and sliding, the tail counter-swinging, the jaws working.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm with no threshold (ModelNastysaurus.java:461-529): each lower leg swings 27 degrees x the walking speed from its foot at 0.325 rad/tick (a 19-tick period), the thigh and shin 11 degrees, the whole foot of seven claw pieces sliding 10 px fore-aft and lifting 4 px on the up-swing; the thigh and shin are re-placed 17 px up the lower leg each tick; the right leg runs half a cycle behind the left.
- Two branches on the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (Nastysaurus.java:41-42), which the melee goal holds at 1 while the dinosaur stands within 4.5 blocks plus half the target's width and drops when it chases or the goal stops (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:132, :145, :168, :176): attacking, the lower jaw and its ten teeth chomp 29 degrees about a half-open rest at 0.5525 rad/tick (:431-433, :452-459) and the last two tail pieces lash 22 and 45 degrees at 0.494 rad/tick (:540-543, :548-551); calm, the jaw sits shut at 11 degrees and the tail sways 7 and 14 degrees at 0.169 rad/tick (:544-546).
- At rest the jaw has a random twitch: a per-entity latch (:404, :437-450; Nastysaurus.java:46-55) rerolls one chance in twenty on every wrap of a 13.8-tick phase clock and, when it hits, runs one sine cycle of the attacking chomp - a client-side roll, not a synched state.
- The head turns with the look at 0.35 of the head yaw: the middle neck takes half of it, the top neck, both skull pieces, the lower jaw and all twenty-three teeth the full angle (:406-429); nothing pitches. The chest, belly, neck base, the first two tail pieces and the three back spikes are static.
- The bite is doHurtTarget (Nastysaurus.java:311-326: 32 of damage and a 1.2 knockback) from the goal's swing roll (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:170-173, nextInt(4) == 0 or nextInt(5) == 1 on a 1-in-5 cadence tick in reach) - no animation event today.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: a heavy two-legged stride: each lower leg swings from its foot, the foot sliding forward and back and lifting on the up-swing, the thigh and shin following at a smaller angle; the right leg in counter-phase — `c = cos(t * 0.65 / 2) (right: + pi), s = sin(...), both 0 when amount <= 0.001; lclaw*.y = 21 - (s > 0 ? s * 4 * amount : 0); lclaw*.z = 15 + 10 * c * amount; leftleg3.y/z = lclaw1.y/z; leftleg3.xRot = -0.523 + c * pi * 0.15 * amount; leftleg1.xRot = -0.576 + c * pi * 0.06 * amount; leftleg2.xRot = 0.977 + c * pi * 0.06 * amount; leftleg1/2.y = leftleg3.y - cos(leftleg3.xRot) * 17; leftleg1/2.z = leftleg3.z - sin(leftleg3.xRot) * 17; the right leg likewise; every claw xRot = 0` (ModelNastysaurus.java:461-529; wingspeed 0.65 (:25), pscale 2 (:394), clawZ 15 / clawY 21 / clawZamp 10 / clawYamp 4 (:397-400))
- **jaw**: the lower jaw and its ten teeth chomp wide and fast while the attacking flag is up; shut at rest, with a random single chomp now and then — `a = attacking ? 0.5 + cos(t * 0.85 * 0.65) * pi * 0.16 : (latch ? 0.5 + sin(t * 0.85 * 0.65) * pi * 0.16 : pi / 16); jaw1.xRot = jaw5.xRot = tooth14..23.xRot = a; the latch: phase = |t * 0.7 * 0.65 mod 2pi|; on each wrap (phase < last) ri1 = nextInt(20) == 1` (ModelNastysaurus.java:431-459; the RenderInfo latch (:404; Nastysaurus.java:46-55, :346-349))
- **tail**: the last two tail pieces sway side to side, the tip twice as far as the piece before it: slow and small at rest, fast and wide while attacking — `ts, amp = attacking ? (0.76, 0.25) : (0.26, 0.08); tail3.yRot = cos(t * ts * 0.65) * pi * amp / 2; tail4.z = tail3.z + cos(tail3.yRot) * 11; tail4.x = tail3.x + sin(tail3.yRot) * 11; tail4.yRot = cos(t * ts * 0.65) * pi * amp` (ModelNastysaurus.java:540-551)
