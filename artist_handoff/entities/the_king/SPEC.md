## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the remainder slice (2026-09-15; hitbox profiles excluded - the classic single hitbox stays until the profile phase). The King is the three-headed flying boss the Queen answers to, on the same family of rig as hers: a body with a chest, back plates and six ridge spines, three four-segment necks each ending in a maned, spiked, jawed and toothed head, two clawed legs of thigh, upper leg, lower leg and foot with three double claws and a rear claw each, two enormous ten-panel wings, and a seven-segment tail ending in two tip pieces and a spike (ModelTheKing.createBodyLayer, ModelTheKing.java:309-940). It has 7000 HP, 350 attack and 21 armour (MobStats.THE_KING, createAttributes TheKing.java:232-240), a red boss bar (:118-119), no physics and no gravity (:155-157) — it flies from spawn — and five manual hitbox parts that ride the root: body 5x5, head 3x3, wingL and wingR 5x2, tail 3x3 (:167-172, :463-467; the head takes full damage, the body half, the rest a quarter plus one, :411-419). Its primary goal (KingPrimaryGoal, aiStepPrimary :735-905) wanders it around its home and, on a 1-in-5 pass (1-in-3 when hurt or enraged), takes its revenge target or the nearest suitable thing in an 80x64x80 scan, raises the attacking flag and flies to just above it (:776-830); within 30 blocks it stomps — a 15-block damage ring on a 1-in-2 roll and the bite (:843-848) — and rings the ground 20 blocks ahead on a 1-in-3 (:853-857); beyond 30, by a 1-in-3 choice, it fires a fireball, a lightning bolt or an ice ball when its head is aimed, ten, five and eight to a stream (:863-881); enraged (the end game's phase 2) it spews purple power (:888-902). Its damage doubles with each quarter of health lost (:496-507), it heals back to 2000 while it has taken fewer than ten player hits (:603-611), takes clamped damage (:972-999), and calls up its invisible gaze-tracking head sidecar (:808-815). The end game freezes the nearest player for a dialogue and a countdown before the enraged phase (aiStepEndGameDialogue :638-693). Its wings sound every 30 ticks (:484-490). It has no natural spawn (it is summoned, or grown from the Prince Adult after 288000 tame ticks). Read it as the Queen's mate: a vast slow wing-beat, three heads snaking on their own rhythms, the tail sweeping, and a fury of claws, legs and jaws when it attacks.
