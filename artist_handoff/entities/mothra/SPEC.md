## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


Mothra is the boss moth: the shared butterfly rig — a body bar, a head cube and four flat wing panels a side (ButterflyModel.createBodyLayer, ButterflyModel.java:54-102) — drawn ten times over in the eyemoth skin (MothraRenderer.java:19-20, :24), with a boss bar (Mothra.java:76-77) and four manual hitbox parts that ride the root each tick: body 4x3, wingL and wingR 5x1.5, head 2x2 (Mothra.java:96-100, :211-214; hits on the head count full, the body half, the wings a quarter plus one, :183-192). She is a butterfly subclass (Mothra.java:46) that flies by her own AI: a flight target she wanders between, re-picked when stuck, on a 1-in-300 roll or on arrival (customServerAiStep :393-413), and a 1-in-10 tick in which she sights the nearest survival player in a 25/20/25 box, flies to a point four blocks above them and, on a 1-in-3 roll (1-in-2 on Hard), shoots a fireball (:414-434) — a SmallFireball or a BetterFireball from a muzzle 2.25 blocks ahead (attackWithFireball :326-363), healing 1 HP per shot; with no player she hunts other mobs the same way (:427-433). Her wings sound every 30 ticks (:228-234), she heals 1 HP every 200 ticks (:235-239), being hurt turns her flight target on the attacker (:243-251), and dying bursts 20 explosion particles and releases 20 luna moths (:455-477). She spawns above y 70 under open sky (:513-514) in badlands, the Nether and the Chaos biome (the boss_mothra and nether biome modifiers, chaos_biome.json), gated to a nearby spawner block while the config says so (:499-511), never within 64 blocks of another Mothra (:495-497). Read her as a slow, vast moth: the same flutter as the butterfly at a fifth of the rate, and the hitboxes follow the wings.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One rhythm runs at all times: the eight wing panels roll about the body's long axis at 0.26 rad/tick (a 24.17-tick beat — the butterfly's 1.3 scaled by this renderer's wingspeed 0.2, MothraRenderer.java:30), 45 degrees each way, the left set mirroring the right (ButterflyModel.java:109-115). The body and the head are static; the walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part — she flaps the same whether wandering, strafing a player or shooting.
- The flight is the entity's own: customServerAiStep blends the delta movement toward the flight target at 0.5 horizontal and 0.7 vertical (Mothra.java:436-443) and turns the yaw a quarter of the way toward the heading each tick (:445-448); tick :225-226 damps the vertical speed by 0.6. The model never banks or pitches the body with that motion.
- The fireball (attackWithFireball, Mothra.java:326-363) and the death burst (die, :455-477) are timed by the AI, not by animation events; the four manual part hitboxes are placed from the root position each tick (:203-214), not from the pose.
- The renderer draws the head, then the body, then the wing pairs inner to outer (renderToBuffer, ButterflyModel.java:119-130).
