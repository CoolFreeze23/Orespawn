## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A small, vicious squid: one body block and eight tentacles fanned around it, each tentacle its own bone at its own rest angle (ModelAttackSquid.java createBodyLayer; 9 parts on a 64x32 sheet). It is a water-creature monster - 10 HP, 8 damage, no armour (MobStats.ATTACK_SQUID, AttackSquid.java:94-101) - that walks on the ground path and floats at the surface (:87), hunts players, girlfriends and boyfriends, zombies, villagers, spiders and lizards within 10 blocks (:228-267), raises the synched attacking flag and bites within 3 blocks (:193-201, doHurtTarget) and, farther out, swims at the prey while spitting from its water cannon: one throw in five, an ink sack (1 in 3) or a water ball from a muzzle 1.2 blocks ahead (:276-297). Out of water it hunts for water and dries out (:170-191); its own kind, water balls and water dragons cannot hurt it (:136-146); a Squid Zooka shot lives 250 ticks and attacks anything (:117-119, :159-168, :266). It spawns above y 50 by day (:372-376). Read it as a squid on legs: the body swaying, eight tentacles each waving at its own rate - lazily at rest, wildly on the move.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Ten cosines, all of them always running, in two branches on the walking speed (ModelAttackSquid.java:100-122): above 0.1 every amplitude is scaled by the walking speed - pi x 0.4 (72 degrees) on the tentacles, pi x 0.04 (7 degrees) on the body; at or below 0.1 the amplitudes are fixed - pi x 0.1 (18 degrees) and pi x 0.01 (2 degrees). The rates never change: the body pitches at 0.25 and rolls at 0.39 (wingspeed 1.0, :21), the eight tentacles wave at 1.2, 1.1, 1.0, 1.9, 1.8, 1.7, 1.6 and 1.5 rad/tick (periods 3.3 to 6.3 ticks).
- Each tentacle is one bone at its own rest angle (:123-130): tentacles 1, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8 pitch (about x), tentacles 2 and 7 roll (about z); the rests are -1.03, -0.26, -1.03, +0.63, +0.6, -0.48, +0.37 and +0.43 rad. Because the eight rates are all different the tentacles drift in and out of step continuously - a squirm, never a stroke.
- The body turns three quarters of the look yaw (:133), so the LookAtPlayerGoal (AttackSquid.java:89) swings the whole mantle; there is no head pitch.
- The classic model never reads the attacking flag: the synched DATA_ATTACKING (AttackSquid.java:52-53) is raised within 3 blocks of prey (:196-197) and cleared when none is found (:210) - a held state - but the pose is the same either way; the bite (doHurtTarget :200) and the water cannon (:276-297) have no pose of their own.
- Below the threshold the walking branch's amplitude would be 7 degrees at a speed of 0.1 against the standing branch's 18 - the code steps DOWN as the squid starts to move, then climbs to 72 degrees at full stride; the seam's speed scaling will smooth this.

### 4.2 The math

- **tentacles**: the eight tentacles each wave about their own rest at their own rate - eight rates from 1.0 to 1.9 rad/tick - lazily when standing, three times as wide and speed-scaled on the move — `kN = walking ? pi * 0.4 * limbSwingAmount : pi * 0.1; tent1.xRot = cos(t * 1.2) * k - 1.03; tent7.zRot = cos(t * 1.1) * k + 0.37; tent5.xRot = cos(t * 1.0) * k + 0.6; tent6.xRot = cos(t * 1.9) * k - 0.48; tent4.xRot = cos(t * 1.8) * k + 0.63; tent2.zRot = cos(t * 1.7) * k - 0.26; tent3.xRot = cos(t * 1.6) * k - 1.03; tent8.xRot = cos(t * 1.5) * k + 0.43 (wingspeed 1.0)` (ModelAttackSquid.java:100-130)
- **body_pitch**: the body nods slowly, a little more on the move — `body.xRot = cos(t * 0.25 * 1.0) * (walking ? pi * 0.04 * limbSwingAmount : pi * 0.01)` (ModelAttackSquid.java:101, 112, 131)
- **body_roll**: the body rolls slowly at a second rate, a little more on the move — `body.zRot = cos(t * 0.39 * 1.0) * (walking ? pi * 0.04 * limbSwingAmount : pi * 0.01)` (ModelAttackSquid.java:102, 113, 132)
- `ModelAttackSquid.setupAnim` (ModelAttackSquid.java:88-134; orig ModelAttackSquid.java:101-133 in `render` (func_78088_a, :85-143; func_78087_a at :151-153 is a bare super call), wingspeed 1.0 from `new ModelAttackSquid(1.0f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:437): ten cosines of the age in ticks times a rate times the wingspeed - 0.25 and 0.39 for the body, 1.2, 1.1, 1.0, 1.9, 1.8, 1.7, 1.6 and 1.5 for tentacles 1, 7, 5, 6, 4, 2, 3 and 8 - times pi, times an amplitude that is 0.04 (the body) and 0.4 (the tentacles) times the walking speed while the walking speed is above a tenth, else 0.01 and 0.1; tentacle 1 pitches by its cosine minus 1.03, tentacle 7 rolls by its plus 0.37, tentacle 5 pitches by its plus 0.6, tentacle 6 pitches by its minus 0.48, tentacle 4 pitches by its plus 0.63, tentacle 2 rolls by its minus 0.26, tentacle 3 pitches by its minus 1.03 and tentacle 8 pitches by its plus 0.43; the body pitches by the 0.25 cosine, rolls by the 0.39 cosine and yaws by the look yaw in radians times 0.75. The walk position, the head pitch and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (AttackSquidGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
