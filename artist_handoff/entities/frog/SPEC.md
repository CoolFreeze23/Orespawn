## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A small passive frog: an 8x11x2 body slab pitched forward 42 degrees, an 8x8 jaw plate hinged beneath it, two stubby front legs, two long jointed hind legs (a 9-long thigh and a 10-long shin each) and two eye nubs (ModelFrog.java:52-100, ten parts). 8 HP, speed 0.1, no fall damage and silent feet (Frog.java:70-75, :210-217). It hops: a random leap about every 70 ticks after a 50-tick cooldown (:96-108, :124-127) and one whenever it is hit (:229-236); it floats and is pushed by water (:65, :92-94); it hunts ants, butterflies, crickets, mosquitoes, fireflies and small worms within 8 blocks, biting for 3 and healing 1 on a kill (:220-226, :239-276); it croaks with a synched singing state of 35 ticks on half of its ambient rolls (:49-50, :183-192). A sneaking, empty-handed player pops it into a Boyfriend frog-prince or a Girlfriend princess in a puff of smoke (:132-179). It spawns in daylight above y 50, at most five together (:284-292; ModEntities.java:381-382). Read it as a frog: a squat, a leap with the hind legs kicked straight, a croak.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (ModelFrog.java:107-113): the front legs paddle 99 degrees x the walking speed about the vertical at 1.4 rad/tick (a 4.5-tick period), the right the mirror of the left, and the shins swing half as far the other way; at speed 0.1 (Frog.java:53) the stride is a slow paddle.
- Two state branches. The jaw plate croaks 27 degrees about its 70-degree rest at 0.85 rad/tick while the synched DATA_SINGING count runs (ModelFrog.java:115-118; Frog.java:49-50, set to 35 at :188-189 on half the ambient rolls, counted down at :115-120) and hangs still otherwise. The thighs roll from a 13-degree tuck to a 140-degree kick whenever the vertical velocity passes 0.1 either way (:120-126) — the hop, which the entity fires at random about every 70 ticks (Frog.java:124-127, jumpAround :96-108) and on every hit (:231-234); the shins are re-placed at the thigh ends each tick (:128-131).
- The body, the jaw's hinge and the two eyes are static; the head does not follow the look (there is no head bone). The bite on an insect (doHurtTarget, Frog.java:220-226, from the hunt at :243-252) and the prince transformation (:132-179, the frog is discarded at :135) draw no pose.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the front legs paddle wide about the vertical with the stride, left against right, and the shins swing half as far the other way — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.0 * 1.4) * pi * 0.55 * amount : 0; lfleg.yRot = a; rfleg.yRot = -a; lleg2.yRot = -a / 2; rleg2.yRot = a / 2` (ModelFrog.java:107-113; wingspeed 1.0 (:23))
- **jaw**: the jaw plate croaks open and shut while the singing state runs, and hangs at its 70-degree rest otherwise — `jaw.xRot = 1.22 + (singing != 0 ? cos(t * 0.85 * 1.0) * pi * 0.15 : 0)` (ModelFrog.java:115-118)
- **thighs**: the hind legs tuck when standing and kick straight out while the frog is rising or falling, the shins riding the thigh ends — `z = |dy| > 0.1 ? 2.44 : 0.227; lleg1.zRot = z; rleg1.zRot = -z; lleg2.y = lleg1.y - cos(z) * 9; lleg2.z = lleg1.z + sin(z) * 9; rleg2 likewise` (ModelFrog.java:120-131)
