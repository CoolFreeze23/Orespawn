## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A mid-range gun platform: a stacked torso on hips and waist rings, two legs, two arms held bent, and a laser turret that swings with its gaze. It tracks targets to about sixteen blocks and fires laser balls on a 35-tick reload; it closes distance but never crowds the melee robots. When it fights, the arms pump; otherwise they hold a bent ready pose.

### 4.2 The math

- **legs**: a stiff stride, left leg against right, both segments of a leg together — `walk = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t*0.55)*pi*0.12*amount : 0; lleg*.xRot = walk; rleg*.xRot = -walk` (ModelRobot3.java:159-190; Robot3GeoReplacement.java:39-49)
- **arms**: the arms pump while attacking (the swing is switched on at a cosine zero-crossing when the attacking flag is set, off otherwise) around a permanently bent pose — `swing = attacking ? cos(t*1.0)*pi*0.15 : 0; rarm1/larm1.xRot = swing - 1.0; rarm2/3, larm2/3.xRot = swing + 1.0` (ModelRobot3.java:159-190; Robot3GeoReplacement.java:51-66)
- **turret**: the laser turret turns with the gaze at half rate — `lazer.yRot = rad(netHeadYaw / 2)` (Robot3GeoReplacement.java:50)
- `ModelRobot3.poseFrom` (ModelRobot3.java:165-199, called from `setupAnim` :159-163; orig ModelRobot3.java:163-186, `render`): (1) the legs — while the walking speed is above a tenth, the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.55, times pi, times 0.12, times the walking speed (up to 21.6 degrees; orig :163-167), the left leg's two parts positive and the right leg's the negative; (2) the laser turret yaws by half the head yaw in radians; (3) the latch — the arm swing is the cosine of the age times pi times 0.15 (up to 27 degrees), and at every upward zero crossing (the swing 0.3 ticks ahead positive while the swing now is negative) the per-entity `ri1` latches to 1 while attacking, else to 0; while `ri1` is 0 the swing is 0 (orig :169-180); (4) the arms — the upper arms pitch by the swing minus 1.0 radian, the lower arms and hands by the swing plus 1.0 radian, the bent ready pose (orig :181-186). At rest the arms hold the bent pose and only the legs move. The GeckoLib hook is the same (Robot3GeoReplacement.java:39-71).
