## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A player-shaped companion: the vanilla biped - a head with a hat layer, a body, two arms and two legs (ModelBoyfriend.java:15-42, the HumanoidModel layout on a 64x32 sheet) - drawn with one of 28 numbered skins, 18 swimshorts skins for the 500 ticks after a swim, or a frog-prince skin once kissed (BoyfriendRenderer.java:42-59; Boyfriend.java:92-93, :102-103, :305-311), and wearing whatever armour he is given (BoyfriendRenderer.java:30-33). He is a tameable fighter: 80 HP, speed 0.3, 8 damage plus his held weapon (:224-229), fire-immune (ModEntities.java:477); cooked beef or cooked peacock tames him one time in three (:454-477), a dead bush breaks it off (:479-489), leather or a peacock feather cycles his outfit (:515-529), a ruby silences him and an amethyst restores his voice (:491-513). Tamed, he follows his owner (:132), hunts creepers and monsters within 20 and 15 blocks (:172-192), is jealous of other boyfriends (:196-197) and, with a weapon in hand, swings it every 25 ticks within 4 blocks - 10 with Big Bertha (:349-379, doHurtTarget :368) - or, at range, shoots an Ultimate Arrow from the Ultimate Bow or throws his shoes every 20 ticks within 10 blocks (:137, :415-448). He floats (:138), panics (:139), opens doors (:143), shelters indoors (:146), heals 1 HP per 150 ticks (:312-318), takes at most 10 per hit and ignores cactus (:668-673). Read him as a player character: the Steve walk, a weapon swing, a bow draw, the arms swaying at rest.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The port's ModelBoyfriend adds no motion of its own: it is the vanilla HumanoidModel (ModelBoyfriend.java:9, no setupAnim override), so the biped's standard rules pose it - the head turns and pitches with the look, the hat copies the head; the legs stride at 1.4 rad x the walking speed and the arms swing against them at 1.0 rad x the walking speed, both phased by the distance walked (limbSwing x 0.6662) rather than by time; at rest the arms sway 3 degrees outward (cos at 0.09/tick) and fore-and-aft (sin at 0.067/tick) in opposition.
- The vanilla swing plays on the main arm whenever the entity swings: the held-weapon melee calls swing(MAIN_HAND) before every hit (Boyfriend.java:367) and the ranged attack swings after each shot or throw (:447); the vanilla arm poses for a held item and a drawn bow apply as for a player.
- The armour layer draws the vanilla armour models over the same seven bones (BoyfriendRenderer.java:30-33), so every bone keyed here moves his armour with him.
- The skin, not the pose, carries his states: numbered dry skins, the swimshorts set for 500 ticks after water (:305-311), the frog-prince skins (BoyfriendRenderer.java:44-53); the synched values (DATA_SKIN, DATA_WET_SKIN, DATA_IS_PRINCE, DATA_VOICE, Boyfriend.java:79-88) are skin and voice, not an attacking flag.
- There is no rig to package today: ModelBoyfriend (the vanilla biped) has no entry in tools/reference_model_proofs.json (TEST-010), so no reference-leg geo exists and the generator lists the species as unpackaged; the bone names above are the createBodyLayer names (ModelBoyfriend.java:19-39) and will be the rig's when it is covered.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the vanilla biped walk: the legs stride in opposition and the arms swing against them, the stride phased by the distance walked rather than by time — `right_leg.xRot = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662) * 1.4 * limbSwingAmount; left_leg.xRot = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662 + pi) * 1.4 * limbSwingAmount; right_arm.xRot = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662 + pi) * 2.0 * limbSwingAmount * 0.5; left_arm.xRot = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662) * 2.0 * limbSwingAmount * 0.5` (vanilla HumanoidModel.setupAnim (net.minecraft.client.model.HumanoidModel; ModelBoyfriend.java:9 extends it with no override))
- **arm_sway_roll**: at all times the arms sway gently outward and back about the shoulder, in opposition — `right_arm.zRot += cos(ageInTicks * 0.09) * 0.05 + 0.05; left_arm.zRot -= cos(ageInTicks * 0.09) * 0.05 + 0.05` (vanilla HumanoidModel.setupAnim (ModelBoyfriend.java:9 extends it with no override))
- **arm_sway_pitch**: a second, slower sway of the arms fore and aft, in opposition — `right_arm.xRot += sin(ageInTicks * 0.067) * 0.05; left_arm.xRot -= sin(ageInTicks * 0.067) * 0.05` (vanilla HumanoidModel.setupAnim (ModelBoyfriend.java:9 extends it with no override))
