### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the legs trot with the walk: front-left and back-right forward while the other two swing back, the swing growing with the walking speed — `swing = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662) * 1.2 * amount; leg_front_left/leg_back_right.xRot = swing; leg_front_right/leg_back_left.xRot = -swing` (CannonFodderModel.setupAnim; CannonFodderGeoReplacement.java)
- **head**: the head follows the gaze — `head.yRot = rad(netHeadYaw); head.xRot = rad(headPitch)` (CannonFodderModel.setupAnim; CannonFodderGeoReplacement.java)
- `CannonFodderModel.setupAnim` (CannonFodderModel.java; a port-authored model - 1.7.10 registered no renderer for EntityCannonFodder, so there is no orig line): (1) the head yaws by the head yaw in radians and pitches by the head pitch in radians; (2) the leg swing is the cosine of the walk DISTANCE times 0.6662, times 1.2, times the walking speed (up to 69 degrees each way, one stride every 9.43 units of walk distance - the vanilla quadruped gait): the front-left and back-right legs pitch by it, the front-right and back-left by its negative. The age in ticks and the entity's state play no part; standing still, nothing moves. The GeckoLib hook is the same (CannonFodderGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
