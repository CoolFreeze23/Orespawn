## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A small hostile rat: a 5x3x10 body with a spine ridge, a 3x2x4 head with a nose and two nub ears, two stub front legs, two haunches and a two-piece tail 21 long (RatModel.java:55-77, twelve parts), drawn at x0.75. 5 HP, 3 damage, 1 armour, speed 0.25 (EntityRat.java:77-84). On a 1-in-5 tick it picks the nearest living thing in a 9-block box - not the water creatures, other rats, the dungeon beast or a creative player (:157-165, :200-233) - runs at it at 1.25 with its attacking flag up and, within 2 blocks, bites on a double dice (:160-165); a rat with an owner (the summons of the Rat Sword, setOwner :114-118) spares the owner, their pets and, by config, all players (:212-222), follows the owner beyond 8 blocks at 1.75 and teleports to them beyond 16 (:168-181); wild rats despawn, owned rats stay (:100-104). It spawns in the dark, in the Crystal dimension only below y 50 with air around its head, at most eight together (:261-278; ModEntities.java:277-279). Read it as a rat: a scurry on four stubs, the tail whipping when it hunts.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (RatModel.java:86-90): the four stubs scurry 45 degrees x the walking speed in diagonal pairs at 1.7 rad/tick, a 3.7-tick period - a fast patter at the 0.25 speed (EntityRat.java:81).
- One branch on the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (EntityRat.java:47-48), raised on the 1-in-5 pass whenever prey is found in the 9-block box - at any distance, through the whole chase (:157-160) - and dropped when the pass finds none (:167): the two-piece tail sways 4.5 and 11 degrees at 0.4 rad/tick at rest and thrashes 22 and 56 degrees at 1.5 rad/tick while the flag is up, the second piece placed 9 px along the first (RatModel.java:95-101).
- The body, spine ridge, head, nose and ears are static; the head does not follow the look. The bite (doHurtTarget within 2 blocks when nextInt(8) == 0 or nextInt(7) == 1, EntityRat.java:162-165) and the owned rat's teleport to its owner (:174-179) draw no pose.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four stubs scurry fore and aft in diagonal pairs with the stride — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.7 * 1.0) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; rfleg.xRot = lrleg.xRot = a; lfleg.xRot = rrleg.xRot = -a` (RatModel.java:86-90; wingspeed 1.0 (:83-84))
- **tail**: the tail sways side to side, the tip more than the root: a gentle sway at rest, a fast wide thrash while the attacking flag is up — `a = attacking ? cos(t * 1.5) * pi * 0.25 : cos(t * 0.4) * pi * 0.05; tail1.yRot = a * 0.5; tail2.yRot = a * 1.25; tail2.z = tail1.z + cos(tail1.yRot) * 9; tail2.x = tail1.x + sin(tail1.yRot) * 9` (RatModel.java:95-101)
