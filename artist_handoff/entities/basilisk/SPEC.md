## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Basilisk is a giant hostile serpent: a tall head with a snout, a hinged lower jaw and six brow spikes, two neck blocks, six body blocks and a four-piece tapering tail, laid out nose to tip (ModelBasilisk.createBodyLayer, ModelBasilisk.java:71-180). It has 200 HP, 24 attack and 15 armour (MobStats.BASILISK, createAttributes Basilisk.java:92-101), walks through villages and wanders (:74-85), and fights on a 1-in-5 tick re-scan (customServerAiStep :201-237): the scanned target is slowed (Slowness V for 100 ticks — the 'gaze', :227) whether or not it is in reach, and within six blocks plus half its width the attacking flag rises and a bite lands on nested dice (:214-220) — one bite in three poisons for 8 to 14 seconds by difficulty and every bite knocks back (doHurtTarget :157-185). Hits are refused for 30 ticks after each one (hurt :188-194) and it heals 1 on a 1-in-75 roll (:234-236). It spawns at night in the dark with clear air above and no other Basilisk within 20 blocks (checkSpawnRules :284-291) in dark forests, jungles and old-growth birch (the hostile_basilisk biome modifiers); a baby is drawn at half size (BasiliskRenderer.java:41-42). Read it as a serpent: the whole body is one travelling wave, and the head never turns.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One speed-scaled travelling wave at 0.39 rad/tick (a 16.1-tick period) runs from the first body block to the tail tip: each of the ten segments yaws 18 degrees at top speed, an eighth of a period behind the one before, and is placed at the end of the previous segment (ModelBasilisk.java:186-213). Standing still the body is straight.
- The lower jaw chomps 32 degrees at 0.45 rad/tick about −1.0 while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (Basilisk.java:46, raised at :216 within six blocks plus half the target's width) and hangs shut at −1.1 otherwise (ModelBasilisk.java:214).
- The head, snout, brow spikes and the two neck blocks are static: the code never turns the head with the look, and the walk position plays no part.
- The bite (doHurtTarget, Basilisk.java:157-185) and the Slowness 'gaze' (:227) are rolled by the 1-in-5 scan — nothing in the code fires an animation for them.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the six body blocks and four tail segments yaw in a travelling wave with the walking speed, each an eighth of a turn behind the last, so the body snakes; still when standing — `seg_k.yRot = cos(t * 1.3 * 0.3 - k * pi/4) * pi * 0.1 * amount (k = 0..9: body1..body6, tail1..tail4); seg_(k+1).x/z = seg_k.x/z + cos/sin(seg_k.yRot) x length_k` (ModelBasilisk.java:186-213; wingspeed 0.3 (:24))
- **jaw**: the lower jaw chomps while the attacking flag is set and hangs still otherwise — `jaw.xRot = attacking ? -1.0 + cos(t * 0.45) * pi * 0.18 : -1.1` (ModelBasilisk.java:214)
