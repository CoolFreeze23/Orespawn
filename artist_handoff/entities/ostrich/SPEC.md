## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A tameable, rideable ostrich: a two-block body with a rump and an under-tail block, a 21-tall neck to a skull with an upper and a lower beak, three zero-thickness tail plumes, two stub wings, and two legs of a hip, a thigh, a shin and a four-plate foot with four zero-thickness claws (OstrichModel.java:107-295, 38 parts; the two hat pieces stay hidden, :412-413). 25 HP, speed 0.38 and an unused attack of 6 - it has no attack goal (Ostrich.java:125-144). It flees monsters within 8 blocks, panics when hurt, is tempted by wheat and tamed by it one time in two (:130-132, :160-174); an empty hand mounts it, tame or wild (:176-182, :204-215), and the rider drives a client-predicted sprint with a throttle to 0.75, a 1.85 yaw lag and a 'FAST' hop on the jump key (:49-71, :237-243); it takes no fall damage (:289-292) and shrugs off cactus (:146-154). It spawns in daylight above y 50 on a 1-in-4 roll, no other within 16 (:302-310; ModEntities.java:555-556). Read it as an ostrich: long strides that snap to a kick at speed, the plumes waving, an occasional wing flap, the head leaning into a ridden turn.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm with no threshold (OstrichModel.java:313-343): the two legs stride as stiff chains at 0.8125 rad/tick (a 7.7-tick period) with an amplitude of pi x 0.4 x the entity's TRUE horizontal speed - read from its last position, not limbSwingAmount - and snapped to ±0.75 rad whenever it passes ±0.5, so a running ostrich kicks to a fixed 43 degrees; every piece of a leg, feet and claws included, takes the same angle, the right leg the negative of the left.
- Three unscaled rhythms: the three tail plumes pitch 11 degrees together on a 126-tick wave while the side plumes fan 14 degrees on 103- and 87-tick waves (:344-347); the wings flap 27 degrees one-sided on a 9.7-tick cycle only when a per-entity latch, rerolled one chance in three at each cycle's zero crossing, is set (:389-406).
- The head branches three ways. Ridden (:355-365), the head yaw becomes the entity's own turn rate x 20, smoothed at a sixtieth per tick and clamped to ±50 - a lean into the turn; otherwise the look yaw is halved (:366-368). In the sitting pose (:371-377) the skull, both beaks, the neck and the hats flip upside down (xRot pi) with no yaw - though no code path in the port sets the ostrich sitting (mobInteract :157-185 tames and mounts only; mounting clears it :179), so the branch is dormant; else they sit level (:378-384). The skull, beaks and hats then yaw at 0.65 of that head yaw (:385-388); the neck's yaw is not written.
- The body blocks, rump and under-tail are static. The ridden run (Ostrich.java:237-243, the RiderFlightController), its FAST hop (:49-58) and the taming are not animation events.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the two legs stride fore and aft as stiff chains, opposite each other, the swing growing with the real speed and snapping to a fixed kick when it runs — `s = sqrt((xOld - x)^2 + (zOld - z)^2); a = cos(t * 1.25 * 0.65) * pi * s * 0.4; a = a > 0.5 ? 0.75 : a < -0.5 ? -0.75 : a; leftleg.xRot = -0.297 + a; LLeg1.xRot = 0.483 + a; LLeg2.xRot = -0.437 + a; Lfoot1..4.xRot = LClaw1..4.xRot = a; the right leg: rest - a` (OstrichModel.java:313-343; wingspeed 0.65 (:22))
- **tail**: the three tail plumes rise and fall together on a slow wave while the two side plumes fan in and out on their own slower rhythms — `Tail1.xRot = Tail2.xRot = Tail3.xRot = -0.594 + cos(t * 0.05) * pi * 0.06; Tail3.yRot = -0.334 + cos(t * 0.061) * pi * 0.08; Tail2.yRot = 0.334 - cos(t * 0.072) * pi * 0.08` (OstrichModel.java:344-347)
- **wings**: the wings flap out and back in a one-sided beat, one cycle in three on average, still the rest of the time — `w = cos(t * 1.0 * 0.65) * pi * 0.15; n = cos((t + 0.3) * 1.0 * 0.65) * pi * 0.15; if (n > 0 && w < 0) ri1 = nextInt(3) == 1; if (ri1 == 0) w = 0; w = |w|; Lwing.zRot = -w; Lwing.yRot = w / 2; Rwing.zRot = w; Rwing.yRot = -w / 2` (OstrichModel.java:389-406; the RenderInfo latch (:351; Ostrich.java:75-93))
