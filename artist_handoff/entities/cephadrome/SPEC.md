## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the first Tier-1 slice (T1a, 2026-09-15; hitbox profiles excluded - the classic single hitbox stays until the profile phase). The Cephadrome is a rideable flying hammerhead shark on legs: a hammer-headed head with a hinged mouth on a three-segment neck, a body of several blocks with a tall chest keel, two three-segment legs with feet, two four-spar wing fins with a membrane, a four-spar dorsal fin with four membranes, and a three-segment tail ending in a four-spar tail fin (ModelCephadrome.createBodyLayer, ModelCephadrome.java:129-283). It is a PathfinderMob in the ambient category (Cephadrome.java:41; ModEntities.java:733-734) with 300 HP and 70 attack (createAttributes :137-141), wanders and looks about on the ground (:130-134), and on a 1-in-7 tick hunts monsters, Mothra, the Ender Dragon, untamed Leons, Gamma Metroids and Water Dragons — and players once one has hurt it below nine tenths of its health, or when in a bad mood, or after a refused mount (customServerAiStep :352-386, isSuitableTarget :401-420) — running at 1.7 with the attacking flag up and biting within six blocks plus half the target's width for 70 (doHurtTarget :309-331, x1.5 against the Kraken, a 2.5 knockback). Fed raw meat it heals to full and lets a player mount (:56-60); ridden it flies with no gravity on the client-predicted RiderFlightController (:73-84, :182) and its wings sound every 22 ticks (:164-173) — the model's activity 1 is that ridden flight. Hits are refused for 25 ticks after each one and turn it on the attacker (hurt :334-349); it heals 2 on a 1-in-100 roll (:356-358). It spawns by day above y 50 with clear air above and no other within 16 blocks (checkSpawnRules :544-555; a spawner-born one is born hostile) through the ambient_cephadrome biome modifier. Read it as a shark that walks: a flat-bodied stride on the ground, and in the air a slow wide beat of the wing fins.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- On the ground the two legs stride as stiff pieces at 0.4125 rad/tick (a 15.2-tick period), the swing scaled by the entity's TRUE horizontal speed (from its last position, ModelCephadrome.java:299-300) rather than by limbSwingAmount, and clamped at 43 degrees (:301-307); ridden (DATA_ACTIVITY 1, Cephadrome.java:53) both legs tuck one radian forward and hold (:312-321).
- The wing fins branch three ways (:333-343): in ridden flight they beat 50 degrees each way at 0.3025 rad/tick (a 20.8-tick beat); on the ground they hang folded at −0.85 with a 5-degree stir, and while the attacking flag is set they half-raise to −0.65 and quiver 12 degrees at 0.495 rad/tick. The right fin mirrors the left.
- The dorsal fin folds on a very slow rectified wave (a 76-tick period, folding twice in it), the front spar 9 degrees and each spar behind half the last (:345-353). The tail sweeps in a travelling wave an eighth of a period per segment — 18 degrees at 0.418 rad/tick in flight or when attacking, 5 degrees at 0.121 rad/tick at rest on the ground — and the tail fin's spars and membranes ride its end (:355-389).
- The neck turns toward the look in three steps (an eighth, a quarter, a half) and the head three quarters, the hammer-head lobes and the mouth riding it (:413-432); ridden, the look is replaced by a filtered turn rate clamped to ±50 degrees (:395-405). - The bite (doHurtTarget, Cephadrome.java:309-331) is rolled by the 1-in-7 hunt (:365-385) and the wing sound every 22 ticks while ridden is the entity's (:164-173) — neither is an animation event.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: on the ground the two legs stride with the real speed, each leg a stiff piece; ridden, they tuck forward and hold — `s = amount > 0.001 ? |velocity_xz| : 0; a = clamp(cos(t * 0.75 * 0.55) * pi * s * 0.4, ±0.75 past ±0.5); activity != 0 ? (leg1.xRot = -0.58 + 1, leg2 = 0.98 + 1, leg3 = -0.52 + 1, foot = 1, both sides) : (right: -0.58 + a, 0.98 + a, -0.52 + a, a; left: -0.58 - a, 0.98 - a, -0.52 - a, -a)` (ModelCephadrome.java:298-331; wingspeed 0.55 (:24))
- **wing_fins**: the wing fins beat wide and slow in ridden flight, hang folded with a faint stir on the ground, and half-raise and quiver while attacking — `w = activity != 0 ? cos(t * 0.55 * 0.55) * pi * 0.28 : (attacking == 0 ? -0.85 + cos(t * 0.2 * 0.55) * pi * 0.028 : -0.65 + cos(t * 0.9 * 0.55) * pi * 0.068); left spars and membrane zRot = w; right = -w` (ModelCephadrome.java:333-343)
- **top_fins**: the dorsal fin folds and rises very slowly, the front spar most and the rear least; the wave is rectified, so it folds twice per period — `u = cos(t * 0.15 * 0.55) * pi * 0.05; topfin1.xRot = -1.85 - |u|, topmem1.xRot = -0.26 - |u|; topfin2 = -2.07 - |u/2|, topmem2 = -0.52 - |u/2|; topfin3 = -2.42 - |u/4|, topmem3 = -0.89 - |u/4|; topfin4 = -2.63 - |u/8|, topmem4 = -1.11 - |u/8|` (ModelCephadrome.java:345-353)
- **tail**: the tail sweeps side to side in a travelling wave — brisk in flight or when attacking, a slow sway at rest on the ground — `ts = (activity == 0 && attacking == 0) ? 0.22 : 0.76; amp = (activity == 0 && attacking == 0) ? 0.03 : 0.1; tail1.yRot = cos(t * ts * 0.55) * pi * 0.04; tail2.yRot = cos(t * ts * 0.55 - pi/4) * pi * amp; tail3.yRot = cos(... - pi/2) * pi * amp; tailfin1.yRot = cos(... - 3pi/4) * pi * amp; tailfin2/3/4 and tailmembrane1/2/3 = tailfin1 (positions along the chain by cos/sin x 13 / 13 / 10)` (ModelCephadrome.java:355-389)
- **mouth**: the lower jaw works while the attacking flag is set and hangs still otherwise — `mouth.xRot = attacking ? -0.61 + cos(t * 0.5 * 0.55) * pi * 0.14 : -0.87` (ModelCephadrome.java:434-435)
- `ModelCephadrome.poseFrom` (ModelCephadrome.java:300-450; orig ModelCephadrome.java:387-554 in `render` (func_78088_a, :373-555; func_78087_a at :563-565 is a bare super call), wingspeed 0.55 from `new ModelCephadrome(0.55f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:446 - the port's WINGSPEED): while the walking speed is above a thousandth the gait is the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.75 times the wingspeed, times pi, times the length of the entity's own movement since the last tick, times 0.4, clamped to 0.75 past 0.5 and -0.75 past -0.5 (0 on a probe that does not move), else 0. While the activity is not 0 the eight leg parts take a constant 1.0: the three right leg pieces pitch -0.58, 0.98 and -0.52 plus it and the right foot by it, the left the same; else the right side pitches the same rests plus the gait and the left side minus it.
