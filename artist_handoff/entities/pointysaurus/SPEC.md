## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the fifth Tier-2 slice (T2e, 2026-09-15). Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A hostile three-horned dinosaur: a 22x9x30 body slab with two upper body blocks, four stumpy block legs, a 12x10x12 head with a nose, two 23-long brow horns and a nose horn, a 28x23 neck frill studded with sixteen bumps, and a stub tail (ModelPointysaurus.java:92-240, 30 parts). 80 HP, 10 damage, 16 armour, speed 0.35 (Pointysaurus.java:219-228). It hunts players only: any player whose box meets its own grown 12 x 5 x 12, on a 1-in-6 cadence (:123-152), and - a modern addition behind a config flag - any player who stares at it from within 32 blocks for five ticks (:83-91; PointysaurusStareGoal.java:47-59, :86-96); it chases at 1.25 and, within 4 blocks plus half the target's width, rolls its gore on a 1-in-6 cadence (:62-64; DinosaurMeleeAttackGoal.Presets.pointysaurus; BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:159-174), landing 10 with a 0.8 knockback (:269-284). The synched DATA_ATTACKING int (:44-45) is held at 1 while it stands in reach (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:168) and dropped when it chases or stands down (:132, :145, :176). It spawns in the dark at night above y 50 under five blocks of clear air (:303-312; ModEntities.java:125-126). Read it as a triceratops: a four-legged plod, the head and frill swinging with the look, the tail lashing when it closes in.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (ModelPointysaurus.java:249-253): the four block legs swing 45 degrees x the walking speed in diagonal pairs at 1.3 rad/tick, a 4.8-tick period - a quick plod at the 0.35 walk (Pointysaurus.java:224).
- The head, nose, nose horn, both brow horns, the frill and its sixteen bumps turn together with the look at 0.45 of the head yaw and pitch (:255-296): the brow horns splayed 0.14 rad out and 0.16 rad down off the head, the frill and bumps 0.262 rad back. The body blocks are static.
- The tail has one branch on the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (Pointysaurus.java:44-45; held at 1 while it stands within 4 blocks plus half the target's width, BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:168, dropped at :132 / :145 / :176): a 9-degree wag at 0.3 rad/tick at rest, a 45-degree lash at 1.3 rad/tick while attacking (:298-299); over both a 27-degree lift-and-drop on a 314-tick wave (:300-301).
- The gore is doHurtTarget (Pointysaurus.java:269-284: 10 of damage and a 0.8 knockback) from the goal's swing roll (BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:170-173, nextInt(5) == 0 or nextInt(6) == 1 on a 1-in-6 cadence tick in reach); the stare lock turns the look control onto the starer's eyes for five ticks before taking the target (PointysaurusStareGoal.java:86-96) - neither is an animation event today.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four block legs swing fore and aft in diagonal pairs with the stride — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3 * 1.0) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; lfleg.xRot = rrleg.xRot = a; rfleg.xRot = lrleg.xRot = -a` (ModelPointysaurus.java:249-253; wingspeed 1.0 (:23))
- **tail_sway**: the tail sways side to side: a slow small wag at rest, a fast wide lash while the attacking flag is up — `tail.yRot = attacking ? cos(t * 1.3 * 1.0) * pi * 0.25 : cos(t * 0.3 * 1.0) * pi * 0.05` (ModelPointysaurus.java:298-299)
- **tail_lift**: the tail lifts and drops on a very slow wave at all times — `tail.xRot = 0.28 + cos(t * 0.02 * 1.0) * pi * 0.15` (ModelPointysaurus.java:300-301)
- `ModelPointysaurus.poseFrom` (ModelPointysaurus.java:254-310; orig ModelPointysaurus.java:237-286 in `render` (func_78088_a, :232-317; func_78087_a at :325-327 is a bare super call), wingspeed 1.0 from `new ModelPointysaurus(1.0f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:509 - the port's model field): while the walking speed is above a tenth the leg swing is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed, else 0; the left-front and right-rear legs pitch by it and the right-front and left-rear by its negative. The head, nose, centre horn, guard and sixteen bumps yaw by the look yaw in radians times 0.45, the left horn 0.14 less and the right horn 0.14 more; the head, nose and centre horn pitch by the look pitch in radians times 0.45, both side horns 0.16 less, and the guard and the sixteen bumps 0.262 less. The tail yaws by the cosine of the age times 1.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.25 while the attacking flag is set, else by the cosine of the age times 0.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.05; and pitches by 0.28 plus the cosine of the age times 0.02 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.15. The walk position plays no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (PointysaurusGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
