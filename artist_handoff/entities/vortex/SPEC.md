## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A tornado: one tall textured cube that drifts through the air to random points, fills itself with a column of smoke when it has prey, drags players and mobs toward and up into itself, and hurts what it swallows. The cube itself never animates; the tornado's life is particles and pull.

## 11. What 'done' looks like for this entity
