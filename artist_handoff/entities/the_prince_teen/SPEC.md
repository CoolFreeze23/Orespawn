## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Prince Teen is the royal hatchling grown: a three-headed dragon on the Nightmare's kind of rig — a two-block body with two shoulders and two back fins, three necks of three segments each ending in a two-piece head with a head fin and a two-piece jaw, two three-segment legs on clawed feet, two four-spar wings with four membranes each, and a five-segment tail with three spikes (ModelThePrinceTeen.createBodyLayer, ModelThePrinceTeen.java:171-530). It is a TamableAnimal and a rideable flyer with 1500 HP, 0.32 speed and 50 attack (createAttributes ThePrinceTeen.java:191-199), follows its owner, is tempted by beef, wanders and looks about (:147-153), avenges and defends (:160-163) and hunts monsters (:177-189). Its activity byte is 0 on the ground and 1 in flight (no physics when flying, :232; the wings sound every 20 ticks, :238-248): it takes off on a 1-in-10 tick with prey in sight (:617-626), when its owner flies or is far (:255-259, :660-665), or when hurt (hurt :286-310, a 20-tick guard). Flying (:748-790) it flies to just above its target with the attacking flag up and bites within eight blocks plus half the target's width (doHurtTarget :276) or, within twenty blocks with its fire lit on a 1-in-2, shoots — a big fireball, a thunderbolt or an ice ball from six blocks ahead and 3.5 up when its head is aimed (shootSomethingAt :528-580); a tamed one below a quarter health flees. Ridden, it fires a canon trio on the strafe keys and bites for its rider within eight blocks (:499-518). Its three necks rise and fall on synced head extensions random-walked between 0 and 60 (:261-272). After 25 kills and 10 days it grows into the Prince Adult (:629-632, transformToAdult :874-885); a diamond regresses it to the Prince (revertToBaby :1031-1040); a diamond block tames and heals it, an empty hand mounts it, ice and flint and steel take and give its fire, any other item sits it and grounds it (:926-1026). It has no natural spawn (it grows from the Prince). Read it as an adolescent Nightmare: a bat-winged flyer with three snapping heads.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- On the ground the legs stride 45 degrees at top speed at 0.3575 rad/tick (a 17.6-tick period) above a walking-speed threshold of 0.1, the lower legs a quarter period ahead; attacking, they swing at 0.65 rad/tick whatever the speed; in flight (DATA_ACTIVITY 1, ThePrinceTeen.java:67) and calm they tuck up and the claws curl (ModelThePrinceTeen.java:587-657).
- The wings branch four ways (:551-559): 72 degrees at 0.91 rad/tick in flight, 72 at 1.105 attacking, 36 x the speed at 0.845 moving on the ground, 7 at 0.195 still; the second spar beats 1.25 times the rest and the membranes ride their spars (:561-585). The tail sweeps in a travelling wave along five segments and three spikes, 14 degrees at 0.169 rad/tick at rest and 34 at 0.364 attacking, still when sitting (:659-688).
- The three heads share the look — two thirds of the look yaw and pitch to the heads on the side the look turns to, half of that to the others — and in flight the look is replaced by a filtered turn rate clamped to ±50 degrees (:690-717); each neck takes an eighth, a quarter and a half of its head's yaw and pitches by a third, two thirds and the whole of its synced head extension, which random-walks between 0 and 60 (ThePrinceTeen.java:261-272; ModelThePrinceTeen.java:719-744, :783-849). The jaws chomp 18 degrees on three rates attacking and breathe 3.6 at rest (:746-781).
- The body blocks, shoulders, back fins and neck base are static; the walk position plays no part beyond the speed.
- The bite (doHurtTarget, ThePrinceTeen.java:276), the shot (shootSomethingAt :528-580), the rider's canon trio (:499-518), the growth into the Adult (:874-885) and the regression to the Prince (:1031-1040) are rolled by the AI or the player — nothing in the code fires an animation for them.
