## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A low, creeping insect horror: a body block with five 'insides' pieces at its centre, four two-part legs splayed out to the sides, two two-part pincers at the front, a three-segment tail curling up behind and five spikes on its back (ModelCreepingHorror.java:48-73; 26 parts on a 128x128 sheet). It is hostile but weak: 10 HP, 3 damage, 2 armour, speed 0.25 (MobStats.CREEPING_HORROR, CreepingHorror.java:85-93). Every fifth tick it hunts anything alive and in sight within 16 blocks - its own kind, the mod's scenery and ally mobs and creative players excepted (:145-200) - walks at it at 1.25 and bites within 2.2 blocks on 1-in-12-or-1-in-14 dice (:153-163, doHurtTarget :159); it retaliates when hit (:82), floats (:69), panics (:70), walks through villages (:78) and strolls avoiding water (:79). It spawns in darkness at night in the Chaos dimension or below y 15 (:100-112) and tends to vanish by day (:114-123). Read it as a skittering bug: legs sweeping wide with the stride, pincers working, the tail pumping up and down, the five spikes quivering on fifteen different beats.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (ModelCreepingHorror.java:189-193): the cosine of the age x 1.25, x pi x 0.35, x the walking speed (63 degrees at full speed every 5 ticks, no threshold) is added to the +0.576 rad rests of the left front and right back legs and subtracted from the -0.576 rests of the other two, the lower part of each leg copying the upper - a wide sideways sweep; at a standstill the legs sit on their rests.
- The pincers open and close at all times (:194-197): the cosine of the age x 0.48, x pi x 0.15 - 27 degrees every 13.1 ticks - on the right pincer's two pieces, and its negative on the left.
- The tail pumps (:198-202): the absolute value of the cosine of the age x 0.11 x pi x 0.25 (45 degrees, rectified - a rise and fall every 28.6 ticks) is added to the base's -0.55 rad, the middle's 0 and the tip's -0.22, all three segments turning by the same angle.
- The five spikes quiver on fifteen beats (:203-222): each pitches 14 degrees around 0.7 rad at 0.81, 0.87, 0.99, 0.103 and 0.107, yaws 14 degrees at 1.11 to 1.31 and rolls 14 degrees at 1.41 to 1.61 - spikes 4 and 5 pitch ten times slower than the first three (0.103 and 0.107 read like 1.03 and 1.07 mistyped in the original; kept).
- The body and the five insides pieces never move; nothing reads the look (netHeadYaw unused), so the LookAtPlayerGoal (CreepingHorror.java:80) turns nothing visible. There is no attacking flag: the bite (doHurtTarget :159, within 2.2 blocks on dice) has no pose of its own.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four legs sweep wide fore and aft with the stride, upper and lower parts together: the left front and right back one way, the other two the opposite — `a = cos(ageInTicks * 1.25) * pi * 0.35 * limbSwingAmount; leg1/leg1part2.yRot = 0.576 + a; leg2/leg2part2.yRot = -0.576 - a; leg3/leg3part2.yRot = -0.576 - a; leg4/leg4part2.yRot = 0.576 + a` (ModelCreepingHorror.java:189-193)
- **pincers**: the two pincers open and close together at all times — `p = cos(ageInTicks * 0.48) * pi * 0.15; pincer1/pincer1part2.yRot = p; pincer2/pincer2part2.yRot = -p` (ModelCreepingHorror.java:194-197)
- **tail**: the tail pumps up from its curl and settles, slowly, all three segments together — `n = abs(cos(ageInTicks * 0.11) * pi * 0.25); tailseg1.xRot = -0.55 + n; tailseg2.xRot = n; tailseg3.xRot = -0.22 + n` (ModelCreepingHorror.java:198-202)
- **spikes**: the five back spikes quiver, each on its own three beats, never in step — `spikeN.xRot = 0.7 + cos(t * wx_N) * pi * 0.08 with wx = 0.81 / 0.87 / 0.99 / 0.103 / 0.107; spikeN.yRot = cos(t * wy_N) * pi * 0.08 with wy = 1.11 / 1.17 / 1.25 / 1.28 / 1.31; spikeN.zRot = cos(t * wz_N) * pi * 0.08 with wz = 1.41 / 1.47 / 1.55 / 1.58 / 1.61` (ModelCreepingHorror.java:203-222)
