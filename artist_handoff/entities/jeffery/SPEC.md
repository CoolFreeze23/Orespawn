## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- The rig is twenty-nine bones drawing a two-legged, two-armed robot from eighteen classic parts: the thigh (three pieces), shin and foot (three pieces) are drawn twice per frame, once per leg with that leg's angles and mirrored hip offsets, and so are the three arm pieces and the knuckles (ModelGiantRobot.java:241-262, renderLeg :269-300, renderArm :307-317) — the converter's render_instances form gives each draw its own bone (`<part>__i0` the left side, `<part>__i1` the right; owner 2026-09-14, item 2), the distinct left and right bones the design's row requires, and the FINAL bone set; the hook animates each side's bones with that side's angles when the rig lands.
- The walk is scaled by 0.65 x the walking speed capped at 1 (:187-190): the hip pitches and yaws 18 degrees on a 0.25 rad/tick wave (a 25-tick period) and bobs 4 units at twice that rate (:193-194, :205-208); the thighs swing 27 degrees a quarter turn ahead of the hip and the shins 36 degrees, the two legs half a period apart (:195-202); the whole torso, neck and head ride the bob (:232-235) and the head turns the look yaw and a third of the pitch (:236-237).
- Not attacking, each arm swings with the other leg's thigh angle and the shoulders yaw against the hip's sway; while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (GiantRobot.java:49, raised at :168 within eight blocks plus half the target's width) the arms windmill 36 degrees at 0.5 rad/tick in punches and the shoulders twist 36 degrees with them, the upper back block half as much (:210-229).
- The laser (fireLaserBall, GiantRobot.java:192-216) and the punch (doHurtTarget :120-132) are rolled by customServerAiStep (:139-181), inherited unchanged by Jeffery (Jeffery.java:17) — nothing in the code fires an animation for them.
