## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A lean flying creature: a long thin body with a dorsal fin, two broad flat wings, a long tail with a club at the end, and a small head with a beak. It flies in wide circles and is passive. Read it as a gliding animal with one slow wingbeat.
