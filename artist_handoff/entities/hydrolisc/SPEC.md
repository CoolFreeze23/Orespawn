## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the fifth Tier-2 slice (T2e, 2026-09-15). Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A water-loving four-legged pet lizard: a three-part body with a four-spine ridge down its back, a three-part head crowned by three fanned feathers, four legs of six cubes each sharing one hip pivot, and a three-segment tail (HydroliscModel.java createBodyLayer; 40 parts on a 64x128 sheet). It is passive and tameable with cod - one chance in two per fish (EntityHydrolisc.java:222-232) - then follows its owner (:70), sits and stands on a click (:273-278), breeds (:68), is untamed with a glass block (:247-261) and slowly heals its owner at the cost of its own health (:123-130). It flees monsters (:69), panics when hurt (:72), floats (:67) and bobs up in water (:102-105), seeks the nearest water when hurt or at random and heals in it (:117-121, :133-153); every hit is capped at 10 (:204-207). It never attacks: the attack attribute is 1.0 (:95) and no goal strikes. Read it as a wet, skittish pet: legs paddling with the stride, the tail sweeping, the crest fanning - fast and wide when healthy, slow and small when hurt.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm on the legs (HydroliscModel.java:317-341): above a walking speed of 0.1 the cosine of the age x 1.3 x 0.65 (wingspeed), x pi x 0.25, x the walking speed, is added to every part of the left front and right back legs and subtracted from the right front and left back - a 45-degree swing at full stride every 7.4 ticks, nothing at all when standing (the ternary at :317 gives 0 below the threshold, so the legs snap to their rests).
- Each leg is six cubes on one pivot with fixed rest angles (0, -0.488, -2.347, -0.628, -0.628, +0.174 rad: the shoulder, an upper leg, a folded forearm, two paw cubes and a toe), all swinging as one rigid unit; the three body blocks, the three head blocks and the four back spines never move.
- The tail sweeps side to side (HydroliscModel.java:342-352): the cosine of the age x 1.0 x 0.65, x pi x 0.15, taken at a quarter, a half and three quarters along the three segments, each next segment's position re-derived 5 and 8 units along the previous segment's yaw (an FK chain written by hand on flat bones: when this rig lands, a keyframed sweep must either key the positions too or the tail bones must be parented). While the pet sits (isInSittingPose, :343-345) the sweep is zeroed - the ONLY visible sitting pose the code has; the legs and body do not change.
- The crest reads the pet's health (HydroliscModel.java:353-357): the health fraction multiplies both the rate and the amplitude of the three feathers - the outer pair mirror each other around +-0.9 rad (52 deg) at 0.75 x 0.65 x hf, the centre feather wags at 1.25 x 0.65 x hf, each pi x 0.2 x hf. At full health a 36-degree fan every 12.9 (outer) and 7.7 (centre) ticks; near death almost still.
- The head does not turn: netHeadYaw and headPitch are unused, so the LookAtPlayerGoal and RandomLookAroundGoal (EntityHydrolisc.java:73, 75) move nothing visible.
- In water it bobs upward every tick (EntityHydrolisc.java:102-105) with the FloatGoal at the surface (:67); the renderer halves the scale for a baby (HydroliscRenderer.java:37-41).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four six-part legs swing fore and aft with the stride, every part of a leg together: the left front and right back legs one way, the right front and left back the other — `a = limbSwingAmount > 0.1 ? cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 0.65) * pi * 0.25 * limbSwingAmount : 0; lf*/rb*.xRot = rest + a; rf*/lb*.xRot = rest - a` (HydroliscModel.java:317-341)
- **tail**: the tail sweeps side to side, each segment swinging wider than the one before it, and hangs still while the pet sits — `s = sitting ? 0 : cos(ageInTicks * 1.0 * 0.65) * pi * 0.15; tail1.yRot = s * 0.25; tail2.yRot = s * 0.5; tail3.yRot = s * 0.75; tail2 and tail3 positions re-derived along the previous segment's angle (5 and 8 units)` (HydroliscModel.java:342-352)
- **crest_outer**: the two outer crest feathers fan open and closed in mirror, wide and quick when healthy, slow and shallow as the pet is hurt — `hf = health / maxHealth; c = cos(ageInTicks * 0.75 * 0.65 * hf) * pi * 0.2 * hf; feather1.yRot = c - 0.9; feather3.yRot = -c + 0.9` (HydroliscModel.java:353, 355-357)
- **crest_centre**: the centre feather wags faster than the outer pair, and slows and shrinks with the pet's health — `hf = health / maxHealth; feather2.yRot = cos(ageInTicks * 1.25 * 0.65 * hf) * pi * 0.2 * hf` (HydroliscModel.java:353-354)
- `HydroliscModel.poseFrom` (HydroliscModel.java:322-366; orig ModelHydrolisc.java:308-348 in `render` (func_78088_a, :302-389; func_78087_a at :397-399 is a bare super call), wingspeed 0.65 from `new ModelHydrolisc(0.65f)` in orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:422): while the walking speed is above a tenth the leg angle is the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.3 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed, else 0; the four six-part legs pitch by it around rests of 0, -0.488, -2.347, -0.628, -0.628 and 0.174 - the front-left and rear-right positive, the front-right and rear-left the negative. The tail angle is the cosine of the age times 1.0 times the wingspeed, times pi, times 0.15, or 0 while sitting; the first ring yaws by a quarter of it, the second by half and the third by three quarters, the second ring placed 5 units from the first ring's bind pivot along the first ring's yaw (the cosine in z, the sine in x) and the third 8 units from the second along the second's yaw. The health fraction is the health over the max health; the middle feather yaws by the cosine of the age times 1.25 times the wingspeed times the fraction, times pi, times 0.2, times the fraction; the outer feathers yaw by plus and minus the cosine of the age times 0.75 times the wingspeed times the fraction, times pi, times 0.2, times the fraction, around -0.9 and 0.9. The walk position and the head angles play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (HydroliscGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
