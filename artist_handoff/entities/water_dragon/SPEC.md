## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Water Dragon is a tameable swimming dragon: a four-block body with a back fin, a four-piece neck with a neck fin, a head with a nose, a hinged jaw, a head fin and two ear fins, four flipper legs, and a tail of a base, a middle and a top and bottom fin (ModelWaterDragon.createBodyLayer, ModelWaterDragon.java:74-174). It is a TamableAnimal in the water-creature category (WaterDragon.java:64; ModEntities.java:596-597) with 150 HP, 20 attack and 8 armour (MobStats.WATER_DRAGON, createAttributes :163-170), a smooth swimming mover and water-bound navigation (:114-116, :174-175), faster in water than out (:167, :223). Wild it hunts monsters — and, untamed, players and the attackable non-mobs — in a 14-block box (isSuitableTarget :447-456; babies never hunt, :426): the dinosaur melee goal bites within four blocks plus half the target's width and, beyond that, fires the water cannon — eight-round bursts of water balls, one round in fifteen a small fireball, from a muzzle 1.5 blocks ahead and 1.75 up, the jaw held open (attacking 2) while it fires (WaterCanonAttackGoal :555-630). Cod tames it (:234-238), a tamed one follows its owner on any path, sits when told with an empty hand, breeds with crystal apples (:125-142, :275-276, :520); glass and a name tag have their uses (:255-270). Out of water it seeks the nearest water on a 1-in-25 roll (:341-350) and in water heals 1 on a 1-in-100 roll (:375). Cactus, water balls and its own kind cannot hurt it and hits are refused for 10 ticks (hurt :300-328). It spawns by day above y 50 with no other within 16 blocks (checkSpawnRules :525-531) in oceans, deep oceans and rivers (the hostile_water_dragon biome modifiers); a baby is drawn at half size (WaterDragonRenderer.java:43-44). Read it as a serpentine swimmer: the whole rear body and tail are one big wave, the flippers row, the fins tremble.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One speed-scaled travelling wave at 0.65 rad/tick (a 9.7-tick period) runs from the third body block to the tail: each of the four segments yaws 72 degrees at top speed, an eighth of a period behind the one before, placed at the end of the previous segment, the two tail fins riding the middle (ModelWaterDragon.java:184-198); the four flippers row 36 degrees at top speed on the same wave above a walking-speed threshold of 0.1, the left pair against the right (:183, :199-202). Standing still the body is straight and the flippers rest.
- The ear fins flap 18 degrees at 0.4 rad/tick (:203-205), the back fin rolls 3.6 degrees at 0.35 and the neck fin sways 18 degrees at 0.3, both still in the sitting pose (:206-215). The head fin's own 0.25 rad/tick wave is computed (:216-220) and then overwritten by the head's yaw (:229) — it never shows.
- The head turns three quarters of the look yaw with the nose, jaw, head fin and ears placed on it (:222-237). The jaw chomps 45 degrees at 0.6 rad/tick while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is 1 (the melee goal in reach), holds open at 0.45 while it is 2 (the water cannon firing, WaterCanonAttackGoal :572) and hangs shut at −0.25 otherwise (:221).
- The first two body blocks and the four neck segments are static; the walk position plays no part. The same waves run faster in water, where the dragon moves at its water speed (WaterDragon.java:223).
- The bite (doHurtTarget, WaterDragon.java:285-293) and the cannon (:567-629) are the melee goal's — nothing in the code fires an animation for them; the jaw's open pose while firing is the model's reading of attacking 2.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the rear body and tail snake side to side in a travelling wave with the swimming or walking speed, and the four flippers row with it, the left pair against the right — `w_k = cos(t * 1.3 * 0.5 - k * pi/4) * pi * 0.4 * amount (k = 0..3: body3, body4, tail1, tailmiddle = tailtop = tailbottom); positions along the chain by cos/sin x 7 / 5 / 3; a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3 * 0.5) * pi * 0.2 * amount : 0; Leg8.yRot = 0.58 + a; Leg2.yRot = -0.58 + a; Leg7.yRot = -0.58 - a; Leg1.yRot = 0.58 - a` (ModelWaterDragon.java:183-202; wingspeed 0.5 (:21))
- **ears**: the two ear fins flap slowly out and in together, riding the head's turn — `e = cos(t * 0.8 * 0.5) * pi * 0.1; leftear.yRot = 0.62 + e + headYaw; rightear.yRot = -0.62 - e + headYaw (placed on the head by the look)` (ModelWaterDragon.java:203-205, :232-237)
- **body_fin**: the back fin rolls faintly, and holds still when the dragon sits — `Bodyfin.zRot = sitting ? 0 : cos(t * 0.7 * 0.5) * pi * 0.02` (ModelWaterDragon.java:206-210)
- **neck_fin**: the neck fin sways slowly, and holds still when the dragon sits — `neackfin.yRot = sitting ? 0 : cos(t * 0.6 * 0.5) * pi * 0.1` (ModelWaterDragon.java:211-215)
- **jaw**: the jaw chomps wide while biting, holds open while the water cannon fires, and hangs shut otherwise; it rides the head's turn — `jaw.xRot = attacking == 1 ? cos(t * 1.2 * 0.5) * pi * 0.25 : (attacking == 2 ? 0.45 : -0.25); jaw.yRot = headYaw, placed 7 units ahead of the head` (ModelWaterDragon.java:221, :226-228)
