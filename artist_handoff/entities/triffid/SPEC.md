Closed (the synched DATA_OPEN_CLOSED at 0, :44-45) it cannot be hurt (:186-193); it opens one roll in eight every eighty ticks (:210-215) or whenever prey - anything but creepers, the ender reaper, its own kind, the two terrors, the pitch black, dragons and creative players - enters its 10-block box (:218-221, :264-277), turning to face it every tick (:138-146); within 5 blocks it raises its attacking flag and bites every 1-in-10 tick for 20 (:222-228, :42-43). A hit that lands closes it for 300 ticks, roots it and clears its fire (:150-177, :122-129, :200-204). It spawns wherever it is placed (:280-284; ModEntities.java:302-304). Read it as a trap plant: leaves curled tight when closed, swaying wide when open, the tongue whipping out straight and snapping back while it feeds.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Two synched states drive everything (EntityTriffid.java:42-45). DATA_OPEN_CLOSED (TriffidModel.java:1283): closed, every leaf segment adds a fixed 0.1225 rad to the one before, so the four leaves - 13 to 15 segments each, placed link by link (:1284-1349, :1593-1616) - hold a tight curl around the stem; open, the same angle sways as a 25-tick cosine between +0.1225 and -0.1225 per segment, and the leaves breathe from the full curl to a full uncurl, the tips swinging up to 1.8 rad.
- DATA_ATTACKING (:1350-1355): at rest the fifteen tongue segments hold 90 degrees alternating about -0.6 - an accordion folded at the mouth; attacking, |cos| sweeps that angle from 90 to 0 and back every 12.6 ticks, so the chain (:1357-1395, placed 3 and 6 px per segment) shoots out into a straight 45-unit lash and folds back - the bite's motion, though the hurt itself is the 1-in-10 pass (EntityTriffid.java:222-228).
- The base, roots, the sixteen stem blocks, the eleven petals and the twenty-three head plates are static; there is no head look, and the entity turns its whole body to face prey every tick (EntityTriffid.java:138-146). It does not walk: no wander goal, a re-anchor to its own position every hundred ticks (:135-137), no pushing (:117-120), and no horizontal motion during the hurt lockout (:122-129).
