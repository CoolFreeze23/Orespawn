## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A hostile armoured trooper bug: a ten-plate head with two eyes on bases, two antennae, two ridges, an upper jaw of nine plates with two ridges and six teeth, a lower jaw of twelve plates with a tongue, four mouth edges and an inner mouth, two mandible arms of six plates and two palps of four, and four legs of a coxa, a shoulder, a two-plate femur with an elbow, a three-plate tibia and a four-plate tarsus, all on a two-piece hip hub (TrooperBugModel.java:294-966, 134 parts), drawn at x1.1. 200 HP, 20 damage, 15 armour, speed 0.4 (MobStats.java:106; EntityTrooperBug.java:99-106). On a 1-in-5 tick it picks prey in a 12-block box - anything but the hydrolisc, the ender reaper and knight, endermen, creepers, its own kind, spit bugs and creative players (:237-238, :269-282, :307-337) - and one time in thirty conjures a Spit Bug between itself and its target (:240-251); it chases at 1.2 and, within 5 blocks plus half the target's width, rolls its bite on a 1-in-5 cadence for 20 with a 1.8 knockback (:88, :172-189; BugMeleeAttackGoal.Params.trooperBug; BugMeleeAttackGoal.java:159-174); between 4 and 8 blocks, on the ground, one cadence tick in ten it LEAPS - a 1.15 upward, 0.2-0.65 forward jump with its navigation cut (TrooperBugLeapAttackGoal.java:29-42; :147-170); the synched DATA_ATTACKING int (:46-47) is held at 1 in reach and on the leap. It takes one hit per 20 ticks and ignores falls and cactus (:191-216). It spawns in the dark, by day only on a 2-in-20 roll, with clear air about it (:344-351; ModEntities.java:307-309). Read it as a paratrooper beetle: a slow heavy four-leg scuttle, every mouthpart twitching at rest and thrashing when it fights, the leap its signature.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four legs sweep about the vertical in two opposed pairs and each leg bends at the knee as it swings forward, the whole leg placed link by link - slow and heavy — `s = sin(t * 2.0 * 0.22) * pi * 0.12 * amount; up = (sin at t + 0.1 > s) ? |cos(t * 2.0 * 0.22) * pi * 0.12 * amount| : 0; leftFront(s, up); leftRear(-s, up); s' = sin(t * 2.0 * 0.22 + pi) * pi * 0.12 * amount (up' likewise); rightFront(-s', up'); rightRear(s', up'); per leg: femur, elbow, tibia and tarsus pieces yRot = rest + angle; femur xRot = 1.115 / 1.078 + up; tibia xRot = 1.871 / 1.817 / 1.762 - up; tarsus xRot = 1.08 + up; positions chained by cos / sin of the yaw x 26 and 32` (TrooperBugModel.java:1013-1028 (the phases), :1169-1311 (the four leg helpers); wingspeed 0.22 (:21))
- **mandibles**: the two mandible claws pitch against each other: a slow small working at rest, a wide fast chomp while the attacking flag is up — `m = attacking ? cos(t * 2.6 * 0.22) * pi * 0.2 : cos(t * 0.3 * 0.22) * pi * 0.05; arm1part3.xRot = arm1part3b.xRot = 1.56 + m; arm2part3.xRot = arm2part3b.xRot = 1.56 - m` (TrooperBugModel.java:988-992)
- **palps**: the two palps wave in opposition: slowly at rest, fast and wide while attacking — `p = attacking ? cos(t * 2.5 * 0.22) * pi * 0.15 : cos(t * 0.5 * 0.22) * pi * 0.05; arm4part1.yRot = arm4part1b.yRot = arm4part1c.yRot = p; arm3part1.yRot = arm3part1b.yRot = arm3part1c.yRot = -p` (TrooperBugModel.java:981-987)
- **antennae**: the antenna tips sway in and out in opposition: slowly at rest, faster and wider while attacking — `a = attacking ? cos(t * 1.4 * 0.22) * pi * 0.1 : cos(t * 0.4 * 0.22) * pi * 0.05; antenna2part2.yRot = 0.78 + a; antenna1part2.yRot = -0.78 - a` (TrooperBugModel.java:978-980)
- **ridges**: the two head ridges and two upper-jaw ridges flare in and out in opposition: barely at rest, wide while attacking — `r = attacking ? cos(t * 1.0 * 0.22) * pi * 0.1 : cos(t * 0.1 * 0.22) * pi * 0.02; headleftridge.yRot = -0.25 + r; headrightridge.yRot = 0.25 - r; upperjawridgeleft.yRot = -0.372 + r; upperjawridgeright.yRot = 0.372 - r` (TrooperBugModel.java:993-997)
- **jaw**: the twelve lower-jaw plates open and shut together: a tiny slow breath at rest, a wide fast chomp while attacking — `j = attacking ? cos(t * 2.6 * 0.22) * pi * 0.1 : cos(t * 0.3 * 0.22) * pi * 0.015; jawbase, jawbase2..7, jawend, jawleft, jawright xRot = 0.22 + j; jawbase8.xRot = 0.2146 + j; jawbase8.yRot = 0.1487 + j; jawbase9.xRot = 0.1 + j; jawbase9.yRot = 0.07 + j` (TrooperBugModel.java:998-1012)
