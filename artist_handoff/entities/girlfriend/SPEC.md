### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the vanilla biped walk: the legs stride in opposition and the arms swing against them, the stride phased by the distance walked rather than by time — `right_leg.xRot = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662) * 1.4 * limbSwingAmount; left_leg.xRot = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662 + pi) * 1.4 * limbSwingAmount; right_arm.xRot = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662 + pi) * 2.0 * limbSwingAmount * 0.5; left_arm.xRot = cos(limbSwing * 0.6662) * 2.0 * limbSwingAmount * 0.5` (vanilla HumanoidModel.setupAnim (net.minecraft.client.model.HumanoidModel; ModelGirlfriend.java:9 extends it with no override))
- **arm_sway_roll**: at all times the arms sway gently outward and back about the shoulder, in opposition — `right_arm.zRot += cos(ageInTicks * 0.09) * 0.05 + 0.05; left_arm.zRot -= cos(ageInTicks * 0.09) * 0.05 + 0.05` (vanilla HumanoidModel.setupAnim (ModelGirlfriend.java:9 extends it with no override))
- **arm_sway_pitch**: a second, slower sway of the arms fore and aft, in opposition — `right_arm.xRot += sin(ageInTicks * 0.067) * 0.05; left_arm.xRot -= sin(ageInTicks * 0.067) * 0.05` (vanilla HumanoidModel.setupAnim (ModelGirlfriend.java:9 extends it with no override))
- vanilla `HumanoidModel.setupAnim` (net.minecraft.client.model.HumanoidModel, NeoForge 21.1.223, HumanoidModel.java:137-286 by the jar's line table; ModelGirlfriend.java extends it with no override - the probe's classic side is its transcription `ModelGirlfriend.poseFrom` -> `HumanoidClassicPose.setupAnim` (HumanoidClassicPose.java, the same bytecode statement by statement) after the renderer's three field sets; the classic renderer is a HumanoidMobRenderer - orig ClientProxyOreSpawn.java:386 `new RenderGirlfriend(new ModelBiped(), 0.5f)`, vanilla ModelBiped in 1.7.10 too): the head yaws by the look yaw in degrees times 0.017453292 and pitches by the look pitch the same way (a quarter turn down while gliding on an elytra, which a tameable never does); the legs pitch by the cosine of the walk position times 0.6662, times 1.4, times the walking speed - the right leg, and the left leg with the phase advanced by pi - and yaw and roll by 0.005 (the right) and -0.005 (the left); the arms pitch by the cosine of the walk position times 0.6662 (the left; plus pi for the right), times 2, times the walking speed, times 0.5, and yaw 0; while seated on a vehicle (a boat or a minecart) both arms pitch a further -0.62831855 and the legs take -1.4137167 of pitch, 0.31415927 (the right) / -0.31415927 (the left) of yaw and 0.07853982 / -0.07853982 of roll; the arms bob at all times - each rolls by the cosine of the age in ticks times 0.09, times 0.05, plus 0.05 (the right positive, the left negative) and pitches by the sine of the age times 0.067, times 0.05 (the right positive, the left negative); during a swing - the attack progress above 0, the renderer's `getAttackAnim(partialTick)` read at the FRAME's partial tick, which the seam now carries (PoseInputs.partialTick, the owner's closing set item 3) - the body yaws by the sine of the square root of the progress times two pi, times 0.2 (negated for a left-handed swing), both arms are re-placed 5 units along that yaw and yaw with it, and the swinging arm pitches down by the sine of a smoothed progress times pi, times 1.2, plus the sine of the progress times pi, times minus the head pitch less 0.7, times 0.75, yaws by twice the body yaw and rolls by the sine of the progress times pi, times -0.4; the hat copies the head. The swim branch (visually swimming with a swim amount above 0, `getSwimAmount(partialTick)`) and the crouch never occur for this mob. The walk position phases the stride and the age the bob.
