## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A passive ground bird with a fan: a 5x4x11 body on two 7-long stick legs, a raked neck to a small head with a beak, three zero-thickness head-crest feathers and seven zero-thickness 8x30 tail feathers stacked on one pivot (ModelPeacock.java:62-124, sixteen parts). 15 HP, speed 0.38 and an attack attribute of 4 (Peacock.java:38-39, :67-72). It flees monsters within 8 blocks and players within 12 and panics when hurt (:60-62), strolls clear of water (:63), breeds on a crystal apple (:59, :186-188), and on a 1-in-10 tick hunts the nearest termite within 10 blocks, pecking it for a flat 6 within 2 (:141-151); one tick in 5000 it lays one to three spawn eggs (:152-162). Its fan is a timer: a blink state that flips on for 25-125 ticks and off for 50-350 (:40-42, :78-92) opens the seven tail feathers and raises the crest (ModelPeacock.java:135-151). An ambient creature that spawns in the first half of the day between y 50 and 100 with clear air above, at most two others near (:197-204; ModEntities.java:410-411). Read it as a peacock: a stiff strut, a sudden fan, the crest up.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (ModelPeacock.java:132-134): the two legs swing 27 degrees x the walking speed in opposition at 0.975 rad/tick, a 6.4-tick period, at the 0.38 walk (Peacock.java:38).
- One state branch on the blink timer (ModelPeacock.java:135-169; Peacock.java:78-92): while it is on, the seven tail feathers pitch up 60 degrees and splay by 23-degree steps to either side of the centre feather and the three crest feathers rise; while it is off, the tail lies flat behind and the crest folds back at -61 degrees. The timer is a plain field ticked on both sides from each side's own random (:40-42, :78-92), so the fan is not a synched state — a transport for it waits for the SPEC when the rig lands.
- The body, neck, head and beak are static; the head does not follow the look. The termite peck (Peacock.java:144-146, a flat 6 within 2 blocks) and the egg (:152-162) draw no pose.

Renaming, re-parenting or deleting a locked bone is refused; a `_preview` file delivered (a Blockbench-only aid, never in the jar — ruled 2026-09-06, Q15 (a); leave it out of the delivery); a returned `.geo.json` at all (the shipped rig is used regardless — do not re-export it); a clip length far from the one the sheet states (rule 5: a phase-locked creature's loops at 1.0 s — ruled 2026-09-06, Q14 (a); a native creature's clips at their shipped length; a one-shot at the length its row states); an optional clip not delivered; an alias-named texture (the canonical name is preferred); a `format_version` other than 1.8.0; a number written as a string; an unknown keyframe key; a clip without `animation_length`; an unreadable `.bbmodel`.
