## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A small flat passive fish: a flat 8x1x12 body slab, a nub of a head, a two-piece tail and two side fins (ModelFlounder.java:40-62, six parts). 5 HP, speed 0.25, no attack at all (Flounder.java:65-70, ATTACK_DAMAGE 0). It drifts with the current (pushed by fluid, :73-75), flees players within 8 blocks and panics when hurt (:58-59), breeds on a crystal apple (:57, :185-187, :191-193) and, stranded, scans up to ten blocks for water and flops toward it, drying out 1 HP at a time when none is found (:86-107); in water it splashes and heals (:109-112). A water creature that spawns in daylight above y 50, at most ten together (:154-161; ModEntities.java:376-378). Read it as a flatfish: the side fins sculling, the tail sweeping, a lazy tail flick at rest.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Three gait-scaled rhythms, each gated by a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (ModelFlounder.java:71-81): the left fin rolls 45 degrees x the walking speed at 1.3 rad/tick and the right at 1.7 (:72-73, :78-79), so the fins never beat in step; the two tail pieces pitch together 45 degrees x the speed at 1.2 rad/tick (:80-81).
- At rest the fins go flat (:75-76) and the tail keeps a 9-degree flick at 0.7 rad/tick, a 9-tick period (:80) — the only motion of a still flounder. The body and the head are static; the head does not follow the look.
- The same formulas draw it in water and on land: setupAnim has no water branch, so the swim and the stranded flop toward water (Flounder.java:86-107) look alike today, scaled only by how fast it moves (speed 0.25, :41).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the left and right fins roll up and down with the stride at two rhythms that never line up, and both tail pieces sweep together at a third; at rest the fins go flat and the tail keeps a small slow flick — `lfin.zRot = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.3) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; rfin.zRot = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.7) * pi * 0.25 * amount : 0; tail1.xRot = tail2.xRot = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.2) * pi * 0.25 * amount : cos(t * 0.7) * pi * 0.05` (ModelFlounder.java:71-81)
- `ModelFlounder.setupAnim` (ModelFlounder.java:68-82; orig ModelFlounder.java:67-76 in `render` (func_78088_a, :61-83; func_78087_a at :91-93 is a bare super call); no wingspeed): while the walking speed is above a tenth the left fin rolls by the cosine of the age in ticks times 1.3, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed and the right fin by the cosine of the age times 1.7, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed, else both hold 0; the two tail pieces pitch by the cosine of the age times 1.2, times pi, times 0.25, times the walking speed while the walking speed is above a tenth, else by the cosine of the age times 0.7, times pi, times 0.05. The walk position, the head angles and the entity's state play no part. The GeckoLib hook is the same (FlounderGeoReplacement.applyCustomAnimations).
