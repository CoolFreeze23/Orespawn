## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


The Nightmare (the `pitch_black` registry) is a winged, many-toothed boss that comes in five sizes: a two-block body with two shoulders, a three-segment neck to a seven-piece head with two eyes, twenty-three teeth and a five-piece lower jaw, two wings of three spars, three membranes and three claws each, two three-piece legs on seven-piece clawed feet with a leg spike each, and a nine-segment forked tail with two points and six spikes (ModelPitchBlack.createBodyLayer, ModelPitchBlack.java:229-637). Its size tier (1 to 5, scale 0.5 to 4, PitchBlack.java:106-128) sets its stats — 125 to 1000 HP, 15 to 120 attack, 11 to 18 armour, 0.25 to 0.6 speed (:119-125, applySizeTierAttributes :265-296) — its hitbox (2.5 x 3.5 blocks times the scale, :299-307), its purple boss bar's name (:130-131, :292) and, in the model, the rate of every rhythm. Grounded (DATA_ACTIVITY 0) it wanders and looks about (:172-176) and takes off on a 1-in-10 tick with prey in sight (:461-471) or a 4-in-5 chance of a rare roll (tick :420-441); flying (activity 1) it steers itself between flight targets (:474-488, :516-528) and on a 1-in-8 tick hunts anything living in sight within 16 plus six scales of blocks except its own kind and eight listed allies (:489-511, :566-600), flying to two blocks above the target with the attacking flag up and biting within five blocks plus half the target's width plus the scale (doHurtTarget :365-401: the tier's attack, a knockback scaled by size; the Ender Dragon hit part-wise). It lands only on a rare roll with solid ground below and no prey (:420-437); hits are refused for 20 ticks and turn its flight on the attacker (hurt :531-543); its wings sound every 20 ticks (:406-412) and it heals 1 plus the scale on a 1-in-250 roll (:421). It spawns at night in the dark in the Chaos and Island biomes (chaos_biome.json, island_biome.json) — in Chaos never within 16 blocks of another, and above scale 1 only under a clear air pocket sized by its tier (checkSpawnRules :672-690) (chaos_biome.json, island_biome.json) and strews its loot by its size (:625-630). Read it as a bat-winged stalker: a heavy stride on the ground, a slow wide beat in the air, the forked tail sweeping.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Every rhythm but the jaw's is divided by the size scale (ModelPitchBlack.java:653): a scale-4 Nightmare moves four times slower than a scale-1 one, a scale-0.5 twice faster.
- On the ground (DATA_ACTIVITY 0, PitchBlack.java:93) one speed-scaled stride at 0.4875 / scale rad/tick: each foot — seven claw pieces moved as one — slides 12 x scale units back and forth and lifts 6 x scale on its forward half, the lower leg and its spike ride it and pitch 32 degrees, the thigh and upper piece swing the same above, the right leg half a period behind (:765-847). In the air the feet hang at a fixed height with the claws curled and, while attacking, paddle 11 degrees at 0.5525 / scale rad/tick (:848-904).
- The wings beat 43 degrees at 0.2925 / scale rad/tick in flight, the middle spars 5/3 and the tips twice that, the claws 3/2, each placed at the end of the spar before; on the ground they hang folded at −45 degrees with a 3.6-degree stir (:658-698). The head turns 0.55 of the look yaw on the ground and only 0.2 in flight, the neck's last segment half of that, and the head pieces, eyes, upper teeth and jaw follow (:699-728).
- The five-piece lower jaw and its ten teeth chomp 29 degrees at 0.5525 rad/tick (not scaled) while the synced DATA_ATTACKING byte is set (raised at :494 with prey in the hunt), and at rest one period in twenty on a per-entity latch (:729-760).
