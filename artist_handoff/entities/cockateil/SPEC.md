## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A small passive parrot in one of six plumages: a boxy body and head with a beak, a three-feather crest, three tail feathers, two jointed wings and two legs. It flies up when disturbed, breeds, and can be tamed and carried. Read it as a bright, restless little bird: wings beating, crest and tail never quite still.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- Several independent rhythms run at all times, none scaled by walking speed; the classic code has no rest pose. The transcription ships idle (no bone) plus one clip per rhythm: the primary group (`primary_group`; the first group when the seed names none) carries the bare `walk` - a label, not a semantic: the contract's fly -> walk fallback is what plays a flyer's walk in flight (owner 2026-09-13, addendum item 26 (2)) - and every other rhythm is `walk_<group>`, so the GeckoLib candidate plays the transcription behind the switch.
- Five rhythms: the wing beat (the primary group: its clip is the bare walk), the slow tail rock and three crest feathers each at its own sway; body, head, beak and legs are static. The wing roots bind at -1.561 / 1.595 rad and the formula re-centres them at -/+1.5, so the transcription's keys carry that offset (base minus bind).
- The default renderer poses it from the classic code; the GeckoLib candidate sits behind the dev switch (-Dorespawn.dev.geckolibRenderers) and your clips replace the shipped transcription file for file.

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: the wing roots beat about the body's long axis around a raised rest, the wing tips follow with the same shape and no offset; left and right mirrored — `lwing1.zRot = -1.5 + c; lwing2.zRot = c; rwing1.zRot = 1.5 - c; rwing2.zRot = -c, with c = cos(ageInTicks * 1.5 * 1.0) * pi * 0.35` (ModelCockateil.java:125-129; CockateilGeoReplacement.java)
- **tail**: the three tail feathers rock slowly up and down together — `tailfeather1..3.xRot = cos(ageInTicks * 0.3 * 1.0) * pi * 0.1` (ModelCockateil.java:130-132; CockateilGeoReplacement.java)
- **feather1**: the front crest feather sways side to side — `feather1.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 1.1 * 1.0) * pi * 0.08` (ModelCockateil.java:133; CockateilGeoReplacement.java)
- **feather2**: the middle crest feather sways a little faster — `feather2.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 1.2 * 1.0) * pi * 0.08` (ModelCockateil.java:134; CockateilGeoReplacement.java)
- **feather3**: the back crest feather sways faster still — `feather3.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 1.0) * pi * 0.08` (ModelCockateil.java:135; CockateilGeoReplacement.java)
