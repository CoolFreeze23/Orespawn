## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending, landed on its hook by the first Tier-1 slice (T1a, 2026-09-15; hitbox profiles excluded - the classic single hitbox stays until the profile phase). The Prince Adult is the royal heir full-grown, on the King's own rig at its own size: a body with a chest, back plates and six ridge spines, three four-segment necks each ending in a maned, spiked, jawed and toothed head, two clawed legs of thigh, upper leg, lower leg and foot with three double claws and a rear claw each, two ten-panel wings, and a seven-segment tail ending in two tip pieces and a spike (ModelThePrinceAdult.createBodyLayer, ModelThePrinceAdult.java:265-864). It is a TamableAnimal and a rideable flyer with 3000 HP, 0.36 speed and 100 attack (createAttributes ThePrinceAdult.java:180-188), follows its owner, is tempted by beef, wanders and looks at anything within 20 blocks (:136-142), avenges and defends its owner (:149-152) and hunts monsters (:157-178). Its activity byte is 0 on the ground and 1 in flight (no physics when flying, :219; the wings sound every 30 ticks, :225-235): it takes off on a 1-in-10 tick with prey in sight (:612-621), when its owner flies or is far (:242-246), or when hurt (hurt :278-310, a 20-tick guard). Flying (:740-790) it flies to just above its target with the attacking flag up and bites within ten blocks plus half the target's width (doHurtTarget :272) or, within about 24 blocks with its fire lit on a 1-in-2, shoots — a big fireball, a thunderbolt or an ice ball from six blocks ahead and 3.5 up when its head is aimed (shootSomethingAt :528-577); a tamed one below a quarter health flees. Ridden, it fires a canon trio on the strafe keys, cycling the shot per head (:409-413, :440-490), and bites for its rider within ten blocks (:498-517). Its three necks rise and fall on synced head extensions random-walked between 0 and 60 (:248-266). Tamed and left alone for 288000 ticks with the full-power-King option it becomes The King (:626-635, transformToKing :895-905); a diamond block tames and heals it and fills that counter (:914-918), an empty hand mounts it (:938-939), ice and flint and steel take and give its fire (:973-985). It has no natural spawn (it grows from the Prince Teen). Read it as the King in miniature: the vast slow wing-beat, three heads snaking on their own rhythms, and a real stride on the ground the King never has.
