## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A small ambient butterfly: a one-cube body bar with a one-cube head at the front and four flat wing panels a side — an inner forewing, a larger outer forewing, a hindwing behind it and a long narrow tail streamer (ButterflyModel.createBodyLayer, ButterflyModel.java:54-102). It drifts about the air on the butterfly flight preset (EntityButterfly.java:74; the vertical drag at tick :103-107 keeps it hovering), wears one of four skins (ButterflyRenderer.java:33-37), and an empty-handed right-click on it carries the player to the Chaos dimension and back (mobInteract, EntityButterfly.java:117-149). In the Islands its type-1 form hunts prey and nips for 1.0 on a 1-in-2 roll (doHurtTarget :161-165, reached from ButterflyIslandsHuntGoal.java:83). It spawns by day, in the air, above y 50 or anywhere in the Islands (checkSpawnRules :209-219), in forest, jungle and taiga swarms (the swarm_butterfly biome modifiers). Read it as a fluttering insect: everything is in the wings.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One rhythm runs at all times: the eight wing panels roll about the body's long axis at 1.3 rad/tick (a 4.83-tick beat), 45 degrees each way, the left set mirroring the right (ButterflyModel.java:109-115). The body and the head are static; the walk position, the walking speed, the head angles and the entity's state play no part in the pose.
- The flight itself is the entity's: the butterfly preset of AmbientFlightGoal steers the delta movement (EntityButterfly.java:74; ButterflyIslandsHuntGoal carries the Islands hunt in the same goal) and tick :103-107 damps the vertical speed to 0.6 each tick, so it hovers and drifts rather than falls. The model never banks, pitches or bobs the body with that motion.
- The renderer draws the head, then the body, then the wing pairs inner to outer (renderToBuffer, ButterflyModel.java:119-130).

### 4.2 The math

- **wings**: the eight wing panels flap about the body's long axis together, 45 degrees up and down, the left side mirroring the right; nothing else moves — `rightwing.zRot = rightwing2.zRot = rightwing3.zRot = rightwing4.zRot = cos(ageInTicks * 1.3 * 1.0) * pi * 0.25; leftwing*.zRot = -rightwing.zRot` (ButterflyModel.java:109-115 (wingspeed 1.0 from ButterflyRenderer.java:28))
