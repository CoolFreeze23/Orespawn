## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A tameable, skittish grazer: a 6x6x13 body with a chest wedge, a long raked neck with a throat fluff, a cube head with a nose, a mouth, two ears and two three-segment antlers, four legs of four or five pieces down to a hoof, and a stub tail (ModelGazelle.java:101-269, 34 parts). 15 HP, speed 0.3 and no attack (Gazelle.java:82-87). It flees monsters within 8 blocks and players within 12 (:73, :76), panics when hurt (:75), is tempted by wheat (:74) and tamed by it one time in two (:226-249); tamed it follows its owner (:72), sits on a right-click (:277-282), is untamed with glass (:251-265) and takes at most 10 per hit (:210-216). It grazes: hurt or on a 1-in-750 tick it scans up to ten blocks for carrots, potatoes or grass, walks there, eats the block with a burp and heals (:89-94, :150-182); every 250 ticks or so it drifts toward another gazelle (:184-189). It spawns between y 50 and 100 on dirt or grass (:288-294; ModEntities.java:385-386), breeds on a crystal apple (:325-327) and its fawn inherits the owner (:340-348). Read it as a deer: a light diagonal trot, the ears flicking, the tail wagging, the head down to graze.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm above a limbSwingAmount of 0.1 (ModelGazelle.java:276-297): the eighteen leg pieces swing 22 degrees x the walking speed about their rests at 0.715 rad/tick (an 8.8-tick period), each leg a stiff chain, the left front with the right rear and the right front with the left rear — a diagonal trot at the 0.3 walk (Gazelle.java:53).
- Two unscaled rhythms: the ears flick 4 degrees at 0.5 rad/tick over the head's yaw (:299, :302-303) and the tail wags 11 degrees about 1.0 rad at 0.1 rad/tick, a 63-tick period (:311-313) — the one state branch, which skips the wag while the entity is in the crouching pose (a pose a mob never takes), so in practice it always runs.
- The head, nose and mouth yaw together with the look at 0.45 of the head yaw and never pitch (:300-301); the six antler segments follow the head's yaw (:304-309). The chest, body, neck and throat fluff are static.
- The sit toggle (Gazelle.java:277-282, setInSittingPose) and the graze (:150-182: the walk to the block, the bite, the burp) draw no pose today — the model has no sitting branch and no head pitch.

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: the four legs swing fore and aft as stiff chains, every piece of a leg together: the left front with the right rear one way, the right front with the left rear the other — a diagonal trot — `a = amount > 0.1 ? cos(t * 1.1 * 0.65) * pi * 0.12 * amount : 0; lfleg1..4.xRot = rest + a; rfleg1..4.xRot = rest - a; lrleg1..5.xRot = rest - a; rrleg1..5.xRot = rest + a` (ModelGazelle.java:276-297; wingspeed 0.65 (:24))
- **ears**: the ears flick gently in step, riding the head's turn — `e = cos(t * 0.5) * pi * 0.02; lear.yRot = rear.yRot = 1.57 + head.yRot + e` (ModelGazelle.java:299, :302-303)
- **tail**: the tail wags slowly up and down at all times — `if (!crouching) tail.xRot = 1.0 + cos(t * 0.1) * pi * 0.06` (ModelGazelle.java:311-313)
