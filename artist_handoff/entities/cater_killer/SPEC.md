## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)

A huge hostile caterpillar: a head with a false head, two two-part tusks and two jaws; three front segments with side spikes, top spikes and legs; six rear segments with feet and spikes; a last segment with back spikes. It hunts along the ground. Read it as a spined caterpillar: the head bobbing (violently when it attacks), the jaws working, the front legs paddling, the rear segments stretching and bunching with its stride, the spikes rippling down its length.

### 4.2 The math

- **jaws**: the jaws work slowly at rest and snap fast and wide when attacking, mirrored — `a = attacking ? cos(t*1.7*0.22)*pi*0.07 : cos(t*1.3*0.22)*pi*0.025; ljaw.zRot = 0.139 + a; rjaw.zRot = -0.139 - a` (CaterKillerModel.poseFrom; CaterKillerGeoReplacement.java)
- **head_bob**: the whole head group rides up and down (a POSITION write): gently at rest, hard when attacking; the three front segments follow with a fading share — `headoff = attacking ? cos(t*1.7*0.22)*8 : cos(t*0.3*0.22)*2; Head/falsehead.y = -8 + headoff; tusks.y = -25 + headoff; jaws.y = -1 + headoff; seg1 parts.y = base + headoff/(i+1) + 8*i` (CaterKillerModel.poseFrom; CaterKillerGeoReplacement.java)
- **tusk_tips**: the tusk tips flick about the vertical, each at its own rhythm — `ltusk2.yRot = 0.802 + cos(t*2.11*0.22)*pi*0.08; rtusk2.yRot = -0.802 + cos(t*2.3*0.22)*pi*0.08` (CaterKillerModel.poseFrom; CaterKillerGeoReplacement.java)
- **front_spikes**: the front segments' side spikes ripple, mirrored — `seg1lspike_i.zRot = cos(t*0.91*0.22 + i*pi/8)*pi*0.08; seg1rspike_i.zRot = -that` (CaterKillerModel.poseFrom; CaterKillerGeoReplacement.java)
- **front_legs**: the front legs paddle gently at rest and thrash when attacking, mirrored — `a_i = attacking ? cos(t*2.91*0.22 + i*pi/8)*pi*0.15 : cos(t*0.35*0.22 + i*pi/8)*pi*0.04; seg1lleg_i.xRot = a_i; seg1rleg_i.xRot = -a_i` (CaterKillerModel.poseFrom; CaterKillerGeoReplacement.java)
- **stride**: the six rear segments stretch and bunch along the body with the stride (a POSITION write scaled by the walking speed), the last segment trailing — `zdist_i = cos(t*1.7*0.22 + i*pi/4)*1.5*amount; seg2 parts_i.z = 39 + (16 + zdist_i)*i; seg3 parts.z = seg2_5.z + 16 (back spikes + 6)` (CaterKillerModel.poseFrom; CaterKillerGeoReplacement.java)
- **rear_spikes**: the rear segments' side spikes ripple down the body, mirrored — `seg2lspike_i.zRot = cos(t*0.4*0.22 - i*pi/8)*pi*0.07 (i = 0..5, the last segment i = 6); the right ones the negative` (CaterKillerModel.poseFrom; CaterKillerGeoReplacement.java)
- **back_spikes**: the two back spikes wave, each on its own pitch and yaw rhythms — `seg3lbackspike.xRot = -0.977 + cos(t*0.81*0.22)*pi*0.04; seg3rbackspike.xRot = -0.977 + cos(t*0.87*0.22)*pi*0.04; .yRot = +-0.28 + cos(t*1.11/1.3*0.22)*pi*0.04` (CaterKillerModel.poseFrom; CaterKillerGeoReplacement.java)
