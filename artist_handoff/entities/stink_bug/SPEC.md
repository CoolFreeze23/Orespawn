## 1. What this mob is (AUTHORED — draft for the owner's edit)
> **DRAFT** — DRAFT — authored by a seed lane from the entity code, 2026-09-13; the owner's edit pending. Nothing in this section is ruled: it was drafted from the entity code and the model, every claim citing its line, for the owner's edit.


A tiny passive stink bug: a 6x5x8 body under a head with a jaw plate, two antennae, eight bumps on its back and two rocking nubs, a 4x4x6 tail block studded with twenty-two spots, and six 2x2 feet on six leg stubs (StinkBugModel.java:124-322, 50 parts), drawn at x0.85. 5 HP, speed 0.15, no attack (EntityStinkBug.java:57-62). It flees players within 4 blocks and panics when hurt (:50-51), breeds on a crystal apple (:49, :113-118) and, when it dies, releases a gas of nausea for fifteen seconds on everything within 8 blocks around and 10 above it, with a fart for a death sound (:75-78, :85-102). It spawns above y 50 (:120-126; ModEntities.java:449-451). Read it as a beetle: the feet twitching in a quick patter, the antennae waving, the spotted tail breathing.

## 4. Current animation behaviour — plain English and the source formulas

- One gait rhythm with no threshold (StinkBugModel.java:330-332): five of the six feet patter 54 degrees x the walking speed as a sine at 2.325 rad/tick (a 2.7-tick period), the right front and rear against the other three; the left middle foot is never written and stays still - a quirk of the model, not a design.
- Four unscaled rhythms run at all times: the two back nubs rock 36 degrees in opposition on a 21-tick sine (:333-334), the jaw plate nods 7 degrees on a 42-tick sine (:335-336), the antennae wave 27 degrees on four sines of 17-21 ticks (:337-340), and the tail block with its twenty-two spots breathes 18 degrees on an 84-tick sine (:341-357).
- The body, head, eight back bumps and six leg stubs are static; the head does not follow the look. The death gas (EntityStinkBug.java:85-102) is an effect on the kill, not a pose; there is no attack and no hurt sound (:70-73).

### 4.2 The math

- **gait**: five of the six feet patter fore and aft in two opposed sets with the stride; the left middle foot stays still — `a = sin(t * 3.1 * 0.75) * pi * 0.3 * amount; f1.xRot = f3.xRot = a; f2.xRot = f4.xRot = f6.xRot = -a; f5 unwritten` (StinkBugModel.java:330-332; wingspeed 0.75 (:14))
- **nubs**: the two back nubs rock side to side in opposition — `n = sin(t * 0.4 * 0.75) * pi * 0.2; b9.zRot = n; b10.zRot = -n` (StinkBugModel.java:333-334)
- **jaw**: the jaw plate nods slowly at all times — `jaw.xRot = 0.18 + sin(t * 0.2 * 0.75) * pi * 0.04` (StinkBugModel.java:335-336)
- **antennae**: the two antennae wave on four unrelated slow rhythms, never in step — `h1.xRot = 0.52 + sin(t * 0.4 * 0.75) * pi * 0.15; h1.yRot = -0.3 + sin(t * 0.43 * 0.75) * pi * 0.15; h2.xRot = 0.52 + sin(t * 0.46 * 0.75) * pi * 0.15; h2.yRot = 0.3 + sin(t * 0.49 * 0.75) * pi * 0.15` (StinkBugModel.java:337-340)
- **tail**: the spotted tail block breathes slowly up and down at all times, the spots riding it — `tail.xRot = t1..t22.xRot = -0.2 + sin(t * 0.1 * 0.75) * pi * 0.1` (StinkBugModel.java:341-357)
