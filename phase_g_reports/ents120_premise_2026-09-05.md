# ENT-S-120 premise check — a 1.7.10 player's `posY` on the server (2026-09-05)

Verified against Mojang's official 1.7.10 client jar (the BUG-041 copy: `client.jar` from the version manifest, SHA-1
`e80d9b3bf5085002218d4be59e668bac718abbc6`, 5,256,245 bytes; the jar carries the integrated server's classes),
under the owner's law 11 ("before a mass change that rests on a claim about the original engine, verify the claim
against the real 1.7.10 artifact"). Pure-Python constant-pool scan over the 1,803 classes to find the two obfuscated
classes, then `javap -c -p` (JDK 21) on those alone.

## What the bytecode says

| class (obf) | MCP name | evidence (javap) |
|---|---|---|
| `sa` | `Entity` | declares `public float L;` (line 33 of `javap -p sa`) and `public float W;` (line 45); `public void b(double, double, double)` (`setPosition`) stores `s` / `t` (posX / posY) and subtracts `getfield L:F` at offsets 41 and 65 when placing the bounding box — `L` is `yOffset`, `W` is `stepHeight` |
| `yz` | `EntityPlayer` (extends `sv` = `EntityLivingBase` extends `sa`) | `<init>`: `112: ldc #20 // float 1.62f` → `114: putfield #250 // Field L:F` — the 1.62 `yOffset` the census §0 cites |
| `mw` | `EntityPlayerMP` (extends `yz`; the only `yz` subclass whose pool references `net/minecraft/server/MinecraftServer`) | `public mw(net.minecraft.server.MinecraftServer, mt, GameProfile, mx)`: `222: fconst_0` → `223: putfield #164 // Field W:F` and **`227: fconst_0` → `228: putfield #163 // Field L:F`** — the server player zeroes `stepHeight` and `yOffset` |

Neither `yz` nor `mw` declares its own `L` or `W`; both stores hit `sa`'s fields (the three classes with `1.62f` in
their pools are `blt`, `mw`, `yz`; `blg` is the other `yz` subclass, the client player).

## What it means for the finding

- On the SERVER a 1.7.10 player's `posY` was its feet: the bounding box sat at `posY − 0`, exactly as for every other
  entity — and exactly as the port's `getY()`. The eye-level `posY` (1.62) existed on the client only (`yz` / `blg`).
- Every site the census (`phase_g_reports/ents120_census_2026-09-04.md`) lists runs server-side: entity AI
  (`updateAITasks`, `onUpdate` under `!worldObj.isRemote`), target scans, projectile aims, spawns and effects (the
  server spawns), item use whose entity spawns are server-side. For all of them `player.posY` in 1.7.10 equalled the
  port's `player.getY()`: there is no engine-frame difference to adopt, and the `OrigPos` sweep as ruled would move
  the port 1.62 blocks AWAY from 1.7.10 (flight targets one block too high, aims at the head where 1.7.10 aimed at
  the feet + k, reach shortened, the Kraken hold 1.62 too low, the Molenoid digging up, …).
- The census §0 table (orig horizontal reach `sqrt(R² − 1.62²)` against a level player) and the night ruling's
  "Effect, for the record" (item 19) describe the client frame, which no listed site used.
- What remains of ENT-S-120: code that ran on the CLIENT with a player operand — renderers, client-side particles
  and sounds, client-only item paths. Whether any OreSpawn site of that kind reads a player's `posY` is a separate,
  small census; the T3a refuter's original observation (`EntityButterfly` / the eye-level convention) and the
  Chainsaw lane's ‡ rows (ITEM-070) were both made under the client reading.
