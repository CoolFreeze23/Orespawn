GeoBone.getWorldPosition (GeckoLib 4.8.4 bytecode) = worldSpaceMatrix * (0,0,0,1), and GeoEntityRenderer.renderRecursively sets worldSpaceMatrix = translate(invertAndMultiplyMatrices(poseState, entityRenderTranslations), animatable.position()) where invertAndMultiplyMatrices(a,b) = b^-1 * a (RenderUtil bytecode). CRUCIAL ORDER: GeoEntityRenderer.preRender bytecode does `putfield entityRenderTranslations = new Matrix4f(poseStack.last().pose())` at offsets 0-15 and only THEN calls scaleModelForRender(scaleWidth, scaleHeight, ...) (offset 38), which scales the pose only if a factor != 1 and !isReRender (GeoRenderer.scaleModelForRender bytecode). Therefore any pose scale applied BEFORE super.preRender (the port's current QueenRenderer.java:73-76 pattern) is baked into entityRenderTranslations and cancelled out of every bone's local/world matrix (localMatrix = (P0*S)^-1 * P0*S*R*B = R*B), i.e. it moves NOTHING in MHLib; a scale applied AFTER the capture (GeckoLib withScale/scaleWidth, or poseStack.scale after super.preRender) multiplies every bone world offset (localMatrix = S*R*B) and therefore every part position, while part sizes stay at the profile values. Consequence for today's code: the BOSS-017 claim that 'the nice Queen's hit surfaces shrink too' (QueenRenderer.java:60-65, TheQueen.java:421-424, AUDIT_FINDINGS.md:3521) does not hold — a PlayNicely Queen is drawn at 0.25 with full-size part positions.
